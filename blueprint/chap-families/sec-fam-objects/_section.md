This section defines the objects of the chapter. A member of a family
([def-family-member]) is built from rank data ([def-family-ranks]),
archimedean parameters ([def-harish-chandra-parameters]) and hermitian
spaces over an auxiliary CM field: a definite chain with intermediate,
indefinite and auxiliary spaces ([def-family-hermitian-spaces]). Scales and
size data ([def-family-scale]) fix the weights of a member, and a family
([def-family]) is a set of members, one set for each scale, that covers all
weights. [def-family-data] lists the family hypotheses, the conditions on a
class of families that the rest of the chapter proves.
