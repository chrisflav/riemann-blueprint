The smallness condition gives $l \ne 2$. The theorem of Bhatt and Scholze
[thm-bhatt-scholze-coefficients] gives a crystalline lattice linked to the
block data. A level map and a Hecke module over the coefficient ring exist.
The maximal ideals of the Hecke algebra that meet the subspace cover it. At
each such ideal, Chebotarev's theorem for primes of degree one
[thm-chebotarev-degree-one] gives
the link with the weight data.
