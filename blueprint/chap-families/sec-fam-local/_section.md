This is the second step of the proof of [thm-family-data-exists]. At the
points of a family, [thm-twisting-index-and-archimedean-bounds] bounds the
index of the standard lattices in the lattices $M_j$ at the twisting places
and bounds the archimedean weights below. Height inequalities and integral
models of unitary Shimura varieties bound the depth of the local data at
every prime ([prop-height-inequalities-at-points],
[prop-lattice-and-depth-estimates]), and explicit exponents bound the twists
at the twisting places ([prop-exponent-bounds-twisting-places]). The
definite-side condition of [thm-family-conditions-from-basic] and the slope
bound on the indefinite side use the lower bound on weights.
