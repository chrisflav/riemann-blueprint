Let $D$ be a member of a family ([def-family-member]) with size data and a
choice of parameters ([def-family-scale]), whose real data have the weak
characterisation and good types that the construction provides. Let
$Y_0$ be its first intermediate space ([def-family-hermitian-spaces]) and
$W_i$ a hermitian space whose unitary group is
isomorphic to that of $Y_0$ at every finite place, by an isomorphism $\Phi$
matching the level groups. Then for every irreducible admissible
representation $\sigma$ of $\U(Y_0)(\A_f)$ of good type,
$$m_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\sigma \otimes \pi_\infty) = m_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\sigma \circ \Phi^{-1} \otimes \pi'_\infty),$$
where $\pi_\infty$, $\pi'_\infty$ are the corresponding discrete series of
the two real groups.

This is a transfer of cuspidal multiplicities between inner forms, of the
kind that the simple trace formula with pseudo-coefficients gives. *We read*
$W_i$ as one of the indefinite spaces of the member, which are isometric to
$Y_0$ over the finite adèles.
