Let $p$ be construction data from a system of local parameters
([def-local-parameters]), with no extra condition at the spectral step, for
which the level-three upgrade holds. Then every normalised depth-three
partial construction for $p$ extends to a depth-four one. Depth four adds
data at the split primes, compatibility conditions at the real places, and
identities fixing the signatures and the discriminant.
