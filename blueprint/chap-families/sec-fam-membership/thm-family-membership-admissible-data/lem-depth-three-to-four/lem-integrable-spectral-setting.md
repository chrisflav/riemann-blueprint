Let $D$ be a construction with forms $F_u$ for each index
$u$. Suppose that $D$ satisfies the *gap hypothesis*: the rank $r$ is at
least $1801$, and consecutive highest weights at each real place differ by
at least $16r^{19}$. Suppose also that $D$ satisfies the local compatibility
conditions. Then there is a spectral setting for $D$ in which the test
function is integrable against the measure at the infinite places. The
setting includes a standard additive character, compatible local measures,
and transports of the forms $F_u$ that match levels and identify the
spectral data with the forms.
