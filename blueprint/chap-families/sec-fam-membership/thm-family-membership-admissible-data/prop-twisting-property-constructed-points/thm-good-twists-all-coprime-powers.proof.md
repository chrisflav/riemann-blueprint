Tate's local and global duality and Euler characteristic formulas
([thm-tate-local-duality-global-places], [thm-tate-euler-characteristic-global-places],
[thm-tate-global-euler-characteristic]) and the Greenberg–Wiles formula
([thm-greenberg-wiles]) give a presentation matrix for the Selmer group. A
choice of odd auxiliary primes bounds the residual Selmer group, and
Chebotarev's theorem ([thm-chebotarev-degree-one]) gives a bound
modulo $2$ at the prime $2$. A twisting theorem for lattices of bounded
vectors, with Artin reciprocity for ray class characters
([thm-ray-class-characters-galois]), turns these bounds into the character
$\psi'$. *We read* the argument as a choice of auxiliary primes in the style
of Taylor–Wiles.
