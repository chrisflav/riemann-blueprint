The theta series are rational with bounded coefficients, and Hecke
operators preserve the lattice of bounded vectors
([prop-hecke-integrality-transformation]). These facts, the Hodge numbers
and the roots of unity give the inputs of
[thm-good-twists-all-coprime-powers], which supplies the good twists. At
each twisted place, rational scalars give theta series forms of full rank,
and class sums, through Artin reciprocity for ray class characters
([thm-ray-class-characters-galois]), give a basis of averaged series. The
local conclusion at $D$, which *we read* as a published statement on volumes
and masses of unitary groups, enters here as well.
