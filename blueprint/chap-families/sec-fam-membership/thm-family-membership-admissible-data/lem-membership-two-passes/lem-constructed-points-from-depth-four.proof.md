Combine the depth-four data with the ambient, division and theta data, and
with a lattice layer and kernel datum. Choose a gauge for the CM extension
and assemble the constructed point. The proof uses the ratio of
Siegel masses of standard hermitian lattices of consecutive ranks, from
Siegel's mass formula [thm-siegel-main-theorem-hermitian]; *we read* it as
entering through the lattice layer.
