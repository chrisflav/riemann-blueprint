The covering theorem for discrete series ([thm-discrete-series-exist-upq]),
the published results on Galois representations
([thm-galois-reps-polarized-cuspidal], [thm-galois-reps-csd-above-l]) and on
theta series, and the equality of cuspidal multiplicities they give, yield
the statement on multiplicity types. With the uniqueness of discrete series
of $\U(p, q)$ with given parameter and the internal isotypic
decomposition they give rationality, using the Casselman–Wallach theorem
[thm-casselman-wallach], Harish-Chandra modules [thm-hc-modules-unitary]
and the Weil representation [thm-weil-representation-unitary-exists].
Rationality and the parameter statement give the cuspidal statement. A
statement on the sizes $\eta$, proved from the point statement, and the
archimedean comparison give finiteness. The lists of settings, series and
moduli points exist at every $c$, and a step that normalises them carries
them through the intermediate levels.
