This is the first step of the proof of [thm-family-data-exists]: we build
the members of the families from a system of local parameters and find
admissible data at their points. The key theorem,
[thm-family-membership-admissible-data], gives a ratio of Siegel masses of
standard hermitian lattices, the membership conditions and the admissible
data. Its proof builds partial constructions of depth three and four,
passes twice through them, and gets the twisting property at constructed
points from theta series. One input, the equality of cuspidal
multiplicities on inner forms ([thm-inner-form-multiplicities-equal]),
comes from a simple trace formula with pseudo-coefficients.
