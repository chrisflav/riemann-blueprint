TODO-INTRO Siegel's mass formula for standard hermitian lattices, as an explicit ratio, gives the members and admissible data for them: two passes through depths three and four, constructed points with the twisting property from theta series, and intermediate constructions. One input, equality of cuspidal multiplicities on inner forms, is proved by a simple trace formula with pseudo-coefficients.
