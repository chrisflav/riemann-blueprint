TODO-INTRO Proves [thm-family-data-exists]: given Galois representations for cuspidal representations, some class of families satisfies the family hypotheses. A member is a chain of definite unitary groups U(X_0) -> ... -> U(X_m) of ranks k + j(t+1) over the auxiliary field, linked by theta lifts and carrying lattices. The proof builds the members from Siegel's mass formula and admissible data, bounds the local data at the points by integral models, checks twelve basic conditions from a system of local parameters, and derives the full list of 26 family conditions from them. The theta-lift inputs come from [chap-theta], the covolume bound from [chap-heights] and the error term from [chap-sha].
