This is the third step of the proof of [thm-family-data-exists]. For the
families built from a system of local parameters,
[thm-basic-conditions-from-local-parameters] shows that twelve basic
conditions hold at every admissible datum. Most hold by construction or
follow from a few published results. The multiplicity condition is the
isometric period identity of [chap-theta]
([prop-isometric-period-identity]), and the defect condition
([prop-defect-condition]) is a norm bound on the definite side. The next
section derives the full list of family conditions from these twelve.
