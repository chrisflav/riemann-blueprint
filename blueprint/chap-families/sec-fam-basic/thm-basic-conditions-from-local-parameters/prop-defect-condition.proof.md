At an admissible datum, point evaluation has the required form, the
members satisfy the height bounds, and the lattices $\nu_u$ have full,
positive rank. Large members have full rank, and the sizes of the members
are bounded. Take the constant $C_0$ that
[prop-definite-side-norm-bounds] gives for the size
constants of the family. Families whose size constant exceeds $C_0$ make up
a closed class, and on it that proposition applies at every large scale.
No published result enters this argument.
