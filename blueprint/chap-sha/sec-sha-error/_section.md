This section shows where [thm-sha-bound] enters the estimates for families.
Given a bound at the primes $2$ and $3$, [thm-family-sha-error-bound]
bounds the error term of the $j$-th member of a family by
$\eta_j \le d_j\big(C(fmbL^2 + N\log^2 x) + \sum_l \log l\sum_{e<t}\mathrm{len}\,Ш^1_l(E, e)\big)$
([def-sha-error-term]). The proof cuts each member down to its good
eigensystems and bounds the congruence valuation of each one prime by
prime: at the auxiliary primes $g_0, g_1$, at the primes $q, d$ of the
auxiliary pair, in an intermediate range and at large primes. Each local
bound combines the index theorem of [sec-sha-congruence] with the lifting
theorems of [sec-sha-lifting].
