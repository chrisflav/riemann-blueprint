There is $C_T$ with the following property. Let a member satisfy the Galois
conditions and the character conditions at $g_0, g_1$, and let $i$ be a
local datum with $l = g_k$, where $\log g_{k'} \le 2\log g_k$, $g_k$ is
larger than $C_T$ times a polynomial in the ranks and the twist sizes, and
the groups $Ш^1_l(E, e)$, $0 < e < t$, are finite. Then
$$\eta_i \le \sum_\pi \mathrm{mult}(\pi)\big(s_\pi + V_l + v_l(h_E) + R_\pi\big),$$
the sum over good $\pi$, with $R_\pi$ as in
[prop-congruence-bound-auxiliary-primes] for the constant $C = 8C_T$.
