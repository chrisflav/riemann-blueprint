Both lattices come from Breuil–Kisin-type modules by the theorem of Bhatt
and Scholze [thm-bhatt-scholze-coefficients]. A finite class of $T_2$ is an
extension of crystalline lattices; lift the extension of modules through the
surjection and bound the denominator by the weights and the periods, using
[Fontaine's period ring](@thm-fontaine-bcris). *We have not checked the
details of this proof.*
