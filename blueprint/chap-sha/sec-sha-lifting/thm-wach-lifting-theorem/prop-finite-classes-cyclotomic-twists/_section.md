For every member, every $l \ge 5$ and every unramified situation over
$\Q_l$, the following holds. Let $T_0$ be a lattice in a representation
$\rho$ satisfying explicit conditions on its Hodge–Tate weights and
characters $\chi_c$, and let $X$ be a finite $O$-module. A cocycle with
values in $X$ for the twist $\rho \otimes \chi_1^{-1}$ is a Bloch–Kato finite
class if and only if it is admissible in an explicit sense defined from the
Wach module of $T_0$.
