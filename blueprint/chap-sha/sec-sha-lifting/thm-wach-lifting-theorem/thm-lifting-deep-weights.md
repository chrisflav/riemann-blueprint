Let $C \ge 160$, let $l$ be odd, $K/\Q_l$ finite, and $F : T_1 \to T_2$ a
surjection of crystalline lattices, $T_2$ of rank $r$. Suppose that at each
embedding $\tau$ the Hodge–Tate weights of $T_1$ lie in $[-\eta, \eta]$ or in
a window of length $S$ beyond $b_\tau > \eta$, all in $[-h, h]$, and that
$T_2$ has at most $m$ weights in $[-\eta, \eta]$. Suppose also that
$[K : K_0] = 2$ or that the direction of the windows depends only on the
restriction of $\tau$ to $K_0$. Then the cokernel of
$H^1_f(T_1) \to H^1_f(T_2)$ is bounded by an explicit function of
$C$, $l$, $[K_0 : \Q_l]$, $[K : K_0]$, $r$, $S$, $\eta$, $m$ and $h$.
