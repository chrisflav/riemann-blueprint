The [local theory of Bloch and Kato](@thm-bloch-kato-local),
[local Tate duality](@thm-tate-local-duality-global-places) and the
[Euler characteristic formula](@thm-tate-euler-characteristic-global-places)
compute the length of finite cohomology from the Hodge–Tate weights. Compare
these lengths for $T_1$ and $T_2$ window by window in the Wach module, and
use [Fontaine's period ring](@thm-fontaine-bcris) to control the windows of
small weights.
