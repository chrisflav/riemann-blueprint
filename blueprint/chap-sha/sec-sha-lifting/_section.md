For a surjection $T_1 \to T_2$ of crystalline lattices, the results here
bound the cokernel of $H^1_f(T_1) \to H^1_f(T_2)$ in terms of the
Hodge–Tate weights; [sec-sha-error] uses them to turn local lengths into
Selmer lengths. [thm-wach-lifting-theorem] treats weights clustered along
the embeddings, through Wach modules. The other results treat clustered
windows ([thm-lifting-clustered-weights], also with ramified coefficients),
deep weights ([thm-lifting-deep-weights]) and potentially crystalline
lattices ([thm-lifting-potentially-crystalline], through the lattices of
Bhatt and Scholze), and describe the finite classes of cyclotomic twists
([prop-finite-classes-cyclotomic-twists]).
