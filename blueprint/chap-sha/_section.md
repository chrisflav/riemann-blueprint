TODO-INTRO Proves [thm-sha-bound] and spends it. Poitou-Tate and Greenberg-Wiles bound Sha^1_l(E,-e) along the psi-fields by chi-isotypic Selmer groups of chi tensor Z_l(e) plus second cohomology of Tate twists. The Selmer groups are bounded case by case: Kato's Euler system for ordinary newforms, automorphy of GSp_4-valued lifts, automorphy of 3-dimensional representations through base change, and vanishing for the trivial character. The second half shows where the bound enters the families: the error term eta_j of a member is bounded by Sha lengths through congruence modules, Ribet's method and local lifting theorems for crystalline lattices.
