Besides Selmer groups, the reduction in the proof of [thm-sha-bound] leaves
the groups $H^2\big(G_{L,S}, \Z_l(e+1)\big)$ of Tate twists, whose order
[lem-h2-tate-twists-bound] bounds. The proof builds classes in the homology
of $\GL_n(\mathcal O_L)$ from cyclic subgroups and detects them by reduction
at primes of degree one, a form of Soulé's comparison of this homology with
Galois cohomology. A rank bound of Borel type ([thm-borel-type-rank-bound])
limits how many such classes there are.
