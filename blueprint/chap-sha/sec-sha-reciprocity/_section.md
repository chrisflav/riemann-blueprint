Kato's explicit reciprocity law ([thm-kato-explicit-reciprocity]) computes
the image at $l$ of the zeta classes of [thm-kato-zeta-integral] as the
value of a Hecke-equivariant functional on Eisenstein series; the Euler
system of [thm-kato-euler-system-ordinary] needs it. The proof reduces to
zeta classes of fixed exponent ([thm-reciprocity-fixed-exponent]) and then
to Kummer classes of Siegel units: how they behave under diamond operators,
a Mackey decomposition, and the congruence of their logarithmic derivatives
with Eisenstein series ([thm-siegel-units-eisenstein]).
