Compute the Kummer class of $g_{c,d}$ on an edge of the tower directly from
the product expansion of the Siegel unit: $s$ acts on an $l^n$-th root by a
root of unity, whose discrete logarithm is $t$ times the logarithmic
derivative of the product. The product expansion gives this derivative as
an Eisenstein series, with the factor $N^3$ from the change of variable.
