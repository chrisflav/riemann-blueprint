In the second and third cases of the proof of [thm-sha-bound], Ribet's
method produces the Selmer classes from irreducible lifts, with values in
$\mathrm{GSp}_4$, of reducible residual representations built from $\chi$
and powers of the cyclotomic character. [thm-gsp4-lift-automorphy] shows
that these lifts are automorphic, which bounds the Selmer groups. Its proof
is a potential automorphy argument: the seeds
([thm-potential-automorphy-seeds]) come from ordinary Hilbert modular forms
with mixed weights, and automorphic induction
([thm-automorphic-induction-quadratic]) carries automorphy from rank two to
rank four.
