Let $F$ be a totally real field of degree at least $2$, $l$ an odd prime,
and $\varphi$ a cuspidal Hilbert eigenform of parallel weight $k_0 \ge 4$,
level $\mathfrak c$ and finite-order character $\omega$, ordinary at the
places of a fixed set above $l$ (its eigenvalues there are $l$-adic units),
where $\mathfrak c$ is exactly divisible by those places and by some place of
norm $2$. Let $S$ be a set of real places and $B \ge 0$. Then there are
$t \ge B$ divisible by $l - 1$ and a nonzero cuspidal eigenform $\varphi'$ of
the same level and character, of weight $k_0 + 2t$ at the places in $S$ and
$k_0$ elsewhere, whose eigenvalues $\lambda'_w$ satisfy
$$\Big(\prod l^t\Big)\lambda'_w \equiv \lambda_w \pmod{\mathfrak m},$$
the product running over the places in $S$ attached to $w$.
