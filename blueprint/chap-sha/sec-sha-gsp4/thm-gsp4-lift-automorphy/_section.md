Fix an auxiliary pair for a primitive character $\psi$, with a $\psi$-field
$E$ large for $t$, an odd prime $l$, $0 < e < t$ and an even character
$\chi$ of $\Gal(E/\Q)$. Let $B$ be the set of bad primes attached to $\psi$
and $\chi'_l$ the character obtained from the prime-to-$l$ part of $\chi$,
and suppose that either $e \ge 2$, $\chi'_l$ has even order and $l \in B$
or $l - 1 \mid 2e$; or $l \notin B$, $l - 1 \mid 2e$ and $\chi \ne 1$ has
odd order. Let $\bar\rho : G_\Q \to \mathrm{GSp}_4$ be the residual
representation attached to $(l, e, \chi)$. Then:

* the ring generated by the traces of lifts of $\bar\rho$ of the prescribed
  type is finite over the coefficient ring;
* no such lift has block-diagonal image spanning the full block algebra;
* every irreducible lift of the prescribed type is automorphic over $\Q$.

*We read* the residual representations as the reducible representations
built from $\chi$ and powers of $\epsilon$ whose irreducible lifts, by
Ribet's method, produce the Selmer classes to be bounded.
