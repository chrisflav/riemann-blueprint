In the first case of the proof of [thm-sha-bound], $l$ outside the bad set
and $l - 1 \nmid 2e$, the Selmer groups are those of 2-dimensional
representations $W$ of $G_\Q$, ordinary at $l$.
[thm-kato-selmer-vanishing] shows that their Bloch–Kato Selmer groups
vanish, by Kolyvagin's argument applied to Kato's Euler system.
[thm-kato-euler-system-ordinary] builds that Euler system for $l$-ordinary
newforms from integral norm-compatible zeta classes
([thm-kato-zeta-integral]); the reciprocity law of [sec-sha-reciprocity]
shows that its bottom class is not ordinary at $l$.
