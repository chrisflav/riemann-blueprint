[thm-congruence-module-selmer-bound] is the global index theorem behind the
local bounds of [sec-sha-error]: the length of the congruence module of an
eigensystem $\pi$ is at most a sum of local lengths, a Selmer term, the
lengths of $Ш^1_l(E, e)$ and $v_l(h_E)$. It is a bound of the type of the
numerical criterion of Wiles and Lenstra. Ribet's construction proves it,
turning congruences between $\pi$ and other eigensystems into extensions of
Galois representations. [def-congruence-data] fixes the Hecke algebras and
local data it needs.
