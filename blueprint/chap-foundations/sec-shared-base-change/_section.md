Local results on representations of general linear and unitary groups that
the proof of base change between definite unitary groups and $\GL_n$ uses:
derivatives of products, the Jacquet–Rallis fundamental lemma, wave kernels and
a twisted trace on $\GL_n(\C)$, and a bound for Hecke operators.
