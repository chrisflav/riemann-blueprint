Let $\lambda \in \Z^n$ be dominant and $\tau$ a representation of $\U(n)$ with an
involution $T$ whose trace and twisted trace on the diagonal torus are given by
explicit formulas in the Weyl character of $\lambda$ at $z$ and at $z^2$. Let $V$ be a module, for the
complexified Lie algebra and $\U(n)$, with the infinitesimal character of
$\lambda$, carrying an involution $A$ that intertwines it with its twist by the
involution of $\GL_n(\C)$. If $\mathrm{Hom}_{\U(n)}(\tau, V)$ is finite-dimensional and
non-zero, then the trace of $A \circ T^\vee$ on it is $\pm 2^n$.

This computes the archimedean twisted characters in base change from $\U(n)$ to
$\GL_n(\C)$.
