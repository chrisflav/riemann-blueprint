*We read* the proof as a local one by induction on the rank, in the manner
of Beuzart-Plessis. Its only input on Weil representations is that for every
unramified quadratic extension $L/K$ of finite extensions of $F$ and every
standard character $\psi$, the Weil representation attached to the norm form
exists, with Weil constant given by the quadratic character of $L/K$; this
is proved directly. We have not unfolded the rest of the proof. It uses no
other result of this blueprint.
