This is not a theorem but the conjunction of seven published results:
Fontaine's period ring $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ ([fontaine-bcris]), global
Artin reciprocity ([global-artin-reciprocity]), the Bloch–Kato local
conditions ([bloch-kato-local]), and, for finite extensions of $\Q_l$,
local Tate duality ([local-tate-duality-k]), the local Euler
characteristic formula ([local-euler-char-k]), finiteness
([local-finiteness-k]) and the integral comparison theorem of Bhatt and
Scholze ([bhatt-scholze-integral]).
