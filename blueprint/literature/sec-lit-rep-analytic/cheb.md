Let $F$ be a number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure, $U$ an open normal
subgroup of $\Gal(\Omega/F)$, $g \in \Gal(\Omega/F)$ and $A$ a finite set of
primes. There is a prime $v \notin A$ of degree one over $\Q$ and unramified
over $\Q$, with a place above it whose inertia group lies in $U$ and whose
Frobenius $\sigma$ satisfies $\sigma g^{-1} \in U$. This is a consequence of
Chebotarev's density theorem (1926).
