The proof works in the Fock model of the real metaplectic representation
([real-metaplectic-representation]). It identifies $\mathcal P$ with a
tensor product over the real places of polynomial Fock spaces adapted to
the signatures, shows that every subspace $N$ as above contains the
coinvariants of the first member, and reads off the two properties from
the decomposition of the Fock space under the two Lie algebra actions.
