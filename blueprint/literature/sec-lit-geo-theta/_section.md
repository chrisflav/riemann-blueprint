order = 59

The representation-theoretic results used for theta lifts between unitary
groups: $(\GL_n, \GL_{n'})$ duality on polynomials, the archimedean Howe
duality for the theta lift of the trivial representation, Harish-Chandra's
formal degree, the existence of Weil representations for unitary dual
pairs, Weil's convergence criterion for theta integrals, and the
Siegel–Weil formula in the convergent range. Throughout, $E/F$ is a CM
extension of number fields with non-trivial automorphism $\sigma$.
