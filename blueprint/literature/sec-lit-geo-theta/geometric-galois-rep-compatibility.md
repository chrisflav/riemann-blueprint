We collect under one name the published results of harmonic analysis that
the theta lifts between definite unitary groups use: Weil representations
for unitary pairs ([weil-representation-unitary-pair]); the existence and
classification of discrete series ([discrete-series-exists],
[discrete-series-classification]); Harish-Chandra's analyticity theorem
([harish-chandra-analyticity]); trace class of cusp forms
([cusp-trace-class]); the Dixmier–Malliavin factorisation
([dixmier-malliavin-factorisation]); Flath's factorisation
([flath-factorisation]); the Siegel–Weil formula for unitary groups
([weil-unitary]); the local zeta integrals of the doubling method and their
unramified computation ([doubling-method-local-zeta-integrals],
[unramified-doubling-gl]); the continuation of partial Hecke $L$-functions
([hecke-l-partial-continuation]); and [ht-card], which *we read as* a
continuity condition on the Galois representations attached along the way.
