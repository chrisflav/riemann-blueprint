By [algebraic-character-wd] the Weil–Deligne representation of the
restriction is $\xi \circ \mathrm{Art}_{E_w}^{-1}$, which is unramified because
$\chi$ is unramified at $w$; [global-artin-reciprocity] and
[chevalley-units] enter through the local Artin map. A potentially
semistable character whose Weil–Deligne representation is unramified with
$N = 0$ is crystalline.
