In the situation of [algebraic-character-wd], let $\rec$ be any system of
local parameters for the $\GL_n(E_w)$ ([def-local-parameters]). Then the
Weil–Deligne representation of $r_{\chi,\iota}|_{G_{E_w}}$ is
$\rec(\xi)$, the parameter of $\xi$ viewed as a representation of
$\GL_1(E_w)$.

This restates [algebraic-character-wd], since a system of local parameters
is the local Artin map on characters.
