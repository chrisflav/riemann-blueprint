The proof has three parts: the archimedean components of a cuspidal
representation are generic; Kuga's lemma, that relative Lie algebra
cohomology vanishes unless the infinitesimal characters match; and a direct
computation of relative Lie algebra cochains, which produces a non-zero
class for the algebraic representation $\Xi$ read off the infinitesimal
character. It draws on [cusp-forms-bounded-gl] and [car-u-alt].
