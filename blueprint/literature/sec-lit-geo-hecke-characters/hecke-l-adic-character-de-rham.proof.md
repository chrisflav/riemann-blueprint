*We read the proof as* the classical one: by [chevalley-units] the global
units contribute nothing on a small open subgroup of the inertia group at
$w$, so there the character is the algebraic character
$\prod_\tau \tau^{n_{\iota\tau}}$ composed with the local Artin map, and
characters of this form are de Rham with the stated weights.
