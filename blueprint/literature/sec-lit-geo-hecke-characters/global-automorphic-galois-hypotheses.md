We collect under one name the inputs of the construction of Galois
representations: rapid decay of cusp forms ([cusp-rapid-decay]);
Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem ([harish-chandra-finite]); finiteness of
spaces of automorphic forms on $\GL_n$ ([automorphic-forms-finite-gl]);
Jacquet–Shalika ([jacquet-shalika]); the cuspidal spectrum of $\GL_n$
([cuspidal-spectrum-gl]); base change from unitary groups
([thm-base-change-unitary]); Gelfand–Kazhdan ([gelfand-kazhdan-dual]);
Clozel's purity and cohomologicity theorems ([clozel-purity],
[clozel-cohomological]); Artin reciprocity and ray class fields
([global-artin-reciprocity], [ray-class-field-st]); the results on Hecke
characters above $l$ ([hecke-l-adic-character-above],
[algebraic-character-wd]); and Berger's theorem
([de-rham-potentially-semistable]); together with the segment
classification of essentially square-integrable representations
([ess-square-integrable-segments]).

The list also contains, as two of its items, the existence of Galois
representations for polarized pairs ([def-galois-reps-polarized]) in two
slightly different forms. So it is a list of hypotheses that includes the
conclusion of [thm-galois-reps-polarized], not a list of published results
alone.
