We collect under one name the published results that the study of Hodge
bundles and heights on unitary Shimura varieties uses:

* on abelian schemes: [ab-mul-n-st], [principal-polarisation-one-section],
  [semi-abelian-hom-extension], [semistable-reduction-st],
  [abelian-de-rham], [grothendieck-messing], [prismatic-dieudonne-hodge],
  [formal-algebraisation] and [toroidal-compactification];
* on complex and birational geometry: [kawamata-viehweg],
  [l2-extension-log-canonical], [serre-holomorphic-sections],
  [principalisation] and [chow-form-exists];
* on heights and Arakelov geometry: [arith-hilbert-samuel-of-literature],
  [vol-nef], [vol-ge], [ar-calc] and [good-chern-weil-of-literature];
* on arithmetic and groups: [ray-class-field-st], [hasse-norm-st],
  [landherr-st], [strong-approx-su], [green-cuspidal] and
  [induced-simple-lowest-alcove].
