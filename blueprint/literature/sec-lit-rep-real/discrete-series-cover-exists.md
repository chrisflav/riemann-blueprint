Let $\tilde G$ be the double cover of $\U(p,q)$ on which $\sqrt{\det}$ is
defined and $\lambda$ a Harish-Chandra parameter: injective,
decreasing on compact positive roots, with all $2\lambda_a$ integers and all
differences integers. There is a square-integrable irreducible unitary
representation $R$ of $\tilde G$ that has $\lambda$ as parameter: its Blattner
$K$-type $\lambda + \rho_n - \rho_c$ occurs as a highest weight, and every
highest weight $\mu$ of a $K$-type has Vogan norm at least that of the
Blattner $K$-type.
