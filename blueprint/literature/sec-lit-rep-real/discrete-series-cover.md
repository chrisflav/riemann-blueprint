Let $\tilde G$ be the double cover of $\U(p,q)$, $p + q \ge 1$, on which
$\sqrt{\det}$ is defined. There is a map
$\lambda \mapsto D(\lambda)$ from Harish-Chandra parameters to
square-integrable irreducible unitary representations such that

* the Blattner $K$-type of $\lambda$ has multiplicity one in $D(\lambda)$;
* the Casimir operator acts on $D(\lambda)$ by $\|\lambda\|^2 - \|\rho\|^2$;
* every highest weight of a $K$-type of $D(\lambda)$ lies in the cone of
  $\lambda$ (Blattner);

and every square-integrable irreducible unitary representation of $\tilde G$ is
unitarily equivalent to some $D(\lambda)$ (Harish-Chandra).
