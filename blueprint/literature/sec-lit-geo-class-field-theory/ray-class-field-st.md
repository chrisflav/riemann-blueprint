Let $K$ be a number field and $\mathfrak m \ne 0$ an ideal of $\mathcal O_K$.
There is a finite abelian extension $L/K$ and an isomorphism from the narrow
ray class group of modulus $\mathfrak m$ (ideals prime to $\mathfrak m$
modulo principal ideals $(x)$ with $x$ totally positive and
$x \equiv 1 \bmod \mathfrak m$) onto $\Gal(L/K)$, such that every prime
$\mathfrak p \nmid \mathfrak m$ is unramified in $L$ and its class maps to
its Frobenius.

This is the existence theorem of Takagi (1920).
