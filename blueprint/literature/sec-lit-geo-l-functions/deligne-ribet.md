Let $F$ be a totally real field, $l$ a prime, $n \ge 1$, and let
$\varepsilon$ be a function on ideals prime to $l$ that is constant on the
narrow ray classes modulo $l^n$ and takes $l$-integral rational values. Let
$L(\varepsilon, s) = \sum_I \varepsilon(I) N I^{-s}$. For every ideal
$\mathfrak c$ prime to $l$ and every $k \ge 1$, the number
$$ L(\varepsilon, 1-k) - N\mathfrak c^{\,k}\, L(\varepsilon(\mathfrak c\,\cdot), 1-k) $$
is $l$-integral, whenever both values are rational.

This is the integrality theorem of Deligne and Ribet (1980), also proved by
Cassou-Noguès (1979), for ray class functions of $l$-power conductor.
