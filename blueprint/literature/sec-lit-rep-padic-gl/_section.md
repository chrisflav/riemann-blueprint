order = 1

Smooth complex representations of $\GL_n(F)$ for a non-archimedean local
field $F$: the finiteness theorems of Bernstein and Zelevinsky, the geometric
lemma, Zelevinsky's classification by segments, and the tempered, generic
and unitary representations built from it. We write $\pi_1 \times \pi_2$ for
parabolic induction and $\nu = |\det|$.
