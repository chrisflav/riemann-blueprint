This is not a single theorem: it is the conjunction of the local results
about $\GL_n$ of a $p$-adic field that the proof uses, namely
[bernstein-zelevinsky-finiteness], [bernstein-unitary-product-irreducible],
[geometric-lemma], the Zelevinsky polynomial ring and the classification of
[ess-square-integrable-segments], the irreducibility criterion for
disjoint cuspidal supports, [moy-prasad-jacquet-iso],
[tempered-product-of-square-integrable], [square-integrable-segment],
the existence of local parameters ([def-local-parameters]),
[local-converse-gl], [depth-zero-induction], [brauer-degree-zero],
[chevalley-units] and [green-regular-characters].
