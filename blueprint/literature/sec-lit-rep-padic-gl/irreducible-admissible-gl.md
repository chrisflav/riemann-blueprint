Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field. Every irreducible smooth
representation of $\GL_n(F)$ is admissible: for each open compact subgroup
$K$ its space of $K$-fixed vectors is finite-dimensional. This is Jacquet's
theorem for $\GL_n$ (1975), a case of Bernstein's theorem for all reductive
$p$-adic groups.
