Write $\pi$ as a product of unlinked segment representations
([ess-square-integrable-segments]). Unitarity forces the segments to
pair off under $\nu^s \mapsto \nu^{-s}$, with exponents bounded by
$1/2$; the finite length and Jacquet module computations come from
[bernstein-zelevinsky-finiteness], [standard-functors-finite-length],
[irreducible-admissible-gl], [geometric-lemma] and
[geometric-lemma-filtration].
