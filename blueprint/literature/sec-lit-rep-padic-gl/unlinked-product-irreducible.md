Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field and $\pi_1, \pi_2$ irreducible
representations of $\GL_r(F)$ and $\GL_t(F)$ with cuspidal supports
$\{\sigma_a\}$ and $\{\sigma'_b\}$. If no $\sigma_a$ is isomorphic to an
unramified twist $\sigma'_b \otimes (\chi\circ\det)$ of any $\sigma'_b$, then
$\pi_1 \times \pi_2$ is irreducible. This is a weaker form of the
Bernstein–Zelevinsky criterion (which allows all twists except $\nu^{\pm1}$
and $1$); it follows from [bernstein-zelevinsky-finiteness].
