[bernstein-zelevinsky-finiteness]: the finite length of Jacquet modules
and induced representations ([standard-functors-finite-length],
[irreducible-admissible-gl]), the geometric lemma
([geometric-lemma-filtration], [geometric-lemma]) to compute Jacquet
modules of products, and the Borel–Casselman isomorphism
([borel-casselman]) for the Iwahori-spherical case.
