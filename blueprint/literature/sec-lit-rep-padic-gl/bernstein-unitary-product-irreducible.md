Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field. If $\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$ are
irreducible unitarisable smooth representations of $\GL_r(F)$ and
$\GL_t(F)$, then $\pi_1 \times \pi_2$ is irreducible (Bernstein, 1984).
