Throughout, $E/F$ is a CM extension of number fields, $V$ a non-degenerate
hermitian space over $E$ and $G = \U(V)$. This section collects reduction
theory, strong approximation, Flath's factorisation and the analytic
properties of cusp forms on $G(F)\backslash G(\A_F)$.
