By [dixmier-malliavin-factorisation], $R(f)$ is a finite sum of products
$R(g_i)R(h_i)$. Each $R(h_i)$ on cusp forms has a kernel bounded by the
rapid decay estimate [cusp-decay-bound], so it is Hilbert–Schmidt, and a
product of two Hilbert–Schmidt operators is of trace class.
