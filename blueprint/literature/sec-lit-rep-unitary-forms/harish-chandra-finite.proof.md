The proof uses the rapid decay of cusp forms ([cusp-rapid-decay],
[cusp-decay-bound]) and the boundedness of cusp forms on general linear
groups ([cusp-forms-bounded-gl]). *We read* the latter as entering through
the Levi subgroups of $\U(V)$, which are products of general linear groups
and a smaller unitary group.
