Let $E/F$ be a CM extension, $V$ a non-degenerate hermitian space over $E$
and $v$ a finite place of $F$. For every irreducible unitary representation
$\pi$ of $\U(V)(F_v)$ and every open compact subgroup $K$, the space of
$K$-fixed vectors is finite-dimensional. This is a case of Bernstein's
theorem (1974) that irreducible unitary representations of reductive
$p$-adic groups are admissible.
