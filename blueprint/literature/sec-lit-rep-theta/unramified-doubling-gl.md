Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field of characteristic zero with residue
field of order $q$, $\mu$ the Haar measure on $\GL_n(F)$ with
$\mu(\GL_n(\mathcal O)) = 1$, $\pi$ an unramified representation with Satake
parameter $\alpha$, and $\omega$ its zonal spherical function. For $\Re s$
large the doubling zeta integral of the spherical section $f_s$ converges and
$$
\int_{\GL_n(F)} f_s\big((g,1)\big)\,\omega(g)\,d\mu(g)
= \frac{L(s + \tfrac12, \alpha)\,L(s + \tfrac12, \alpha^{-1})}{d_n(s)},
$$
where $d_n(s)$ is the normalising product of abelian $L$-factors. This is
the unramified computation of the doubling method (Piatetski-Shapiro and
Rallis, 1987) for $\GL_n$.
