Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field with $2 \ne 0$, $W$ a
finite-dimensional symplectic space over $F$ with Heisenberg group $H(W)$,
and $\psi$ a non-trivial continuous additive character of $F$. There is an
irreducible smooth representation of $H(W)$ with central character $\psi$,
and any two such representations are isomorphic (Stone, von Neumann; Mackey
and Weil, 1964, in this setting).
