Representations of $\GL_n(F)$ with vectors fixed by the first congruence
subgroup are controlled by representations of $\GL_n$ of the residue field.
This section collects Green's description of cuspidal characters of
$\GL_d(\mathbb F_g)$, the depth-zero results of Moy and Prasad, and one result
on modular representations of $\GL_N$.
