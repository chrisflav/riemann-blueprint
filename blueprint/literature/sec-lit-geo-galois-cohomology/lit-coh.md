We collect under one name the results of Galois cohomology that the bound
on Tate–Shafarevich groups uses: Poitou–Tate duality
([poitou-tate-normalised]); finiteness of local cohomology
([local-finiteness], [local-finiteness-k]); local duality
([local-tate-duality], [local-tate-duality-k]); the local Euler
characteristic formula ([local-euler-char], [local-euler-char-k]);
finiteness of global cohomology ([global-finiteness]); the global Euler
characteristic formula ([global-euler-char-full]); the orthogonality of
unramified classes ([unramified-orthogonality]); the Greenberg–Wiles formula
([greenberg-wiles]); and the local theory of Bloch and Kato
([bloch-kato-local]).
