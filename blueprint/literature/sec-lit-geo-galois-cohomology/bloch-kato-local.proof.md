The fundamental exact sequence
$0 \to \Q_l \to B_{\mathrm{cris}}^{\varphi = 1} \to B_{\mathrm{dR}}/\mathrm{Fil}^0 \to 0$
([fontaine-bcris]) gives the dimension of $H^1_f$. The Euler characteristic
formula ([local-euler-char-k]) and local duality ([local-tate-duality-k]),
passed to the limit over $l^r$, give the duality statements.
