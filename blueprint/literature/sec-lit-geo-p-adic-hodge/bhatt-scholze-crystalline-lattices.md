The statement of [bhatt-scholze-integral] holds for lattices with
coefficients: for a finite extension $L/\Q_l$ and a $G_K$-stable
$\mathcal O_L$-lattice $T$ in a crystalline representation over $L$, the
underlying $\Z_l$-lattice is realised by a prismatic $F$-crystal, with the
same full faithfulness. We derive it from [bhatt-scholze-integral] by
forgetting the $\mathcal O_L$-structure.
