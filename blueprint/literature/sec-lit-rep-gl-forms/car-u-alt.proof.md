Turn the conjugate self-dual $\pi$ into a polarized pair through
[gelfand-kazhdan-dual], take the Galois representation that the
polarized statement provides, and check the conditions one by one. The
compatibility above $l$ is read off from the local conditions of that
representation in the abelian case, using the Langlands classification
([ess-square-integrable-segments]), [bernstein-zelevinsky-finiteness],
[geometric-lemma], [borel-casselman] and the Hasse norm theorem
[hasse-norm-st].
