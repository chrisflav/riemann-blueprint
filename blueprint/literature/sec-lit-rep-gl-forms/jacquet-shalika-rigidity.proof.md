Compare the Rankin–Selberg $L$-functions $L(s, \pi_i \times \pi'^\vee_j)$:
their poles at $s = 1$ detect equality of cuspidal factors, and the
Jacquet–Shalika bound on Satake parameters controls the finitely many
remaining Euler factors. The boundedness of cusp forms
([cusp-forms-bounded-gl]), the finiteness theorem
([automorphic-forms-finite-gl]) and the cuspidal spectrum
([cuspidal-spectrum-gl]) supply the analytic input.
