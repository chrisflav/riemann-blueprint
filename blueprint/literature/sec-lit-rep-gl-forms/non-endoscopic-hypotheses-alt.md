This is not a theorem but the conjunction of five groups of statements:

* the local representation theory of [local-representation-theory-hypotheses-alt];
* the global results on automorphic forms and Galois representations
  collected in [global-automorphic-galois-hypotheses] (rapid decay,
  finiteness, Jacquet–Shalika, the cuspidal spectrum, base change,
  Gelfand–Kazhdan, Galois representations of polarized pairs and Clozel's
  purity);
* the cohomological results [lit-coh];
* the statements of [literature-nonexistence];
* the functional equation of Hecke $L$-functions
  ([hecke-tate-functional-equation]).
