Let $E$ be a totally complex number field, $a \ge 1$ and $\pi$ a cuspidal
automorphic representation of $\GL_a(\A_E)$ that is algebraic: at each
complex embedding $\tau$ every entry $z$ of the infinitesimal character lies
in $\Z + (a-1)/2$. Then there is $w_0 \in \Z$ with
$$
\mathrm{inf}_{\bar\tau}(\pi) = \{\, w_0 - z : z \in \mathrm{inf}_\tau(\pi) \,\}
$$
for every $\tau$ (Clozel, 1990, Lemma 4.9).
