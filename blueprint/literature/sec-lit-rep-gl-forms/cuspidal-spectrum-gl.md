Let $E$ be a totally complex number field and $a \ge 0$.

* The right regular representation of $\GL_a(\A_E)$ on the cuspidal
  subspace $L^2_{\mathrm{cusp}}$ is a Hilbert direct sum of irreducible
  representations (Gelfand, Graev, Piatetski-Shapiro).
* Every irreducible closed invariant subspace of $L^2_{\mathrm{cusp}}$ has as
  its smooth $K$-finite part the space of a cuspidal automorphic
  representation.
* Cusp forms are bounded ([cusp-forms-bounded-gl]).
