order = 5

Cusp forms and cuspidal automorphic representations of $\GL_a(\A_E)$ for a
totally complex field $E$, usually a CM field: boundedness, finiteness,
multiplicity one, the Jacquet–Shalika theorems, Gelfand–Kazhdan duality,
purity, base change from unitary groups, and the forms of the existence of
Galois representations that the proof uses.
