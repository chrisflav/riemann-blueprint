Let $K \subset \C$ be an imaginary quadratic field and $\mathfrak a$ a
fractional ideal of $\mathcal O_K$, viewed as a lattice in $\C$. Then
$j(\mathfrak a)$ is algebraic, and its orbit under $\mathrm{Aut}(\C/K)$ is the
set of all $j(\mathfrak b)$, $\mathfrak b$ a fractional ideal of $\mathcal O_K$.
This is the main theorem of complex multiplication for $j$ (Weber, Hasse).
