There is a rule that assigns to every variety over a field of characteristic
zero a resolution of its singularities, and to every embedded variety a
strong embedded resolution (a sequence of blow-ups in smooth centres after
which the strict transform is smooth and meets the exceptional divisor with
simple normal crossings). The rule commutes with smooth morphisms and with
extensions of the ground field.
