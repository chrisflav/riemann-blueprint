Assume strict transforms of closed subschemes are blow-ups of the
transformed subschemes. Fix $n$. Suppose that a procedure $L'$ reduces the
maximal order in dimensions $< n$ for $L$, that a family $A_j$ reduces the
boundary divisors in dimensions $\le n$, and that the family $A_j$ does not
depend on the positions of the boundary divisors. Then the order step of the
canonical algorithm, built from $A$, reduces the order in dimensions
$\le n$.
