Suppose that the constant terms of Siegel Eisenstein series split as in
[lem-siegel-weil-from-constant-terms]. Then for every $n$ the two sides of
the Siegel–Weil formula for definite spaces ([thm-siegel-weil-definite]) have
the same constant terms along every maximal parabolic, and the formula holds.
