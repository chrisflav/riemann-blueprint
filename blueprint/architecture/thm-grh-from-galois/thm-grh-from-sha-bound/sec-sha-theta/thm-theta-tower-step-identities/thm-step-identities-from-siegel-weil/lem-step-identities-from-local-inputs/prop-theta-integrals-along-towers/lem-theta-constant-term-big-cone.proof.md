Unfold the theta integral along the parabolic, split the sum over vectors
into those that pair trivially with $H$ and the rest, and apply Poisson
summation on the first set; the Weil representation and its action on the
Levi factor are those of Kudla's splitting ([thm-kudla-splitting]), and the
theta integrals converge by [thm-siegel-weil-weil-range]. Hasse's norm
theorem ([thm-hasse-norm-quadratic]) is used to find the representing
vectors.
