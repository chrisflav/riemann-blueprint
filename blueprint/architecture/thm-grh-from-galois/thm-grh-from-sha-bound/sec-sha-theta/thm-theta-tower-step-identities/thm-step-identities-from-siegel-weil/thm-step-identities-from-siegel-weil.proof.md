Apply [lem-step-identities-from-local-inputs]. Its remaining hypotheses are proved: coinvariants at
finite places and multiplicity one at ramified places (Bernstein's
theory and the geometric lemma), finiteness at ramified, split and inert
places, spherical triples from Howe duality at inert places, the
archimedean Lie algebra statements from lifting of local theta
correspondences, reduction theory on Siegel sets, and existence of the
Weil representation. Hasse's norm theorem enters at the inert places.
