This is [lem-step-identities-from-local-inputs]. The global inputs are the
Siegel–Weil formula for definite spaces ([thm-siegel-weil-definite]), the
bound on Siegel Eisenstein series ([thm-eisenstein-continuation-growth]) and
reduction theory on Siegel sets ([thm-reduction-theory-unitary]). The local
inputs come from Bernstein–Zelevinsky theory ([thm-bz-finiteness]) and the
geometric lemma ([thm-geometric-lemma-filtration]) at finite places, from
Howe duality at inert places, where Hasse's norm theorem
([thm-hasse-norm-quadratic]) also enters, and from lifting of local theta
correspondences at the archimedean places.
