Square-integrable representations have finite length as Harish-Chandra
modules and regular infinitesimal character. A shift of the infinitesimal
character, exhaustion of the Hecke algebra by $K$-types, a decay estimate
and exhaustion of far-regular parameters identify $\sigma$ with a discrete
series.
