[thm-grh-from-theta-integrals] assumes the step identities of theta towers.
This section proves them from the Siegel–Weil formula and the constant terms
of theta integrals, and proves the forms of the Siegel–Weil formula, the
local Weil representation and the classification of the discrete series
that the proof uses.
