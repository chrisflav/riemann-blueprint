In the situation of [lem-siegel-weil-singular-coefficients], let $\beta$ be in block normal form, with a
non-singular upper-left block of size $t < r$ and zeros elsewhere, and
suppose some $x \in V^r$ of rank $r$ has $(x, x) = \beta$. Then the $\beta$-th
Fourier coefficient of the theta integral equals $W_\beta(\varphi)$.
