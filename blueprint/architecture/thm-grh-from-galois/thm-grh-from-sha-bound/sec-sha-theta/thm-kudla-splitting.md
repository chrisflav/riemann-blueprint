Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field of characteristic zero, $E/F$ a
quadratic extension, $Q$ a hermitian form on $E^\alpha$, $\psi$ a non-trivial
unitary character of $F$, and $\chi$ a character of $E^\times$ whose
restriction to $F^\times$ is $\eta_{E/F}^{|\alpha|}$. Then there is a smooth
representation $\omega$ of the split unitary group $\U(\nu, \nu)$ on the
Schwartz–Bruhat space of $\mathrm{Mat}_{\alpha \times \nu}(E)$ that preserves the
$L^2$ norm, intertwines the Heisenberg representation, and on the Levi
subgroup acts by
$$\omega(m(a))\varphi(x) = \chi(\det a)\,|N_{E/F}\det a|^{|\alpha|/2}\,\varphi(xa).$$

It comes from the splitting of the metaplectic cover over unitary groups.
