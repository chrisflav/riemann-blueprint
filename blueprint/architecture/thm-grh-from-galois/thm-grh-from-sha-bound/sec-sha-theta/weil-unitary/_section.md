Let $A$ and $B$ be hermitian spaces over a CM extension, $\dim A \ge 1$, and
suppose $B$ is anisotropic or $\dim A + w(B) < \dim B$, where $w(B)$ is the Witt
index. Then:

* the theta integral over $\U(B)(F) \backslash \U(B)(\A)$ converges
  absolutely, and is bounded by a continuous seminorm of the Schwartz
  function;
* if $2\dim A < \dim B$, the Siegel Eisenstein series of the section
  attached to the Schwartz function converges on $\U(A \oplus -A)$ and equals
  the average of the theta function over $\U(B)(F) \backslash \U(B)(\A)$.

This is Weil's form (1965) of the Siegel–Weil formula for unitary groups.
