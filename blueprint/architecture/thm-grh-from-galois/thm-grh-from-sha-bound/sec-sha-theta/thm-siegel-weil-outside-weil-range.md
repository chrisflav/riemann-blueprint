Let $V$ be a hermitian space with $n < \dim V \le 2n$ and $n + w(V) < \dim V$.
Let $\Phi$ be a Schwartz function on $V^n(\A)$ whose archimedean component
lies in the Fock space. Then the theta integral of $\Phi$ converges, the
Siegel Eisenstein series of the section attached to $\Phi$ converges for
$\Re z > n/2$ and continues holomorphically to the point
$s_0 = (\dim V - n)/2$, and its value there is the theta integral.

This is the Siegel–Weil formula of Kudla–Rallis and Ichino, for unitary
groups and only for Fock vectors.
