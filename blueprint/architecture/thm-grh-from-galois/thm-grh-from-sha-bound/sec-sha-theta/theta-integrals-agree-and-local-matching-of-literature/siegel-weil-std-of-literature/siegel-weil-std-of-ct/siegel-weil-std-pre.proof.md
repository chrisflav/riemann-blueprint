Induction on $n$. Poisson summation computes the constant terms of the
theta integral, and the splitting computes those of the Eisenstein series;
they agree by induction. The difference of the two sides is then a cusp
form orthogonal to all cusp forms, hence zero. The bound on Eisenstein
series, reduction theory on Siegel sets and the norm theorems of Hasse and
Landherr give convergence and the local factors.
