The statements of [leaves] give matching of local data for triples, and
the constant-term computations of theta integrals. The Siegel–Weil
formula and the Rallis inner product formula then give the step constants
$C \cdot L^S(\nu, \tfrac{a - (b+2)}{2} + 1)$. Hecke $L$-functions do not
vanish at $s = 1$, which makes the constants non-zero; Hasse's norm theorem
is used at inert places.
