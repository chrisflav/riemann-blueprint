Choose a discrete valuation ring through $x$ and use the extension property
of the minimal compactification for semi-abelian degenerations. Mumford's
construction over a complete normal base
([thm-mumford-degeneration]) then builds the
family over the complete local ring of the cone, and formal algebraisation
makes it algebraic.
