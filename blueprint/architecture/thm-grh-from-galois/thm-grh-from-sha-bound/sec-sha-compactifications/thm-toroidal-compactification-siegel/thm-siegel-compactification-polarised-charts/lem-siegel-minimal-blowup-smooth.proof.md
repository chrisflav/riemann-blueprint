By [lem-siegel-tautological-charts] every point of $S$ is
the image of the closed point of a tautological Mumford family. *We read*
the rest as the classical argument: the formal neighbourhood of the point is
then that of the torus embedding of a cone of $\varphi$, which is smooth.
