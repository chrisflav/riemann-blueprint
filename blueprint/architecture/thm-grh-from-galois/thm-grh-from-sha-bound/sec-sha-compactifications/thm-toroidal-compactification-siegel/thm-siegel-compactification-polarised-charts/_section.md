Let $g \ge 1$ and let $\varphi$ be a polarised admissible cone decomposition
without self-intersection. Then for every odd $n \ge 3$ the moduli scheme
$A_{g,n}$ has a compactification $S$ with all the properties listed in
[thm-toroidal-compactification-siegel], except that $\omega^{Nk}(-\Delta)$
is ample for one $k$, and with polarised charts at every point.
