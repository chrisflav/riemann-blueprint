In the situation of [lem-siegel-minimal-blowup-smooth], let
$x$ be any point of $S$. Then there are a complete local ring $R$ attached
to a cone of $\varphi$, a semi-abelian scheme $G_1$ over $R$ whose generic
fibre is a principally polarised abelian variety with level-$n$ structure,
and a morphism $\xi : \mathrm{Spec}\,R \to S$ sending the closed point to
$x$, such that the generic point maps into $A_{g,n}$ and the family is the
Mumford family attached to the cone.
