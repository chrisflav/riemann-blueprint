Let $E$ be a CM field with a unitary Shimura datum of signature $(p, q)$,
$p + q \ge 1$, and let $n$ be an odd prime, inert in the imaginary quadratic
field of the datum and prime to the discriminant of $E$. Then for some
$J \ge 1$ the unitary Shimura variety has an integral model at level $n^J$
whose universal abelian scheme carries an ample line bundle with an explicit
system of theta coordinates of level $(n^k)^2$ for some $k$.
