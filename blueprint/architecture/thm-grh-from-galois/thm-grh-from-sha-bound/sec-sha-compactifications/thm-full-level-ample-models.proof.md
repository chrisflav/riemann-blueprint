Grothendieck's semistable reduction theorem ([thm-semistable-reduction])
gives semi-abelian models with theta functions
([thm-semi-abelian-theta-models]). The count of $n$-torsion points of abelian
varieties ([thm-abelian-variety-torsion-count]) gives the full level
structure, and the theta functions give the ample line bundle and its theta
coordinates.
