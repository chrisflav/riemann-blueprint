By [lem-unitary-boundary-prechart] every boundary point of the
blow-up $S$ has a pre-chart. Extension of homomorphisms of semi-abelian
schemes and the existence of Serre's holomorphic sections turn pre-charts
into rigid charts and give the ample bundle.
