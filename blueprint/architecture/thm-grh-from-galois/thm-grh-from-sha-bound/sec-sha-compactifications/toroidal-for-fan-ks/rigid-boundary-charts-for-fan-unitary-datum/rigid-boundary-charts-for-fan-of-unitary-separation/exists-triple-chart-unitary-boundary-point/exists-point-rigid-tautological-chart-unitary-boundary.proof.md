Take a discrete valuation ring through $x$, apply Mumford's construction over
a complete normal base ([has-semi-abelian-uniformisation-complete-normal-base])
to the degeneration, and use the norm theorems of Hasse and Landherr to
match the hermitian data. Semistable reduction gives the degeneration.
