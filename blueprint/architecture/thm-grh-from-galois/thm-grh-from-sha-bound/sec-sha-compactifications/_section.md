The covolume bound computes heights on unitary Shimura varieties, so it needs
their integral models and compactifications. This section constructs
degenerating abelian varieties by Mumford's method, integral models,
toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli schemes and of unitary Shimura
varieties with their boundary charts, and the Dieudonné theory of
$p$-divisible groups over $\mathcal O_C$.
