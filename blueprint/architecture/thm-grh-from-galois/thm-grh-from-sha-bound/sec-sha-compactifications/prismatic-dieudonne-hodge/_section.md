Let $C$ be an algebraically closed complete extension of $\Q_p$ with ring of
integers $\mathcal O_C$, and fix a compatible system $\varepsilon$ of
$p$-power roots of unity, with $\xi \in A_{\inf}$ the corresponding
generator of $\ker\theta$. Then there is a functor $M$ from $p$-divisible
groups over $\mathcal O_C$ to finite free $A_{\inf}$-modules of rank the
height, with a Frobenius-semilinear injection $\varphi_M$ whose cokernel is
killed by $\varphi(\xi)$, such that:

* $M(G)/\xi$ maps onto the de Rham realisation, with kernel exactly $\xi M$;
* there is an exact sequence $0 \to \omega_G \to D(G) \to \mathrm{Lie}(G^\vee)
  \to 0$ compatible with $M$;
* $M$ is fully faithful, and $M(\mu_{p^\infty})$ and $M(\Q_p/\Z_p)$ are free of
  rank one with the expected Frobenius and the period $\mu$ relating them.

This is the classification of Fargues and Scholze–Weinstein (2013), in the
prismatic form of Anschütz and Le Bras.
