Let $R$ be a complete noetherian normal local domain of characteristic zero,
with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and fraction field $K$. For every set of
degeneration data over $R$ (a split torus part with a pairing $\psi$, even,
with values in $\mathfrak m$ and tending to $0$ $\mathfrak m$-adically, and a
bimultiplicative $\tau$), there is a semi-abelian scheme $G$ over $R$ and a
polarised abelian variety $A$ over $K$, such that $A$ is the generic fibre of
$G$ and $G$ has a Mumford uniformisation by the given data.

This is Mumford's construction (1972), as completed by Faltings and Chai.
