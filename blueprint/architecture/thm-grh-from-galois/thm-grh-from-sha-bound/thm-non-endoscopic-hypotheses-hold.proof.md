Each group of hypotheses is a list of published results, each proved
separately. The local part uses the local converse theorem, the theory of
Bernstein and Zelevinsky, the classification of square-integrable and
tempered representations by segments, the theory of Moy and Prasad for
depth zero, and Jacquet–Shalika. The automorphy and Galois part uses
Clozel's purity theorem, base change from unitary groups
([thm-base-change-unitary]), $p$-adic Hodge theory of Hecke characters,
the Bloch–Kato local conditions and Greenberg–Wiles. The cohomology part
uses local and global Euler characteristic formulas, local Tate duality
and Poitou–Tate. The transport statements use Deligne–Ribet, ray class
fields and the units of Chevalley.
