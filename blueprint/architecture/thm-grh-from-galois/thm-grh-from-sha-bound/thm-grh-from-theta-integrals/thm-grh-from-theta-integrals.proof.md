The hypotheses give families of definite unitary groups with the
properties of [def-family-data] ([thm-family-data-exists]). With Green's
classification ([thm-green-cuspidal]) and the Tate–Shafarevich bound, these
families give the zero-free half-plane by [thm-zero-free-from-families].
