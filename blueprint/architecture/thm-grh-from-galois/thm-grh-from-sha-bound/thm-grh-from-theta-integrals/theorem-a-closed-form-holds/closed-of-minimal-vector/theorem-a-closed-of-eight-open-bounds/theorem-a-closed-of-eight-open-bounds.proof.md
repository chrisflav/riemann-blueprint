The bound on levels implies the bound on dimensions of the base blocks.
Together with the bounds on directions and on positive parts these are the
hypotheses of [open-e-of-open4], whose conclusion, with the freeness and
ratio conditions of the blocks, gives the remaining bounds; they add up to
the mean bound on the bad share.
