Suppose that, with the constants chosen in the order of
[theorem-a-closed-form-holds], every admitted local datum satisfies four
bounds on the blocks of the local construction: a bound on the levels of
the base blocks, an orthogonality bound, the range bound, and a bound on
the directions of the blocks. Then the conclusion of
[theorem-a-closed-form-holds] holds. The same is true with the first bound
replaced by a bound on the dimensions of the base blocks, or with the
bounds on directions or on positive parts in place of the others.
