*We read* the proof from the names of its steps. The mean value theorem is proved by following invariant factors along the
progressions: as $n_i$ moves along its progression, the elementary divisors
of the Fock Gram matrix change in a controlled way, so a vector can be bad
for only a bounded proportion of the steps. The block sum bound is the
mean value theorem applied to each product of ranges, with $C_M = 1$.
