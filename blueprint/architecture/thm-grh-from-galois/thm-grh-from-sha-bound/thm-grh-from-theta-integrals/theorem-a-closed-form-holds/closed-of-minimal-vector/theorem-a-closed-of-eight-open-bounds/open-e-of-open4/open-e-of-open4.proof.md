Take $C_M$ from [rest-tm-closed], which gives the bound on block sums. The
block bound for dual families follows from the valuation of blocks over
$\Z_p$, and the bounds on positive parts combine with the rank of the
lattices on digits. Thresholds that work for some constants work for larger
ones, so the constants can be chosen in the required order.
