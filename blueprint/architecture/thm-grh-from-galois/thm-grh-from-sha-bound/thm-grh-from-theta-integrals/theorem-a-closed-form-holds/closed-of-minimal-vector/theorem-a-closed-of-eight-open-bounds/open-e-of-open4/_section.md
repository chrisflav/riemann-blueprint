Suppose that, with the constants chosen in the order of
[theorem-a-closed-form-holds], every admitted local datum satisfies the
bound on the dimensions of the base blocks, the two bounds on positive
parts, and the bound on directions. Then there is a constant $C_M \ge 0$
such that every admitted local datum satisfies the full list of block
bounds with constant $C_M$: these four, the block bound for dual families,
and the bound on block sums of [rest-tm-closed].
