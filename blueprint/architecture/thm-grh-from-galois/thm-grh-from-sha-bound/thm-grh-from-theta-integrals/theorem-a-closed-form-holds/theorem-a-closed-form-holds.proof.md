Apply [closed-of-minimal-vector]. Its three inputs hold: every nonzero
symmetric $p$-adic form on a lattice has a vector of minimal norm that
splits off, a diagonalised pairing $X\,\mathrm{diag}(c)\,Y^{T} = p^D\cdot 1_n$
has at least $n$ diagonal entries of valuation at most $D$, and the bound on
ranges of exponents holds by counting.
