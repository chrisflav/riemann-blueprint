*We read this proof from the names of its steps.* Choose an idempotent of
the Hecke algebra and bump functions at good places. Decompose the
Eisenstein measure into cells, show that the singular cells are null, and
identify the residue measure with the theta measure cell by cell; the
Hasse principle for hermitian forms [hermitian-hasse-principle] enters in
the count of orbits. A cut-off argument with Fourier coefficients then
gives the vanishing of the higher Laurent coefficients and the identity
for the residue.
