Take the measured setting of [exists-setting]. The local compatibility
conditions give the full data, and the gap hypothesis gives decay of the
test function, hence its integrability. Finite dimensionality then gives
finiteness, and the identification with the forms holds index by index. *We read*
the step as an instance of local uniqueness of Bessel models.
