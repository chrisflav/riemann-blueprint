The intermediate constructions exist by
[all-stages-at-member-of-specializations-alt]. A weight-case condition comes
from the residue characters, and a fine adelic datum extends to larger
fields by the covering theorem for discrete series. These give finiteness and
the passage to a second, enlarged choice of norms. A system of local
parameters then gives depth-three partial constructions for $p$, and the
level-three upgrade holds both for $p$ and for the enlarged data. Apply
[exists-base-lattice-of-pair-alt] to the two choices of norms.
