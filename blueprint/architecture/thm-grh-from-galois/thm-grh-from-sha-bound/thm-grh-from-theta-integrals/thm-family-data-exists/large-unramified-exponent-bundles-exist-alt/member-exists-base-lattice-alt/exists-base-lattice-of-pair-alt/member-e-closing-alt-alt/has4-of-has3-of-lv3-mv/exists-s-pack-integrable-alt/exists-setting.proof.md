Under the gap hypothesis, Bessel coefficients do not vanish and weighted
local coefficients behave well. Build a lattice from the forms, the
character $\psi$, reference data and a block of functions at the real places
from the Bessel coefficients. Rescale the measures with the Siegel–Weil
formula. For the transports, Witt's theorem gives an isometry, which is then
matched to the genus and conjugated to the right level.
