The published results give the hypotheses for a compatible system of
members: Artin reciprocity, Weber's ray class fields, CM elliptic curves with
good reduction outside a prime, Harish-Chandra modules, discrete series and
their formal degrees, and the Siegel–Weil formula. The mass ratio is one of
these hypotheses. A second group of results gives rational weights and
multiplicity one for the members: Harish-Chandra's analyticity, the
factorisations of Flath and of Dixmier–Malliavin, the geometric lemma,
Green's classification, Siegel Eisenstein series, the doubling method and
Schur orthogonality, together with the rationality of test vectors.
[member-exists-base-lattice-alt] then gives membership for the data with
origin $c$. Imposing $A$ keeps membership, because $A$ holds for all large
auxiliary data. A non-vanishing statement for the blocks of the Weil
representation gives the admissible data.
