Assume the published results on unitary groups listed in
[thm-grh-from-theta-integrals], and fix a system of local parameters
([def-local-parameters]) and a real constant $c$. Build the families of
definite unitary groups from the construction data that these parameters
and $c$ determine. Then, for all large auxiliary fields, the twelve basic
conditions hold at every admissible datum of these families: primitivity,
the $\lambda'$ condition, the divisor condition, decomposition points,
selection jumps, compatibility conditions, labels, goodness, weak geometric compatibility,
multiplicities, the $\kappa$ condition and the defect condition.
