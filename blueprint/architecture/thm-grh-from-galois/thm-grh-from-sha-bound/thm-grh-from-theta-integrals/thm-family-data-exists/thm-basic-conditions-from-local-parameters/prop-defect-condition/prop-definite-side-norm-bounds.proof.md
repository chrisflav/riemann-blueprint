Identify the data of the admissible datum with the kernel data of the
member: the fields, primes, characters, the choice of places above each
prime and the twisting characters all match. The norm bounds are then the
bounds for kernel data of [lem-norm-bounds-from-kernel-sizes], applied with
the frame given by the characters of the second coordinates.
