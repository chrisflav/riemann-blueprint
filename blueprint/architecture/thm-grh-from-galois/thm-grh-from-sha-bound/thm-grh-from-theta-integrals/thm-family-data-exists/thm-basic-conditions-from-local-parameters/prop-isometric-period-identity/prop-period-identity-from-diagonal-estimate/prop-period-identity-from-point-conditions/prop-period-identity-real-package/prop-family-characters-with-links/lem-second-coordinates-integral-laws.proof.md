A seesaw argument gives the frames of lattices and the finite integral
laws, and so the units, operations and exponents among the constants; it
uses the theory of discrete series ([thm-discrete-series-parametrisation],
[thm-hc-modules-unitary]) and strong approximation for special unitary
groups ([thm-strong-approximation-su]). The remaining scalars are non-zero
constants times roots of unity. Apply the hypothesis to these constants.
The Weil representation attached to a Gram matrix of rank one then gives
the characters, and a level identity for quadratic families gives the
integral laws and the remaining conditions.
