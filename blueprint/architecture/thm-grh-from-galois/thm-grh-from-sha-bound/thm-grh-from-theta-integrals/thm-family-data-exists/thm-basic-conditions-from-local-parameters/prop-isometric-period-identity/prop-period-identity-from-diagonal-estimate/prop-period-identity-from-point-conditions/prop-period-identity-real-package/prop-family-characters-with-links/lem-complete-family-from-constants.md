Let $W$ be a real package satisfying all family
conditions, $a$ a geometric family for $W$ at $u$ whose top intertwiner is
the identity matrix, and fix constants for $a$: units $\varepsilon$,
bilinear operations, exponents $r, e$, and scalars $c_G$ and $s_t$. Take a
choice of first coordinates that is the same at all points. Then there are
a choice of second coordinates, vectors $w_0$ and $w$ and a family $F$ such
that the completed family satisfies the condition on second coordinates, and its output
matches the given constants: same places, the integral laws, levels,
rational vectors, the twisting data $\varepsilon$ and theta combinations, and
unit ratios of the constants against the base point $\mu_0$.
