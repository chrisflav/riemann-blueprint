At a good datum the conditions at points show that the attached real
package satisfies all family conditions. The diagonal estimate then shows
that the projections $P_u$ do not vanish on the ramified part. With
$r_S \ge 1$ these are the hypotheses of
[prop-period-identity-real-package].
