1. Unramified Howe duality, the Siegel–Weil formula, the doubling method
   and unramified doubling for $\GL_n$ show that the theta maps along each
   chain of localizations are surjective.
2. With this surjectivity, [prop-family-characters-with-links] gives a
   central element $z$ and, for each $u$, a family character with a bottom
   datum pinned at $z$ that has the required links.
3. The bottom datum serves the package. The classification of discrete
   series and the uniqueness of hermitian discrete series then turn the
   non-vanishing of $P_u$ into the constants $a_u \ne 0$ of the identity.
