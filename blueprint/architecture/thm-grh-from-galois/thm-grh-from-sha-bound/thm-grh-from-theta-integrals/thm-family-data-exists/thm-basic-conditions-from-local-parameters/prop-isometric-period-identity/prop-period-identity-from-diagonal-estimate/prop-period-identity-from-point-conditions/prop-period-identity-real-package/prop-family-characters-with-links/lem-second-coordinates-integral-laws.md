Let $W$, $a$ and the first coordinates be as in
[lem-complete-family-from-constants], and suppose the conclusion of that
lemma holds for every choice of constants. Then there is a choice of
second coordinates for which the family is admissible, satisfies the
integral laws, the coordinate map vanishes at zero, the link has the tail property,
the links commute, and the ratio condition holds.
