For each point $t$ choose a quadratic extension read off from $a$, and
take the relation between first coordinates, the volume data and a kernel
that $a$ provides. Local twist values give finite combinations of theta
functions. The joint twist relation then fixes the remaining scalars. The
group of characters involved has one element, so the scalar at the top is
determined. Define the second coordinates from these coefficients and check
the output conditions one at a time.
