The family conditions give a bottom datum and a central element. For each
$u$ they also give a geometric family, and the rapid decay of cusp forms
gives a choice of first coordinates that is the same at all points.
[lem-complete-family-from-constants] completes this choice for every
choice of constants. [lem-second-coordinates-integral-laws] then gives a
choice of second coordinates with the integral laws, the vanishing at
zero, the tail property, commutation and the ratio condition. Descent comes
from the choice of second coordinates. Surjectivity comes from the
hypothesis on theta maps.
