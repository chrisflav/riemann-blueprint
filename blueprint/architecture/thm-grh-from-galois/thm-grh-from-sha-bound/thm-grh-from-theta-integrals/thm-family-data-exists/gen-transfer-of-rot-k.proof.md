From the rotated gauge condition, the radial gauge move, the constant
gauge, a normal form for the curvature and the integrality and reality of
the gauge, obtain a trace identity for the reference curvature, then the
reference connection, then the gauge connection. With the gauge function
and the second shift of the gauge this gives the transfer.
