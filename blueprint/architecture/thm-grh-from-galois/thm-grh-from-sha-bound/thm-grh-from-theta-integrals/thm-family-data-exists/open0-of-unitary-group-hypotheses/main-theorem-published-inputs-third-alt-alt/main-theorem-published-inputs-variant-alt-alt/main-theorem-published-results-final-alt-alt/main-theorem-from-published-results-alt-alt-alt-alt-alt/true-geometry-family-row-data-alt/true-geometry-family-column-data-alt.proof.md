A seesaw argument gives the frames of lattices and the finite integral
laws, and so the units, operations and exponents among the constants. The
remaining scalars are non-zero constants times roots of unity. Apply the
hypothesis to these constants. The Weil representation attached to a Gram
matrix of rank one then gives the characters, and a level identity for
quadratic families gives the integral laws and the remaining conditions.
