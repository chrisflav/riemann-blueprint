Suppose that for every datum of [thm-bessel-transform-radial-space] and every real
parameter $Y$ with $Y - 1, Y, Y + 1$ in the admissible set, the composite of
the level operators $G\,\xi(Y)\,R(Y)$ is the radial part of an invariant
differential operator on $G$, and that the same holds for the adjoint
action. Then the conclusion of [thm-bessel-transform-radial-space] holds.
