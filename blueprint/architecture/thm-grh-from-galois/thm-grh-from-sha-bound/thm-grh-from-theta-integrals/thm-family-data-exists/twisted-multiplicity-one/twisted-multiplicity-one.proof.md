The family hypotheses give a weak characterisation of the representations
involved. Compare the traces of test functions that are pseudo-coefficients
of the discrete series at infinity
([local-pseudo-coefficient-existence-unitary-group]) and agree at the
finite places through $\Phi$. Reduction theory on Siegel sets
[siegel-set-unitary] and the Borel–Casselman theorem control the
continuous spectrum, and linear independence of characters, with a
polynomial ring argument of Radford, separates the representations.
