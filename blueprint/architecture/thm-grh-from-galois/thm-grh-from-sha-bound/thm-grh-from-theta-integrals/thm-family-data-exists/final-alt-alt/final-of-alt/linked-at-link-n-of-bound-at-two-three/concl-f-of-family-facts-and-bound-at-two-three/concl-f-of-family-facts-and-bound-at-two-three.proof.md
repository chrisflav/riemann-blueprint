Combine four local results. The local lifting theorem
([theorem-e-corollary-a-closing-all-data]) supplies the constants. The
intermediate range is [star-ad-and-bb-le-of-joint-cond], the large primes
are [eventually-cong-bound-of-facts], and the primes $q, d$ are
[embedding-jumps-aux2-of-setup]. At $2$ and $3$ the bound is the
hypothesis; at the other small primes it follows in the same way. The
lattices come from the theorem of Bhatt and Scholze, and the global index
theorem for lattices
([global-lattice-index-theorem-of-length-bound-at-places]) assembles the
local lengths.
