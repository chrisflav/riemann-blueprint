Assume global Artin reciprocity, the local theory of Bloch and Kato, local
Tate duality and the local Euler characteristic formula. Let $C \ge 2080$ and
let $Q$ be a polynomial with $Q(f, r, t) \ge 260 f^2t^2(r+t)$. Suppose the
Galois representations $r_\pi$ of a member and their parts $\rho_\pi$
satisfy a list of local conditions: an index condition away from the types,
Iwahori admissibility, small Selmer groups at the twisted places, Hodge–Tate
weights in windows at $q$ and $d$, compatibility of Fontaine's functor with
sums, tame generators at $q$ and $d$, and a polynomial bound on the
parameters. Then the congruence bound at $q$ and $d$ of
[embedding-jumps-aux2-of-setup] holds with $C$ and $Q$, together with two
auxiliary conclusions which we have not unfolded.
