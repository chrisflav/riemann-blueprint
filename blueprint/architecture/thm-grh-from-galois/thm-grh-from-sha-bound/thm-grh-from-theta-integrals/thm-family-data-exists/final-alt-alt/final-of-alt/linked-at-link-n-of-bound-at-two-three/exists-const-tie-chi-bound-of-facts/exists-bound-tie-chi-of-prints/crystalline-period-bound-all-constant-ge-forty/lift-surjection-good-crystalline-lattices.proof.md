Both lattices come from Breuil–Kisin-type modules by the theorem of Bhatt
and Scholze. A finite class of $T_2$ is an extension of crystalline
lattices; lift the extension of modules through the surjection and bound the
denominator by the weights and the periods. *We read* the proof only through
the names of its steps.
