At inert primes, the local lifting bound
[local-lifting-theorem-b-of-bloch-kato-tate-duality-euler-char] bounds the
cokernels of the maps on finite cohomology. When the coefficients are
ramified, [prv-twenty-five-of-bloch-kato-tate-duality-euler-char] does the
same. Local Tate duality and the Euler characteristic formula turn these
cokernels into the Selmer and local lengths, and Chebotarev's theorem and
the global index theorem for lattices sum them.
