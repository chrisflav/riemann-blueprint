At large $l$ the lattices are crystalline with small weights compared with
$l$. The description of finite classes of twists in
[bloch-kato-top-of-local-tate-duality-euler-char] then identifies the
congruence module with a Selmer group up to the Tate–Shafarevich groups and
the class group. The lattices come from the theorem of Bhatt and Scholze,
and global Artin reciprocity handles the characters $\chi_c$.
