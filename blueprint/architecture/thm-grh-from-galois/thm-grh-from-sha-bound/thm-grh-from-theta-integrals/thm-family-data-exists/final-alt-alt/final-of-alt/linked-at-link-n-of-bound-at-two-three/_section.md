Assume the $l$-adic inputs ([hypotheses2]) and the cutting hypothesis of
[final-of-alt]. Let $\mathcal F$ be a family with good twists whose members
satisfy, from some scale on, the Galois conditions and the character
conditions at $g_0, g_1$, and which satisfies the bound at $2$ and $3$. Then
$\mathcal F$ satisfies the Tate–Shafarevich error bound
([def-sha-error-term]). The same holds for the classes of families of
[def-family-data] built from the local parameters.
