Build models of the Weil representations for the separating data, extend the
separating family by a twist using a see-saw pair, and obtain a tower of
multiplicity spaces along the first row. The theta integrals along this
tower converge and decay rapidly ([cusp-rapid-decay]). From
[real-input-e-holds], with Harish-Chandra modules [harish-chandra-modules]
and the Casselman–Wallach theorem [casselman-wallach], the real local
periods do not vanish. Unramified Howe duality, the Siegel–Weil formula
[weil-unitary], the doubling method
[doubling-method-local-zeta-integrals], [unramified-doubling-gl] and the
discrete series results make the twisting map onto. These give a weak
separation by real functionals; the non-vanishing of Bessel periods up the
tower ([bessel-period-nonvanishing-ascent-of-depth-data]) and a gap
condition on the sizes then upgrade it to separation by Bessel periods,
which is the claim.
