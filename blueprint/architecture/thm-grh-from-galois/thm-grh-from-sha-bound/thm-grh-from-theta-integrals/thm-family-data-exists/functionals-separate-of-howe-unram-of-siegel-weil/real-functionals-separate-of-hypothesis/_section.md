Assume the published results of [thm-grh-from-theta-integrals], unramified
Howe duality, the Siegel–Weil formula in its convergent range
([published-siegel-weil-unitary-convergent]), and the statement on matrix
coefficients of discrete series of [real-input-e-holds]. Then the
conclusion of [functionals-separate-of-howe-unram-of-siegel-weil] holds:
theta integrals detect every nonzero vector.
