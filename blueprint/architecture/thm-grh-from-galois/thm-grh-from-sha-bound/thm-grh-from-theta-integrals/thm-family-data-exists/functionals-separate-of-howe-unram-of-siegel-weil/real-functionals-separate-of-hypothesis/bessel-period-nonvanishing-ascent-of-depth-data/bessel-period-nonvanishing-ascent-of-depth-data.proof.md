By [exists-good-grid-frames-of-depth-fixed] the chain of hermitian spaces
admits frames that end at the given volume and are good for some lattice.
Along good frames the period of each step unfolds into the period of the
next, with a nonzero factor, so non-vanishing passes from $\Psi_0$ to
$\Psi_m$ one step at a time. The Hasse norm theorem [hasse-norm-st] enters
in choosing the frames.
