Howe duality at unramified places and the Siegel–Weil formula show that
the relevant functionals separate representations. A test-vector
construction gives rational rows: it uses Siegel sets, real Howe duality,
Green's characters and the functional equation. Twisted Weil
representations, matched local factors and equal twisted theta integrals
give a Fourier-coefficient fit on a grid of evaluation points. Large
unramified exponents and the covolume bound then produce the families.

Local Tate duality, the local Euler characteristic formula, Artin
reciprocity, the theory of discrete series and the Siegel mass formula
supply the remaining conditions. The local bounds at $2$ and $3$ come from
the local parameters. The covolume bound comes from arithmetic intersection
theory: the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorem, Moriwaki's bigness theorem,
the Poincaré–Lelong formula, toroidal compactifications, a theorem of Zarhin and
an exact sequence of Fargues.
