The reference covolume identity holds; it is proved separately, from the
covolumes of the fundamental domains. It gives the archimedean volume
identity ([lem-archimedean-volume-identity]). The Siegel–Weil formula
([thm-siegel-main-theorem-hermitian]) gives the volumes of the stabilisers.
Then [lem-counting-constant-from-archimedean-volume] gives the equality of
constants.
