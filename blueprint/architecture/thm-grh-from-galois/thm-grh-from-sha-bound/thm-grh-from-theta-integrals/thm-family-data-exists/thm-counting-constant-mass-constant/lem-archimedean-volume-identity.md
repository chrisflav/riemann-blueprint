In the setting of [thm-counting-constant-mass-constant], for every positive
constant $a'$ allowed by the archimedean clause,
$$c_{\mathrm{dis}}\,\mu_H(H_\infty)\,\mu_N(\mathcal F)\,\mu_N(\mathcal F') =
a'\,2^{k[F:\Q]}\Big(\frac{\pi^k}{(k-1)!}\Big)^{[F:\Q]} T_u^{-k[F:\Q]}\,|d_F|^{e(k,r)},$$
where $c_{\mathrm{dis}}$ is the constant of the disintegration of Haar
measure, $\mathcal F, \mathcal F'$ are fundamental domains for the
unipotent groups and $T_u$ is the size attached to the member $u$.
