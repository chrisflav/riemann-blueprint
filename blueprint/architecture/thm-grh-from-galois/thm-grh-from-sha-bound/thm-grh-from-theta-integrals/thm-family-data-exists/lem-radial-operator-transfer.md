Keep the setting of [thm-bessel-transform-radial-space], with $k \ge 2$. Suppose the
*rotated gauge condition* holds: every endomorphism of the radial space
that commutes with the gauge operators and acts as a sum of diagonal
Euler operators plus a constant $s$ is carried by each admissible frame to
an explicit first-order operator on the model. Then for every admissible
level structure there is a frame $e$ whose transfer map intertwines each of
the level operators (the operators $\zeta^{\pm 1}$, $\bar\zeta^{\pm 1}$,
$\Gamma^{\pm 1}$, $\nabla$, $\bar\nabla$ and the rest) with an explicit
operator on a model space of functions.

*It is not clear* what the model space is; *we read* the statement as
identifying the radial system with an explicit system of differential
equations in a few variables.
