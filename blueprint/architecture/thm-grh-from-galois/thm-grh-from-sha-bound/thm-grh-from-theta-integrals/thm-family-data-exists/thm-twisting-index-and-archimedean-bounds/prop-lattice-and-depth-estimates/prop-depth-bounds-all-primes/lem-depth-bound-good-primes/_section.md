Assume, for every prime $p$, the following *estimate at unramified
primes*. Let $T$ be a complete local noetherian domain of characteristic
zero in which $p$ is not a unit, $A$ a polarised abelian scheme over $T$ with
an action of the ring of integers $\mathcal O$ of a CM field, and $F$ a
formal group law of dimension $d$ giving coordinates on the formal group of
$A$, compatible with the action. Let $p \ne 2$ not divide the discriminant
of $\mathcal O$. Then at a place of $\mathcal O$ above $p$, eigenbases for
the action of $\mathcal O$, taken over the valuation ring of an
algebraically closed field, have valuations bounded as prescribed.

Then there is $C_0$ such that the depth bound at good primes of
[prop-lattice-and-depth-estimates] holds at every point satisfying the conditions of
[prop-depth-bounds-all-primes].
