Pass to the points of the integral model of a unitary Shimura variety. Three
local statements about the formal group of an abelian variety with an action
of $\mathcal O_E$ hold at every prime $p$: a colength bound at split places,
a Hodge–Tate inclusion, and an estimate at unramified primes. The third gives
the bound at good primes by [lem-depth-bound-good-primes]. The
bounds at $q$, $d$ and the level have separate proofs, and the bound at $2$
uses the first two statements.
