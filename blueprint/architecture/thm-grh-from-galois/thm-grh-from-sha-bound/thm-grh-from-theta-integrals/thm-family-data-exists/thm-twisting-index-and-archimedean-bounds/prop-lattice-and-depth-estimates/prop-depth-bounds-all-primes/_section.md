There is a constant $C_0$ such that the four depth bounds of
[prop-lattice-and-depth-estimates] (at good primes, at $q$ and $d$, at $2$, and at the
primes of the level) hold at every point of a family that satisfies the
recursive conditions, provided that the scale satisfies $x \ge 3$ and
$x^{\varepsilon/5} \ge 3$, that $k b' \le b$, that $C \ge C_0$ and a weight
constant is at least $10$, and that the local numbers of the point are those
of its data.
