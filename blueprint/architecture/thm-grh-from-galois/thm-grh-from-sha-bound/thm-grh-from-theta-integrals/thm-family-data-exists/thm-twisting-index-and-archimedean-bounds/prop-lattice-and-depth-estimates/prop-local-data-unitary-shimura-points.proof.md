Each kind of data has its own construction from the estimate at unramified
primes for the formal group of an abelian scheme with an action of
$\mathcal O_E$, which bounds the valuations of eigenbases of that action at
the primes $p \ne 2$ prime to the discriminant. The last two kinds are
[lem-orbit-data-inert-primes].
