There are a constant $C_L$ and functions $C_q, Z_0$ with the following
property. At every point of a family, every prime $p$ and every index $j$
in range, suppose the parameter conditions of [prop-height-inequalities-at-points] and the
size conditions hold, and $Z_0(C_A, C_K)$ is at most the base size. Then
the inputs of the height inequality hold with constants $C_L$ and
$C_q(C_A, C_K)$. These inputs are a base condition, a net of points, global
family data and local point data with height bounds. A support condition
holds as well.
