In the situation of [lem-height-inequality-inputs],
suppose the function fields are algebraically closed, $C_L > 0$ bounds the
logarithmic sup norms from below, and the global family data $G$ have good
generators, a basis, the expected compatibility conditions, and an invariant $\delta$ with
$\log\delta \le 1041\,Z_b^3$, where $Z_b$ is a size parameter. Suppose also
that a combined bound holds. Then for every prime $\lambda$ of the
coefficient field there are local point data at $\lambda$ that lie on a
curve, are tied to the scalars, lift, and satisfy height bounds.
