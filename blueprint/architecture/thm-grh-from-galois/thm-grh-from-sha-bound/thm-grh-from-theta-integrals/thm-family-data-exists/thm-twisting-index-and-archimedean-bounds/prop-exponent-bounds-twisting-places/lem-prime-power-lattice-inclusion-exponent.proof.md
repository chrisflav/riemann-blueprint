Inside the weight range, the local models give the inclusion with exponent
$J^{\mathrm{coarse}}(l)$. For this, the exponents are independent of the
Frobenius data, the links give weight inputs, and an inclusion with weight
$7$ holds. Outside the range, use the given inclusion. In both cases the
formula for $J^{\mathrm{rec}}$ gives the bound. The proof also uses the
subadditivity of exponents of Weyl-module lattices under cutting a
partition, which comes from their exact exponents.
