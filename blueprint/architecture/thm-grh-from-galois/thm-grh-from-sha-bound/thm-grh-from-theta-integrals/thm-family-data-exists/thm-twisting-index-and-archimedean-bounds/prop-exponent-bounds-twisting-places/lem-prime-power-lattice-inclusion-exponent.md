Let $K$ be a number field with an involution, $w$ a finite place of $K$
over the prime $l$, and $\Lambda \subseteq \Lambda'$ lattices of forms
attached to a subspace $Z$. Let $J^{\exp}(w)$ be the exponent of the
inclusion $l^J\Lambda \subseteq \Lambda'_w$, divided by the ramification
index. Let $J^A(l)$ be the exponent prescribed by the weights and
$J^{\mathrm{coarse}}(l)$ a coarser weight bound, and put
$J^{\mathrm{rec}}(l) = J^A(l)$ if $l$ is small, excluded or special, and
$J^{\mathrm{rec}}(l) = J^{\mathrm{coarse}}(l)$ otherwise. Suppose that the
weights satisfy the numerical conditions of the construction, that
outside the weight range the inclusion holds with
exponent $J^A(l)$, and that inside it local models cover the forms. Then
$$J^{\exp}(w) \le J^{\mathrm{rec}}(l).$$
