Assume the theorem of Bhatt and Scholze on crystalline lattices and
Chebotarev's theorem for primes of degree one. Suppose that at the twisting
places a smallness condition, an integrality condition on the coefficient
rings, and a Galois condition hold. Let $w$ be a twisting place whose residue
characteristic $l$ lies in the range where the weights matter. Then $w$ has
a local model: Frobenius and conjugation data, and finitely many Hecke
modules over local coefficient rings in which $l$ is not a unit. These
modules cover the relevant space of forms, and each is linked to the weight
data at $l$.
