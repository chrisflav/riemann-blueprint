Take for $J_j(l)$ the recursive exponent $J^{\mathrm{rec}}_j(l)$ of
[lem-prime-power-lattice-inclusion-exponent]. Each $J_j(w)$ is bounded by the exponent of the lattice
inclusion at $w$, which [lem-prime-power-lattice-inclusion-exponent] bounds by $J^{\mathrm{rec}}_j(p_w)$.
A bound for sums of exponents over a set of primes, which uses the cutting
inequality, gives $\sum_{l \in Q}J_j(l)\log l \le E_J$.
