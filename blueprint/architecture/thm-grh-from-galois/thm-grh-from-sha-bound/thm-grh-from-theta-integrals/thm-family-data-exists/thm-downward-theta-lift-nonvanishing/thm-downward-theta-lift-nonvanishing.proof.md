Rapid decay and the unramified doubling integral give the Euler product of
the doubling zeta integral far to the right, and continuation of the
partial Hecke $L$-function gives it a simple pole. The doubling method, with
the continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series
[thm-siegel-eisenstein-continuation] and, as we read it, the residue identity
[thm-siegel-eisenstein-residue-theta], turns the pole into a nonzero downward theta lift. Howe duality
at unramified places shows that the lift of an eigenform is an eigenform
and that lifts generate at the finite places; generation at the real
places is separate. Detection follows from non-vanishing and generation,
and injectivity from detection.
