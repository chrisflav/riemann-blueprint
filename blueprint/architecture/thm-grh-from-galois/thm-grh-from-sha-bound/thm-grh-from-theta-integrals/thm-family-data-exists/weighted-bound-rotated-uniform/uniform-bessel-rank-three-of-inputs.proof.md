The threshold $t(n, \varepsilon)$ of the branching argument is at most
$43 n^3$. Uniqueness of discrete series with large gaps, the infinitesimal
characters of discrete series, smoothness of $K$-finite vectors and the
asymptotics of Bessel coefficients of equal and different types give a
bound for Bessel periods on a grid of points. The continuity of Capelli
coefficients then turns it into the weighted bound. The radial analysis of
[actual-compact-theorem] and [gen-transfer-of-rot-k] enters through the
asymptotics.
