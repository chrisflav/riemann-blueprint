The family hypotheses give a weak characterisation of the representations
involved. Compare the traces of test functions that are pseudo-coefficients
of the discrete series at infinity
([thm-pseudo-coefficients-unitary]) and agree at the
finite places through $\Phi$. Reduction theory on Siegel sets
[thm-reduction-theory-unitary] and the Borel–Casselman theorem
[thm-borel-casselman] control the continuous spectrum, and linear
independence of characters, with a polynomial ring argument of Radford
[thm-radford-shuffle], separates the representations. The comparison uses
Harish-Chandra's analyticity and finiteness theorems
([thm-hc-analyticity-density], [thm-hc-finiteness-unitary]), the
classification of discrete series [thm-hc-discrete-series-classification],
rapid decay and trace class of cusp forms ([thm-cusp-rapid-decay],
[thm-cusp-trace-class]), the factorisations of Dixmier–Malliavin
[thm-dixmier-malliavin] and Flath [thm-flath-unramified],
Bernstein–Zelevinsky finiteness [thm-bz-finiteness], the geometric lemma
[thm-geometric-lemma] and Green's classification [thm-green-parametrisation].
