Integrality is checked on the integral models, using semistable reduction
([semistable-reduction-st], [part-i-of-semistable-reduction]). The
transformation law follows from the point laws of Hecke operators, which
come from the transport of theta functions. The formula for the values
uses a linear model at the split prime and a vector fixed by the level
group. *We read* these steps from their names.
