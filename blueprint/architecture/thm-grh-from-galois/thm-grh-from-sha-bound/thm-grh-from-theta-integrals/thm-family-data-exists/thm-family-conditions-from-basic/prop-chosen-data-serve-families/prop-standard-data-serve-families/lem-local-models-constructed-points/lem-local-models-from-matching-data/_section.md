Assume the admissibility of theta data at inert primes and the existence of
the needed $l$-adic CM curves. Let a kernel datum be given, with matching
local data at every place for every tuple $\mu$ in use, with the twisted
forms normalised at the reference tuple $\mu_0$, with trivial stabilisers at
step zero, and with $2r_G \le k$. Then the twisted forms have local models
as in [lem-local-models-constructed-points].
