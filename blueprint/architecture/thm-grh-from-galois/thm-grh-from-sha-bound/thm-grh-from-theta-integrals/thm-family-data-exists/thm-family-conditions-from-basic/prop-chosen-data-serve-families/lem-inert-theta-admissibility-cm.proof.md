The admissibility follows from the first kernel law for small lattice
chains, which holds once CM curves have good models; they do, because
[singular moduli are integral and CM curves have good reduction](@thm-cm-j-integral-good-reduction)
at the primes in question. *It is not clear* from
the statements how the good models enter that law.
