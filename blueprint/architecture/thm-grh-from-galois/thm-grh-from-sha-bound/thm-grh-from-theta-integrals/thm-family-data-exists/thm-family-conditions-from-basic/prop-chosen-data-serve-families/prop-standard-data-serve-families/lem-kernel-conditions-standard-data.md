Assume the first four facts of
[prop-standard-data-serve-families]. Every datum with a
standard kernel satisfies, eventually in $x$, the *kernel conditions*: the
scalars of the kernel pairs are units; the kernel map is rational; the kernel
class sums carry the forms of the chain complex; the stabilisers at step zero
are trivial; at a prime inert in $K$ with non-conjugate Frobenius the
Frobenius conjugation conditions hold; the jets of the kernel are integral;
the theta series of the kernel datum have the expected shape at $d$; and the
orbits of the standard series at inert places are bounded.
