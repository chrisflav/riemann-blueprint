Pass from the matching datum to its dictionary of lattice tuples. This shows
that the pairs of lattice tuples are admissible, that the place gives
embeddings of positions, that the arithmetic group moves frames, that the
series do not depend on places away from $w$ over $l$, and that powers of
the series scalars are integral. These give the kernel law and the law for
the stabiliser of a fixed coordinate. Extend $w$ to an isomorphism
$\Qbar \to \C_l$ and apply the admissibility of theta data at inert primes
([lem-inert-theta-admissibility-cm]), checking that the
frame values are $l$-adic units.
