A shape bound that holds uniformly in each family gives $x_0(\varepsilon)$
beyond which the splitting bound is at least $3$. Above $x_0$ each condition
is proved at the standard construction underlying the datum. The
algebraicity of translates gives the theta inputs; the genus-one models,
the dictionary and integrality at inert primes give an inert prime with
non-conjugate Frobenius and an admissible kernel series there. The remaining
conditions follow from the size bounds of the construction.
