For any construction data and any choice of admissible data, the following
six conditions hold at the good data of the families, for all large
auxiliary fields:

1. on the reference lattice chain of each member the period map is
   injective, the stabiliser maps are bijective, and the periods are
   rational, integral, and bounded by the majorants of the lattices;
2. the twisted points are integral;
3. the periods at the reference tuple lie in the field of definition, with
   valuation at least $-u_w$ at every place $w$, for explicit $u_w$;
4. the same holds for the twisted periods, at places outside the exceptional
   set of the tuple;
5. the conditions at the real places hold: the forms are symmetric and
   their characters are of finite order;
6. the valuation bounds at the level primes of
   [lem-valuations-level-primes] hold.

*We read* the periods as the values of the period pairings attached to the
members; *it is not clear* which classical periods they are.
