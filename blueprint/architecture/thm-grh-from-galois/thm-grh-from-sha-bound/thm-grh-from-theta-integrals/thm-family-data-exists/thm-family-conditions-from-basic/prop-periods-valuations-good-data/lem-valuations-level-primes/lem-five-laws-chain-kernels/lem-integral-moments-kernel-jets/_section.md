Let a member carry a CM datum: an ideal $\mathfrak c$ of $O_K$, a twelfth
root $\delta$ of its discriminant and a character $\chi$. Let $\ell$ be a
level prime prime to $N\mathfrak c$, and $\iota : K \to \C_\ell$ an embedding
inducing a place $w$. Then there are $\ell$-adic units $u_\tau$ such that the
jet sums of the theta kernels lie in the closure of the integral
combinations of the moments of the point masses at the support points, with
an extra factor $\ell^{-fN_i}$ for the dual points.
