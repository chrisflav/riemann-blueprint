Let $D$ be a datum whose hermitian space has the right signature, with local
models at the finite places $p$ agreeing with given elements $\delta_p$. For
types $u, u'$ with $T_u \equiv T_{u'} \bmod g^2$ (with $g$ an invariant of
the lattice of $D$) satisfying the prime hypothesis, and for every flag and
set of representative lattices, three inputs of [prop-pair-coefficient-local-decomposition] hold:
the local steps of the product decomposition, stable approximation of the
pair $(u,u')$, and a global patching of frames.
