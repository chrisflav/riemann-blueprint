Combine the compactly supported form of the archimedean theorem
([thm-bessel-transform-radial-space]), the existence of archimedean models for admissible settings
([thm-archimedean-models]), the uniform archimedean bounds
([prop-three-archimedean-inputs]) and the limit formula for energies
([lem-cutoff-energy-limit]). A lifting statement at level $3$ and the uniqueness of
Bessel models with a large gap supply the side conditions at the real
places.
