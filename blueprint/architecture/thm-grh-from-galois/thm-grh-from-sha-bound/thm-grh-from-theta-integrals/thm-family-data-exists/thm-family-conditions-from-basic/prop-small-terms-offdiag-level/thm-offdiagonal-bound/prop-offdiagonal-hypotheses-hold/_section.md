Assume the archimedean model data ([prop-archimedean-model-data]), the bound on non-generic
terms, the finite-dimensionality of spaces of automorphic forms cut out by
discrete series, the uniqueness of Bessel models for discrete series with a
large gap in the Harish-Chandra parameter, and the Siegel–Weil mass data.
Then all nineteen hypotheses of [lem-offdiagonal-bound-from-hypotheses] hold.
