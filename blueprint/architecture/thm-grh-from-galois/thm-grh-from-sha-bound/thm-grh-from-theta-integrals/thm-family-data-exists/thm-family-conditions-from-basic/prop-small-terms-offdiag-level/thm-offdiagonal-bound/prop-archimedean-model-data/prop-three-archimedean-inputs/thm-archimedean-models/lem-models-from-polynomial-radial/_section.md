Suppose the transported test functions are smooth, and that the shift
operators of the archimedean model act radially by polynomials: for
$K$-types $\pi \to \rho$ there are $n$ and a polynomial $q$ with
$$(|\zeta|^2 - T)^n\,S(\zeta) = q\big(\sqrt{|\zeta|^2 - T}\big)\,\theta(\zeta)$$
on the $\rho$-component. Then archimedean models for admissible settings exist
([thm-archimedean-models]).
