Stable approximation gives a representative of each term at every tuple of
places. The fibre counts are functorial and orbits of the zero terms are
controlled, so the fibres decompose. Representatives of the norms exist and
move correctly between $u$ and $u'$. The local partner counts
([lem-local-partner-counts]), the finiteness of the local counts, the
approximate product decomposition of the volumes and the global patching
of frames then give the identities. Finiteness of the eligible terms
completes the proof.
