The core is an abstract bound on a pairing over a CM field: split
$K_0(u,u')$ into large, non-scalar, non-generic and remaining terms. If the
first three are small, the local model, the energy identity and the
densities bound the rest by $(1+b^{-6})\,d_u d_{u'}/D^*$. The other steps
check that the data of $D$, with the archimedean model data, satisfy the
hypotheses of this abstract bound, and use the bound on non-generic terms.
