Let $D$ be a datum with local models $M_p$, and $p$ a finite place. Take
generic local data $t_1$ for a flag of length $r+1$, frames $f_2, f_2'$ and
isometries $\varphi, \varphi'$ between the spans, meeting the local counting
hypotheses. Suppose the Gram matrices of $t_1$ and of $(f_2, f_2')$ have
$LU$-decompositions $u_L\,Z\,u_U$, with unipotent $u_L, u_U$, through the
same model matrix up to the kind of the point. Then the number of lattices
compatible with these data equals the number of isometries between the
classes of the two Gram matrices, each shifted by a corner term.
