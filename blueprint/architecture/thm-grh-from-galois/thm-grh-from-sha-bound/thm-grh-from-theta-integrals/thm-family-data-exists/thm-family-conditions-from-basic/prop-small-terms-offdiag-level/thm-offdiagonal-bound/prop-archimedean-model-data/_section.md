There are constants $C_4 \ge 1$ and $C_{A,0}$ with the following property.
For every $C_A \ge C_{A,0}$ and all size constants, for every datum $D$ over a
CM field whose sizes are large enough, all types $u \ne u'$, and every
identification of the infinite places of $D$ with its embeddings, there are
archimedean model data for $(D, u, u')$. These consist of, at each real
place, finite-dimensional hermitian spaces isometric to the local
components of $u$ and $u'$, a tower of levels, test functions and a model
in which the local integrals of $D$ become energy forms; together with the
real inputs of the abstract off-diagonal bound with constant $C_4$.
