Three archimedean statements enter: the compactly supported form of the
archimedean theorem ([thm-bessel-transform-radial-space]), the existence of
archimedean models for admissible settings ([thm-archimedean-models]), and
uniform bounds at admissible archimedean settings. The last follows from
the first: for every constant $C_b$ there are constants $C_c, C_4$ such
that at every setting of leading forms over a CM field the positivity
statement, integrality, the curvature barriers by place, the lifting
thresholds and a concrete form of the sum with constant $C_4$ hold.
Combine these with the limit formula for energies
([lem-cutoff-energy-limit]). A lifting statement at level $3$ and the
uniqueness of Bessel models with a large gap supply the side conditions at
the real places.
