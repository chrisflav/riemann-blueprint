Suppose that twisted theta lifts along the grid, with the chosen test
functions, send cusp forms to cusp forms. Then for all large scales and
every choice of test data, every arrow $A \to B$ of the grid, every
idempotent sum at the local places, every local vector $f$ attached to the
arrow and every polynomial $Q$ in the Fock model, the following holds: for
each listed cusp form $F$ on $\U(A)$ the theta integral of $F$ against the
Fock vector of $(f, Q)$ converges, and the lift is a listed cusp form on
$\U(B)$.
