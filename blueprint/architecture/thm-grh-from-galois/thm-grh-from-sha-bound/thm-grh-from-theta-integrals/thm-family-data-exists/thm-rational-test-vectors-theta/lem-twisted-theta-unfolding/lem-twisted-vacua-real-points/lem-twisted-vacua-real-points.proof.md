Combine [lem-twisted-vacua-base-comparison], which gives the
comparison with the base vacua up to a constant $\kappa$, with the fact
that the unfolded vacua are real up to the twist and with the description
of the supports of the test vectors by staircase cosets. Terms with zero
expansion contribute nothing.
