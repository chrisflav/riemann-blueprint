For the spaces of automorphic forms on the definite and indefinite unitary
groups of the members:

* *integrality:* the Hecke operators preserve the integral structures
  given by the lattices, both on the forms of good type and on all forms;
* *transformation:* at split-off primes, Hecke operators of even degree
  carry sections to sections and cusp forms to cusp forms by an explicit
  transformation law;
* *reading off eigenvalues:* for an eigenform at a split prime $P$, the
  values of its theta lift satisfy
  $$\phi_g(h) = \sigma(c)\,\mu_P^{-k}\sum_{a \in R}\phi_f(ha),$$
  where $g$ is the image of $f$ under the geometric Hecke operator, $R$ a set
  of coset representatives and $\mu_P$ the central value at $P$.
