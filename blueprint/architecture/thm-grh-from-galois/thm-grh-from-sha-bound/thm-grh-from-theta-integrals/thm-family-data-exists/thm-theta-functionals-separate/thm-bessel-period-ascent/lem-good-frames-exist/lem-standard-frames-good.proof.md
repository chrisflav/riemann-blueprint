The clauses of goodness are checked one by one. Conjugation of Weil data
along the grid, and the fact that lattice stabilisers at each place lie in
the depth congruence subgroups, show that the relevant integrands are
supported on a single double coset $R \cdot G_\infty \cdot K$. The pieces of
the passage from Bessel to Fourier–Jacobi models are admissible. The last
see-saw lattices have nonzero line vacua, which gives the remaining clauses.
