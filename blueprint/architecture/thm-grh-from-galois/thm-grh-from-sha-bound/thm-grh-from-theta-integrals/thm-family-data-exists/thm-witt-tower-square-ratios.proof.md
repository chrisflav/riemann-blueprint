Combine five statements about the Witt tower, each proved from the step
identities: a norm formula for the theta lift at the top stage, a
factorisation of the stage constants, the norm step in small rank, the norm
step in large rank, and the equality of the constant ratio with that at the
bottom. The non-vanishing of $\Lambda$ fixes the $L$-function uniquely. Then
multiply the steps together and track the ranks, which grow by $t$ plus an
odd number at each step.
