Count the pairs of terms with given indices by the masses of the lattices
they determine, and bound these masses by $m_u$ times the amplitudes, using
the mass ratio one rank at a time and the mass of maximal lattices. The
Hasse norm theorem [hasse-norm-st] is also used; *it is not clear* where.
