Bessel functionals on discrete series with large gaps are unique up to
scalars and compatible with Whittaker functionals. With the gap hypothesis
this controls each vector $v_t$ by the weight of its term. The weighted sum
over pairs is then bounded by
[term-pair-count-bound-of-siegel-mass-ratios], with the Hasse norm theorem
[hasse-norm-st] as a further input.
