For all large scales and every choice of test data there are a sign $s$ and
values $z_t$ of the twisting characters with the following property. For
every real point $p$ and every term $\beta$ of the expansion that represents
$s$, the Fock-model values of the twisted vacuum vectors at $p\beta$ equal
$\zeta$ times the corresponding combination of values at the base point,
with $\zeta = z_t^{-1}$ on the staircase cosets.
