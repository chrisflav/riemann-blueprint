The reference covolume identity holds; it is proved separately, from the
covolumes of the fundamental domains. It gives the archimedean volume
identity ([lem-archimedean-volume-identity]). The Siegel–Weil formula
([thm-siegel-main-theorem-hermitian]) gives the volumes of the stabilisers.
To compare the constants, split the measure of the adelic quotient into
local factors, using integrality of the Witt-type decomposition almost
everywhere. The archimedean factor is given by the archimedean volume
identity. The local masses, the level and ordinary volumes, the typed
volumes and the index of the typed lattices are then computed one by one,
and their product is the mass constant.
