Let $\rec$ be a system of local parameters ([def-local-parameters]) and $N$
a choice of norms that refines the choice with a Bloch–Kato law. Let $p$ be
the construction data of $N$. Suppose that partial constructions for $p$
move with changes of the division data, and that the multiplicity condition
of [thm-membership-with-origin] holds for $p$. Then at every good
constructed point of the families built from $p$ there is an admissible
datum compatible with $p$.
