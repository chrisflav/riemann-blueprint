The intermediate constructions exist by
[prop-intermediate-constructions-exist]. A weight-case condition comes
from the residue characters, and a fine adelic datum extends to larger
fields by the covering theorem for discrete series. These give finiteness and
the passage to a second, enlarged choice of norms. A system of local
parameters then gives depth-three partial constructions for $p$, and the
level-three upgrade holds both for $p$ and for the enlarged data. Apply
[prop-membership-two-norm-choices] to the two choices of norms.

The published results used along the way include Jacquet–Shalika
[thm-jacquet-shalika], Bernstein–Zelevinsky finiteness [thm-bz-finiteness],
the geometric lemma [thm-geometric-lemma], Green's classification
[thm-green-parametrisation], base change for definite unitary groups
[thm-labesse-base-change], purity of regular algebraic representations
[thm-clozel-regular-algebraic], the doubling method [thm-doubling-method],
the Siegel–Weil formula [thm-siegel-weil-weil-range], local Tate duality
[thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic], the local and global Euler characteristic
formulas ([thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic],
[thm-tate-global-euler-characteristic]), the Greenberg–Wiles formula
[thm-greenberg-wiles] and Berger's theorem [thm-p-adic-monodromy].
