The choice of norms gives a uniform bound for the zeta factors. Witnesses
pass along the division of sets, and the local parameters reduce the
compatibility with $p$ to the multiplicity condition. A recursion on the
sizes $\eta$, using Artin reciprocity and ray class fields, then builds the
admissible data.
