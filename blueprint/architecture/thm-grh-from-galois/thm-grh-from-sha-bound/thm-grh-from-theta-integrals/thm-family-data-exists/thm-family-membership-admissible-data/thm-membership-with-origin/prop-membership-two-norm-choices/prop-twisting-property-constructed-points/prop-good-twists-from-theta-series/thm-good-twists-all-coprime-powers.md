Let $D$ be a construction over a CM field $E$, with exponent data, and let
$i$ be a twisting index with prime $\ell = \ell_i$ that has a non-zero
place. Suppose the admissibility conditions of Bloch–Kato type hold at $i$,
together with conditions on signs, supports and the action of Hecke
operators (they commute and act semisimply). Suppose further that the theta
forms attached to $i$ have full rank, with chosen bases.

Then for every finite set $A$ of places of $E$ and every $\varepsilon' > 0$
there are a finite set $Q$ of places, an integer $k'$ and a character $\psi'$
of the ray class group of conductor $Q$, with values in the units of the
coefficient ring modulo $\ell^{k'}$, such that $\psi'^a$ is a good twist
avoiding $A$ for every integer $a$ prime to $\ell$.
