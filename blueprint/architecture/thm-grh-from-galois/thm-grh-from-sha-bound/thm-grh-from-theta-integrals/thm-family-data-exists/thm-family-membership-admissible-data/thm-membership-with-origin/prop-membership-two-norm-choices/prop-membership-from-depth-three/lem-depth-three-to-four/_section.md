Assume the theory of infinitesimal characters of discrete series,
Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem, rapid decay of cusp forms, uniqueness
of discrete series of $\U(p, q)$, finite dimensionality of the spaces of
forms involved, the Siegel–Weil formula, existence of discrete series, the
formal degree formula and the Siegel mass ratio. Let $p$ be construction
data from a system of local parameters ([def-local-parameters]), with no
extra condition at the spectral step, for which the level-three upgrade
holds. Then every normalised depth-three partial construction for $p$ extends
to a depth-four one. Depth four adds data at the split primes, compatibility conditions at the
real places, and identities fixing the signatures and the discriminant.
