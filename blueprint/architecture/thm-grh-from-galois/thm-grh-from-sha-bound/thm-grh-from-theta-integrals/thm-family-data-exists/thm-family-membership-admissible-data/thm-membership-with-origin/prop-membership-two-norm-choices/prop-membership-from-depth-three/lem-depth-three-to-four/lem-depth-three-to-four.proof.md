The existence of discrete series ([thm-discrete-series-parametrisation])
gives saturation, and the formal degree formula ([thm-formal-degree-upq])
gives the real-place data. [lem-integrable-spectral-setting] gives an
integrable spectral setting. The ratio of masses that Siegel's formula
([thm-siegel-main-theorem-hermitian]) gives shows that the volumes equal
the masses. Harish-Chandra's discrete series
([thm-hc-discrete-series-classification]) give good idempotents at the real
places.
