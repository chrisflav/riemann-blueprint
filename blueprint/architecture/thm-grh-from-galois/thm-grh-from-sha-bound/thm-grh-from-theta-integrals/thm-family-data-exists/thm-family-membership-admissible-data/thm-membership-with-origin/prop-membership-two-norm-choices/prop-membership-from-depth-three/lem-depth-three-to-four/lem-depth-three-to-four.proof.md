Discrete series give saturation, and the formal degree formula gives the
real-place data. [lem-integrable-spectral-setting] gives an integrable spectral
setting. The volume ratio from the Siegel–Weil formula shows that the
volumes equal the masses. Harish-Chandra's discrete series give good
idempotents at the real places.
