The four results in the hypotheses give the condition on base data. Every
start extends to depth three, then to depth four, and then to a constructed
point by [lem-constructed-points-from-depth-four]; this gives the
condition on untwisted data. The same chain for $p'$, followed by the
passage to an enlarged base, gives the last condition.
