Let $D$ be a construction with forms $F_u$ satisfying the gap hypothesis of
[lem-integrable-spectral-setting]. Then there are a setting $X$ for $D$, an
isomorphism of its adèles with the standard ones, and a standard additive
character $\psi$ of the adèles of the totally real field, such that the
measures of $X$ are compatible with $\psi$ at every place and globally.
Moreover, for every character of the level subgroup and every index $u$,
the form $F_u$ can be transported into $X$ with matching levels.
