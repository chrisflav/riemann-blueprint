The theta series are rational with bounded coefficients, by the rationality
and integrality results, and Hecke operators preserve the lattice of
bounded vectors. These facts, the Hodge numbers and the roots of unity give
the inputs of [thm-good-twists-all-coprime-powers], which
supplies the good twists. At each twisted place, rational scalars give theta
series forms of full rank, and class sums give a basis of averaged series.
