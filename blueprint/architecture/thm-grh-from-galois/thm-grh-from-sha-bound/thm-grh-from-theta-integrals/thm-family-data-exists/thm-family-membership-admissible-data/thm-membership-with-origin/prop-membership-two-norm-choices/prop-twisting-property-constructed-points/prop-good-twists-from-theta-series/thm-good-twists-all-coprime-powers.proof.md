Galois cohomology gives a presentation matrix for the Selmer group. A choice
of odd auxiliary primes bounds the residual Selmer group, and Chebotarev's
theorem gives a bound modulo $2$ at the prime $2$. A twisting theorem for
lattices of bounded vectors turns these bounds into the character $\psi'$.
*We read* the argument as a choice of auxiliary primes in the style of
Taylor–Wiles, controlled by the Greenberg–Wiles formula.
