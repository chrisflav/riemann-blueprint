Take the measured setting of [lem-measured-setting-standard-character]. The
local compatibility conditions give the full data, and the gap hypothesis
gives decay of the test function, hence its integrability. Harish-Chandra's
finiteness theorem ([thm-hc-finiteness-unitary]) then gives finiteness, and
the identification with the forms holds index by index. *We read* the step
as an instance of local uniqueness of Bessel models.
