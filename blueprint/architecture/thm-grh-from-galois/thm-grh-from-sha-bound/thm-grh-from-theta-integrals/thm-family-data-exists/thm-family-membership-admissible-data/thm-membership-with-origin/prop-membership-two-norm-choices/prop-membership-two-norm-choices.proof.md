The twisting property at constructed points comes from
[prop-twisting-property-constructed-points], which rests on the Galois
cohomology of Tate and Greenberg–Wiles ([thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic],
[thm-tate-global-euler-characteristic], [thm-greenberg-wiles]). With it,
[prop-membership-from-depth-three] gives the membership conditions, and
[prop-admissible-data-exist] gives the admissible data.
