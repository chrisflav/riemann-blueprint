Apply [five-point-laws-true-chains] to the twisted chain of each tuple. The
level prime is larger than $2f\,N_m + 1$, where $2f = [E : \Q]$, and the
weights have the right signs. Under these conditions a sharp valuation bound
at the last step of a lattice chain is stable, and it passes to the twisted
chains through their compatibility with the initial lattice.
