Since $p$ splits in $K$, the curve has ordinary reduction and a $p$-adic
period $\delta_0, \Omega$ exists. The ratio $\delta/\delta_0$ is a $p$-adic
unit. Realise the theta function on a Heisenberg setup with a hermitian
form; the translation operators act on it. The moments of translates give a
measure, and its norm is at most $1$ because the translates of prime-to-$p$
order are integral on the lattice. The algebraicity of the coefficients of
the translated functions follows from the hypotheses.
