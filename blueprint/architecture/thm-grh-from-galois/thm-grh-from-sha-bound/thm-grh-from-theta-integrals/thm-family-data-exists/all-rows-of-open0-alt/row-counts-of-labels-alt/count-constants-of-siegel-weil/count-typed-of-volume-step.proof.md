A lowest $K$-type estimate gives decay of the kernel away from the
identity. The volume step gives bounds on absolute volumes and on the
probability that a flag is isotropic. Split the geometric side of the
kernel into near, far, rare and deep terms and bound each one; the identity
term gives $M$.
