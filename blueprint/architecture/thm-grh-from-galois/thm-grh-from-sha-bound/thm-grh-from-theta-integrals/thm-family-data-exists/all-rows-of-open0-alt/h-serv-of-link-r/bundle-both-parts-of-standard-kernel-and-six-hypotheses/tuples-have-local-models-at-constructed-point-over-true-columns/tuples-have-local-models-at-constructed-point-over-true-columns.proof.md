A standard construction gives matching local data at every place, and its
stabilisers at step zero are trivial. Then
[theta-local-models-of-split-unramified-joints] gives local models for the
standard construction itself. They carry over to the point, whose division
lattices and heights differ only by a change that does not affect the
models.
