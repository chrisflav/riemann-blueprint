The archimedean model data exist ([carrier-p]). With them, the bound on
non-generic terms, finite-dimensionality, uniqueness of Bessel models with
a large gap and the Siegel–Weil mass data, all hypotheses of the general
bound hold ([theorem-s-leaves-of-carrier-g]), so the bound holds for every
datum ([theorem-s-of-conj]). It remains to check that the chosen data of the
class meet the size and threshold hypotheses; the rank conditions and the
small-terms bound give this.
