Combine the compactly supported form of the archimedean theorem
([actual-compact-theorem]), the existence of archimedean models for admissible settings
([model-place-built-holds]), the uniform archimedean bounds
([carrier-needs-from-d]) and the limit formula for energies
([lemma-model]). A lifting statement at level $3$ and the uniqueness of
Bessel models with a large gap supply the side conditions at the real
places.
