Five hypotheses are assumed. The coefficient identities come from the
local decomposition of [rows-datum-p-of-deep], whose inputs hold for every
datum ([cut-leaves-deep-outright]). Uniqueness of Bessel models gives decay
of gaps and independence of the volumes at every prime; the counting
formula and the oddness of the volume give the summation identity and the
form of the mass data. The normalising constants come from the covolume
reference and the Siegel–Weil mass data ([nc-tie-c-of-closed]), and the mass
data are positive because the local densities are. The remaining statements
are proved directly.
