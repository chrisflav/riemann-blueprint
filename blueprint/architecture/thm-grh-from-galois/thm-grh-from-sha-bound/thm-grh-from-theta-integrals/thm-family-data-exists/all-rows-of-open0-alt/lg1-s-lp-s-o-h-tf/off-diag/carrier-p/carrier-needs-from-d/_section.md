Three archimedean statements hold: the compactly supported form of the
archimedean theorem ([actual-compact-theorem]), the existence of models at
admissible settings ([model-place-built-holds]), and uniform bounds at admissible archimedean settings.
The last says: given the first, for every constant $C_b$ there are constants
$C_c, C_4$ such that at every setting of leading forms over a CM field the
positivity statement, integrality, the curvature barriers by place, the
lifting thresholds and a concrete form of the sum with constant $C_4$ hold.
