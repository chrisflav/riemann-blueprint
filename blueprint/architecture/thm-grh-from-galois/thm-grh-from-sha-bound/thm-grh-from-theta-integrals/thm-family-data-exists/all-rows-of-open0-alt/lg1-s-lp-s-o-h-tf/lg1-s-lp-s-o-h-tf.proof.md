The Siegel–Weil formula gives the ratio of volumes of unitary groups under
the change $M \to M-1$ of rank. From it follow the rank conditions and the
level bound (3) on the class. The small-terms bound (1) comes from the
finite-dimensionality of the relevant spaces of automorphic forms, the
uniqueness of Bessel models with a large gap, Whittaker–Bessel
multiplicity one, and a pointwise bound for matrix coefficients of discrete
series. The off-diagonal bound (2) then follows from (1) by [off-diag].
