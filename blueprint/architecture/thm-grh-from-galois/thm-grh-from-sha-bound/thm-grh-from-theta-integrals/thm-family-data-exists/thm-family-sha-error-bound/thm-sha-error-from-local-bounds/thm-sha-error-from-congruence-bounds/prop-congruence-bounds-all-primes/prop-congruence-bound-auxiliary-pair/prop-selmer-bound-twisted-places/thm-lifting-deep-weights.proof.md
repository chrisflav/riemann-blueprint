Integrality from a metric: the finite classes of $T_2$ have bounded
denominators against those of $T_1$, which
[Fontaine's period ring](@thm-fontaine-bcris) and the Hodge–Tate weights
control. The [local theory of Bloch and Kato](@thm-bloch-kato-local),
[local Tate duality](@thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic) and the
[Euler characteristic formula](@thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic) count
the classes.
