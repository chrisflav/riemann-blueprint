Let $K$ be a tamely ramified extension of $\Q_l$ with ramification index
$e$, given with a uniformiser, and let $F : T_1 \to T_2$ be a surjection of
good crystalline lattices for $G_K$. Here *good* means that inertia acts
through a set $\Sigma$ of tame characters and the Hodge–Tate weights lie in
a set $U$, with $N, \eta, H$ bounding $|\Sigma|$, $|U|$ and the weights as in
[thm-lifting-potentially-crystalline], and $l$ large. Let $P$ be
the largest period of the characters in $\Sigma$. Then every class in
$H^1_f(G_K, T_2)$, multiplied by $l^{7(NP + \eta(N + P))}$, lies in the image
of $H^1_f(G_K, T_1)$.
