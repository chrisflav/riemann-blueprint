Show that every situation is realised by a presented Wach lattice, check
the admissible closure and the standard admissibility of its classes, and
identify the finite classes with the admissible ones by a residue bound.
The argument uses the standard properties of Wach lattices (freeness,
comparison with étale lattices, and about fifteen more), which come from
the [local theory of Bloch and Kato](@thm-bloch-kato-local) and
[Fontaine's period ring](@thm-fontaine-bcris).
[Local Tate duality](@thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic) and the
[Euler characteristic formula](@thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic) enter
through the count of classes.
