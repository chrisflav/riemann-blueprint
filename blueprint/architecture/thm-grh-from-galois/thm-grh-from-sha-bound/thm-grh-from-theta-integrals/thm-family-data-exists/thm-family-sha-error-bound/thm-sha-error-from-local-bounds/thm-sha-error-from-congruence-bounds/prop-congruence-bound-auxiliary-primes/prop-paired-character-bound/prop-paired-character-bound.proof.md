At a place above $g_k$ the character conditions make inertia act on the
character part of the Galois representation by $u$ at one place and by
$u^{-1}$ at the conjugate place. The bound reduces to a local lifting bound
for potentially crystalline lattices over $\Q_{g_k}$, which
[thm-lifting-potentially-crystalline] gives once $g_k$ is large
compared with the Hodge–Tate weights. The integral comparison theorem of
Bhatt and Scholze ([thm-bhatt-scholze-crystalline-lattices],
[thm-bhatt-scholze-coefficients]) supplies the lattices.
