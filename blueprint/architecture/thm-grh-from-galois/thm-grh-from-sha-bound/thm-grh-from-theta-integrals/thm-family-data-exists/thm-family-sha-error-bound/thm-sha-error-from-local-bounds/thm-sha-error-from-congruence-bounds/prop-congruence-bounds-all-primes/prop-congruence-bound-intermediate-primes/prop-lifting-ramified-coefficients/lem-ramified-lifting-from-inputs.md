Suppose the lattices of a member satisfy the standard local inputs: the
lifting properties of their Wach modules, a condition on the labelling of
embeddings, the natural extension of Herr's classes, the crystalline lifting
bounds, and Fontaine's results. Then the lifting bound of
[prop-lifting-ramified-coefficients] holds with constant
$25$, for every choice of the auxiliary constants.
