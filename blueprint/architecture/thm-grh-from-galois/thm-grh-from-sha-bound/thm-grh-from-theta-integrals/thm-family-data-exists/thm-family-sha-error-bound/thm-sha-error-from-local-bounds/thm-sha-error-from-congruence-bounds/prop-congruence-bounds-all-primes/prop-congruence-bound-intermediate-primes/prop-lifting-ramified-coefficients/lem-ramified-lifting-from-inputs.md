Let $F : T_1 \to T_2$ be a surjection of crystalline lattices of a member,
with coefficient field containing the $l$-th roots of unity, and $l \ge 11$.
Then the lifting bound of [prop-lifting-ramified-coefficients] holds for $F$
with constant $25$, for every choice of the auxiliary constants.
