Let $C \ge 2080$ and let $Q$ be a polynomial with
$Q(f, r, t) \ge 260 f^2t^2(r+t)$. Suppose the Galois representations $r_\pi$
of a member and their parts $\rho_\pi$ satisfy a list of local conditions:
an index condition away from the types, Iwahori admissibility, small Selmer
groups at the twisted places, Hodge–Tate weights in windows at $q$ and $d$,
compatibility of Fontaine's functor with sums, tame generators at $q$ and
$d$, and a polynomial bound on the parameters. Then the congruence bound at
$q$ and $d$ of [prop-congruence-bound-auxiliary-pair] holds with $C$ and $Q$,
together with two auxiliary conclusions which we have not unfolded.
