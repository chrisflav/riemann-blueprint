kind  = "lemma"
title = "The lifting theorem from properties of Wach modules"
lean  = ["Formalism.MainNotions.N.Ln03.local_theorem_of_inputs"]
order = 1
+++
Suppose that the Wach modules attached to the crystalline lattices of a
member have the standard properties: comparison with the étale lattices,
equality of de Rham and crystalline periods in the unramified case,
existence of unit generators for $l \ge 5$, and about a dozen further
conditions. Then the lifting theorem of
[thm-wach-lifting-theorem] holds with constant $800$.
