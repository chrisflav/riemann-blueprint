The lifting theorem with constant $800$ is [lem-wach-lifting-from-properties],
once the Wach lattices have their standard properties; those follow from the
[local theory of Bloch and Kato](@thm-bloch-kato-local),
[local Tate duality](@thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic), the
[Euler characteristic formula](@thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic) and
[Fontaine's period ring](@thm-fontaine-bcris). The corollary follows by
applying the theorem along the clusters.
