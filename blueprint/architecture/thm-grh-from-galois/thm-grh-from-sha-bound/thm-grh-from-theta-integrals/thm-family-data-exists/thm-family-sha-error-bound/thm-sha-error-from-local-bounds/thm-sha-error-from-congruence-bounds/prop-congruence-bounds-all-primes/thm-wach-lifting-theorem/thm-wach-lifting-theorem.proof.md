The lifting theorem with constant $800$ is [lem-wach-lifting-from-properties], once
the Wach lattices have their standard properties; those follow from the
local theory of Bloch and Kato with Tate duality and the Euler
characteristic formula. The corollary follows by applying the theorem along
the clusters.
