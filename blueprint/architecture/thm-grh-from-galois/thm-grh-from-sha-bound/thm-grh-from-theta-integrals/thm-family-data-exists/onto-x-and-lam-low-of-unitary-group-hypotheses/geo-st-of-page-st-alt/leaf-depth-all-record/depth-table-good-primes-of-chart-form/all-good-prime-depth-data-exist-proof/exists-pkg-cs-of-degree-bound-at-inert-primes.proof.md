Choose a complete field over the place above $l$ and base change the formal
coordinates of the point to it. At an inert prime of odd order, label the
Frobenius orbits of embeddings by $Z \times \Z/2d_l$. Since $\sqrt{-d}$ is
fixed by the embeddings in the CM type, the even labels give the CM type,
so the multiplicities are $p$ on even and $q$ on odd labels. Rewrite the
sums of weights over embeddings as sums over labels. The Hodge filtration
then gives the valuation blocks.
