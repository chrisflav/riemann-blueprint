The smallness condition gives $l \ne 2$. The theorem of Bhatt and Scholze
gives a crystalline lattice linked to the block data. A level map and a
Hecke module over the coefficient ring exist. The maximal ideals of the
Hecke algebra that meet the subspace cover it. At each such ideal,
Chebotarev's theorem for primes of degree one gives the link with the
weight data.
