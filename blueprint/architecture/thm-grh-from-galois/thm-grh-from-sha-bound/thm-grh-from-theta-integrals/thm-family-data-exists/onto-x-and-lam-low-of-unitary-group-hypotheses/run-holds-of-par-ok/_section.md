There is a threshold function $x_0(C_A, C_K)$ with the following property.
Take a point of a family and a prime $p$, and suppose that the local data at
$p$ satisfy the parameter conditions: they have the common residue
characteristic $p$, they are oriented, and the function fields involved are
algebraically closed. Then for every index $j$ in range, and every choice of
size data with scale $x \ge x_0(C_A, C_K)$, a height inequality holds for
the data attached to $j$, with a bound of size $r_j\,f\,m\,b\,\delta_2$.
Here $r_j$ is the rank of the lattice attached to $j$, $f, m, b$ are size
parameters of the family and $\delta_2$ is a further size parameter. The
same holds after the data are extended by a second norm.

*It is not clear* which heights these are. The statements used point to
heights of points on curves in an integral model and to sup norms of
integral forms.
