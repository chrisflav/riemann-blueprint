Zuckerman's bound on the gaps of the parameters that occur in a tensor
product with a finite-dimensional representation gives the hypotheses of
[lem-weighted-bound-cubic-threshold], with a threshold of cubic size in
$n = 2r + k + 1$. That result gives the bound.
