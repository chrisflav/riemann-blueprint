The pairing, with Cartan's surjectivity for the explicit map onto the space
in two letters, gives a contraction onto the standard lattice. The member's
admissibility conditions say that the graded lattices are stable, which is
what the contraction needs. The contraction, the injectivity of the
explicit map and Gauss inversion on the lattice in two letters then give
the large direct summand and the degree bound.
