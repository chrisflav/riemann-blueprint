Let $\lambda > 1$. Suppose families have slope data
([def-connected-families]), and that for every primitive $\psi$ auxiliary
fields as in [thm-auxiliary-fields] exist on windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$ of
arbitrarily large $Y$. Then primitive $L$-functions do not vanish right of
the critical line.
