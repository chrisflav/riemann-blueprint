Expand the conditions on Frobenius in $S$ by orthogonality of the
characters of $\Gamma$. The sum of the gaps over $u \in A_0$ then becomes a sum,
over the twists $t$ and the characters $\xi$ trivial on $A_0$, of $\xi(t)$
times the twisted sums $g_\xi$ at the points $a_t/b$, less their main
terms. Since $|a_t/b - x| \le 1$, a bound for the derivative of these sums
replaces $a_t/b$ by $x$ at a cost of at most $4x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$. The
twists in $T$ are equidistributed, up to $x^{-1+\varepsilon}$, for the
characters that correspond to $\langle\psi\rangle$; this produces the weight
$1/(3o+1)$ of [def-chebyshev-weighted]. Dividing the assumed bound on the
gaps by $m|T|b$ and using the size conditions gives the bound
$x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}$.
