Bound a character-weighted form of $G_m$ by
$x^{1/2 + (C+3)\varepsilon}$ plus a sum over the other characters, provided
every gap is at most $x^{C\varepsilon}$ times the error of the window
([thm-chain-averaging]). The slope inequality bounds the gaps, with
$C = e_D + e_L + 3$ ([thm-member-gap-bound]). Then identify the weighted form
with $G_m$, and bound the sum over the other characters by
$4o\,x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ with the conditions on $E$.
