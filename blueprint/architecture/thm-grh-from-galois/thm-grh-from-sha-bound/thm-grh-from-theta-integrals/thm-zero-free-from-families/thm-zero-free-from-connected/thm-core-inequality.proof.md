Bound a character-weighted form of $G_m$ by
$x^{1/2 + (C+3)\varepsilon}$ plus a sum of gaps, provided every gap is at
most $x^{C\varepsilon}$ times the error of the window. The slope inequality
bounds the gaps. Then identify the weighted form with $G_m$ and bound the
remaining sum.
