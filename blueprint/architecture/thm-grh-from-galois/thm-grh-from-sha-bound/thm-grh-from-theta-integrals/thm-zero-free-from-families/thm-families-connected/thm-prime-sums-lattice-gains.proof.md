The local link follows from four properties of the members, each holding
eventually: finiteness of classes with trivial stabilisers, existence of
standard sublattices, the second admissibility condition, and the descent of
valuations through the comparison maps. Group compatibility gives the first,
second and fourth: it gives a ring structure on the lattices, hence standard
sublattices, and a hermitian structure of level zero, hence trivial
stabilisers with finitely many classes; it also gives the comparison of
valuations. The local link is then assembled member by member from the two
local theorems in closed form, the bound for the bad share of Fock lattices
([thm-fock-bad-share-mean]) and the large summand of low degree
([thm-large-summand-low-degree]), together with the reduced form of the local
estimate at inert primes ([thm-local-estimate-inert-primes]), which enters
through extremal projectors. Signs, units and a logarithmic bound for the
family are checked directly. *We read* the first local theorem as controlling
the local lengths and the second the gains.
