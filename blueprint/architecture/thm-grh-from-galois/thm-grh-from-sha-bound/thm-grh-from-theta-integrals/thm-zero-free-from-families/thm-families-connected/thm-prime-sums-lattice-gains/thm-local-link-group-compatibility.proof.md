Group compatibility gives a ring structure on the lattices, hence standard
sublattices, and a hermitian structure of level zero, hence trivial
stabilisers with finitely many classes; it also gives the comparison of
valuations. The local link is then assembled member by member from the two
local theorems in closed form, the bound for the bad share of Fock lattices
([thm-fock-bad-share-mean]) and the large summand of low degree
([thm-large-summand-low-degree]), together with the local estimate at inert
primes, which enters through extremal projectors. Signs, units and a
logarithmic bound for the family are checked directly. *We read* the first
local theorem as controlling the local lengths and the second the gains.
