Assume the reduced form of the local estimate at inert primes holds for
every member ([thm-local-estimate-inert-primes]). Then the conclusion of [thm-prime-sums-lattice-gains] holds
for every class of families whose members eventually satisfy group
compatibility and the second admissibility condition.

More precisely, the local link follows from the following properties of the
members, each holding eventually: finiteness of classes with trivial
stabilisers, existence of standard sublattices, the second admissibility
condition, and the descent of valuations through the comparison maps. Group
compatibility gives the first, second and fourth.
