The operator step and the bounds for words give the bound for words in the
operators. The plane bound and the arithmetic step give, through a
monomial form of an auxiliary lemma, the first half of the membership
condition; the bound for words gives the second. So the membership
condition holds for all constants, and [prop-local-estimate-from-membership] applies.
