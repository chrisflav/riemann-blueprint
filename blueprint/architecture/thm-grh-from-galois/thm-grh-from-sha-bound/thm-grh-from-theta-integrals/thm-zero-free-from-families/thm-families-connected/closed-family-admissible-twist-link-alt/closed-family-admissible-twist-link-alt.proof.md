Families in $\mathcal A_3$ satisfy the size bound of
[closed-serv-f-of-serv-t-of-d-gam-size] by
definition, so [closed-serv-f-of-serv-t-of-d-gam-size] turns the served
choice of twists into the bound for sums over primes. Together with the
other hypotheses and the coprimality of the level with the stabilisers,
which holds eventually for these families, this is the full list of inputs
of the admissibility criterion for families with twists, whose first
conclusion is the second admissibility condition.
