title = "The local estimate from the membership condition"
lean  = ["Formalism.MainNotions.Proposition5Numerics.stmtE_of_mem"]
order = 10
+++
Suppose that for all constants $C_\Lambda, C_k, C_{\mathrm{rk}}, C_{\deg}$
for which a member $D$ is admitted, the *membership condition* holds for all
large $C$: the polynomials produced by the local construction lie in the
lattices that the estimate requires. Then $D$ satisfies the local estimate
at inert primes.
