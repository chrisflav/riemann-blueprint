We say that *families have slope data* if, for every $\lambda > 1$ and every
requirement on auxiliary fields, the following holds. Suppose fields
meeting the requirement exist on windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$, and the members
of every family in the corresponding class eventually satisfy the
Tate–Shafarevich bound. Then for some constants $e_D, e_L$ and every small
$\varepsilon$, some family in the class carries slope data
([def-slope-data]) at every large $x$ of its windows.
