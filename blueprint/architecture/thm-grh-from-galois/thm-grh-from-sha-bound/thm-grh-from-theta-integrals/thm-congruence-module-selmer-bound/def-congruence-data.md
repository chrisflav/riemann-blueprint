Let $D$ be a member with auxiliary field $E$, coefficient field $L$ and rank
data $(k, m, t)$ ([def-family-member]). Write $G_E$ for the absolute Galois
group of $E$ and $\varepsilon_l$ for the $l$-adic cyclotomic character.
*Congruence data* for $D$ consist of local Galois data of $E$ at every prime,
whose Tate–Shafarevich lengths are those recorded in $D$, and a finite set
of *congruence places* $i$. Each congruence place has:

* a prime $l_i$, a step $1 \le j_i \le m$ of the chain, and a place $w_i$ of
  $L$ above $l_i$ where the twisting character is non-trivial;
* a complete discrete valuation ring $\mathcal O_i$, finite over $\Z_{l_i}$,
  with an embedding $L \to \mathrm{Frac}\,\mathcal O_i$ inducing $w_i$;
* a finite set $X_i$ of Hecke eigensystems and a local Hecke algebra
  $\mathbb T_i \subset \mathcal O_i^{X_i}$ that separates them, generated by
  the Hecke operators $T_{v,k}$, $1 \le k \le n_V$, at places $v$ of $E$ split
  over $F$ and outside $l_i$, $S_i$ and $Q_i$; $\mathbb T_i$ acts faithfully
  on a free $\mathcal O_i$-module $M_i$, a lattice in the forms at step $j_i$,
  and every eigensystem occurs in it;
* characters $\chi_{i,c} : G_E \to \mathcal O_i^\times$ with
  $\chi_{i,c} = \chi_{i,1}\,\varepsilon_{l_i}^{1-c}$ for $1 \le c \le t$;
* subsets $Z_i^{\mathrm{good}} \subset Z_i \subset X_i$;
* disjoint sets $S_i, Q_i$ of split places prime to $l_i$, where $S_i$ is the
  set of places above $g_0$, $g_1$ and one of $p, p'$, and $\chi_{i,1}$ is
  ramified at every place of $Q_i$, with tame generators of inertia at the
  places of $S_i \cup Q_i$.

The primes $g_0, g_1$ split completely in $E$, and the data carry labels
at their places: monic polynomials of degree $r$ over $\mathbb F_{g_k}$ and
characters, for a finite level group of order
$\prod_{k}|\GL_r(\mathbb F_{g_k})|^f$.

The *congruence module* of an eigensystem $\pi \in X_i$ measures its
congruences with the other eigensystems in $\mathbb T_i$; the theorem
[thm-congruence-module-selmer-bound] bounds its length.
