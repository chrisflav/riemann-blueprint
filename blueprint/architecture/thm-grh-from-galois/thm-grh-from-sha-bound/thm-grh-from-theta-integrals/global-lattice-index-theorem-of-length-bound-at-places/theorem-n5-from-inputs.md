In the setting of [global-lattice-index-theorem-of-length-bound-at-places],
suppose the family of Galois representations satisfies the admissibility
conditions listed there, and the unramified condition at the places of
$R \cup C$. If the local lengths are bounded by $\mathrm{len}_w$, the lifting
exponents by $e'_w$, and the bound $c_B$ on the tame part holds, then the
length of the congruence module of $\pi$ satisfies the bound of
[global-lattice-index-theorem-of-length-bound-at-places].
