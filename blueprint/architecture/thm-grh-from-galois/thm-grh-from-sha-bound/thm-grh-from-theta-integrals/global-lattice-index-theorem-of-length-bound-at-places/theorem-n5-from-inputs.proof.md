The congruence module of $\pi$ is the cokernel of the Hecke algebra in the
product of its components; its length equals the index of the lattice of
eigenforms. Ribet's construction turns congruences between $\pi$ and the
other eigensystems into extensions between $\rho$ and the characters
$\chi^c$, and so into Selmer classes of the twists $\Z_l(e)$ and of $\rho$.
Their lengths are bounded by the local lengths at the places of $R \cup C$,
the Tate–Shafarevich groups and the class group, using the decomposition of
$r_\pi$ and the local conditions. *We read* this from the shape of the
bound; the proof is not split into named steps.
