Suppose that at every prime $p \ne 2$ unramified in $E$ at which the Hodge
bundles are free, the de Rham and Hodge–Tate lattices of the universal
abelian scheme come, over the local rings above $p$, from a family of
Dieudonné lattices with a Frobenius permutation of the embeddings. Then the
Chern classes of the de Rham and Hodge–Tate flags agree at those primes.
