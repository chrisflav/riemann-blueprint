Assume the inputs of [good-tower-volume-theorem], a geometric bound with
constants $C_h, C_u$ for the nine geometric quantities of the tower, a
comparison of de Rham and Hodge–Tate flags at good primes, triviality of
the flag bundle on the Hodge part, a flat-degree statement, a statement on
twisted automorphisms, and the two comparison statements for intersection
numbers. Then the volume theorem of [good-tower-volume-theorem] holds.
