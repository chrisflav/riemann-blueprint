Assume heights of flags, the inputs on flag models, upper and lower bounds
for successive minima, the perturbation lemma for antisymmetric forms and
Zhang's lower bound for heights on Grassmannians. Let $T$ be a tower of flag
bundles with proportional volumes, satisfying the identities
$I_1, \dots, I_8$, nefness of its models, the propositions on Frobenius,
on positivity and on fibres with constants $C', r_d, L$, equality of volumes,
and a lower bound for slopes with constant $C$. Then $T$ satisfies the
conclusions of the volume theorem with constant $2C + 3C' + 5$.
