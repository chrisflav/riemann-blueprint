Assume an arithmetic intersection theory with resolution in projective
space ([ar-calc]), the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel inequalities and
Moriwaki's theorem (among the results [thm-grh-from-theta-integrals]
assumes). Let $\bar F$ be a hermitian lattice of rank $i \ge 1$ over a number
field $K$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. Then over some finite extension $K'$ of $K$
there is a non-zero vector $v$ whose line has degree
$$\widehat{\deg}(K'v) \ge \mu(\bar F) - \tfrac12\log i - \varepsilon.$$

This is the absolute Siegel lemma of Zhang and Roy–Thunder (1995).
