Assume a bound for self-intersections and injectivity of small sections;
toroidal compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties
([toroidal-for-fan-ks]) and seminegativity of Lie algebras of semi-abelian
schemes ([semi-abelian-lie-seminegative]); Green's classification
([green-cuspidal]); separation of points
([point-separation-pair-of-literature]); the Fubini–Study formula for Chow
heights; the published theorems on Hodge bundles
([published-hodge-bundle-theorems]); toroidal compactifications of Siegel
moduli with charts ([siegel-toroidal-integral-of-literature]) and arithmetic
degrees ([ar-calc]); Grothendieck's deformation theorem
([grothendieck-deformation]); Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing
([kawamata-viehweg]), $L^2$ extension ([l2-extension-log-canonical]), Serre's
holomorphic sections ([serre-holomorphic-sections]) and Chow forms
([chow-form-exists]); and the lower bound for mean arithmetic volumes of
[deep-slim-use]. Then the covolume bound of [top-covol-slim2-use-alt-alt]
holds.
