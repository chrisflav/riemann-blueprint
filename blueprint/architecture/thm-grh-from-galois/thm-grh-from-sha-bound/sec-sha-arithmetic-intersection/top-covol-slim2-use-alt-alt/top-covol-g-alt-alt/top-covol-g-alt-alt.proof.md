At each member of a family, cover the data by deep covers built from the
automorphic line bundles of [deep-slim-use]. The toroidal compactifications
and semi-abelian schemes make the covers good, the theorem of Scholze and
Weinstein gives the flat degree, and Grothendieck's deformation theorem the
degree of the covers. The height lower bound
([height-lower-bound-general-alt]) and the bounds on height sums give a
uniform lower bound for heights on the covers. Then a lower bound for the
lifted lattices, with truncation, the bad set and the bound on $\lambda$,
gives the covolume bound. The Hodge-bundle inputs enter through
[to-classical-theorems].
