Prove the Hilbert–Samuel formula for one block of multihomogeneous
coordinates ([lem-hilbert-samuel-block]), pass to arbitrary varieties by covers of
flag type ([lem-flag-cover-pullback]), and deduce the volume
inequalities by comparing small sections with the self-intersection.
Embedded resolution in projective space ([thm-hironaka-resolution]) and the
properties of arithmetic intersection numbers
([thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory]) are used throughout, and the existence
of a Zariski-dense set of points of bounded height on every arithmetic
variety gives the Kleiman criterion.
