The arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorem for a block of coordinates
([lem-hilbert-samuel-block]) follows from eighteen statements: the index of block
pieces, finiteness of the block modules, the Hilbert function of module
pieces, the cycle of a block module, coordinate changes and changes of
model, the degree of projective frame graphs, transport of the
Hilbert–Samuel property, the degree and mixed terms of resolutions and of
block resolutions, the image of block resolutions, integrability of Green
functions at points, inverse images of blocks, and changes of model for
graphs.
