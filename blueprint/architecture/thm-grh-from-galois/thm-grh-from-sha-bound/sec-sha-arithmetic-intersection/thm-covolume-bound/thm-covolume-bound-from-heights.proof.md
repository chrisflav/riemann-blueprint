At each member of a family, cover the data by deep covers built from the
automorphic line bundles. The toroidal compactifications
([thm-toroidal-compactification-siegel], [thm-toroidal-compactification-unitary])
and seminegativity of the Lie algebras of semi-abelian schemes
([thm-hodge-bundle-semipositive]) make the covers good, the theorem of
Scholze and Weinstein gives the flat degree, and Grothendieck's deformation
theorem ([thm-grothendieck-messing]) the degree of the covers. The height
lower bound ([lem-height-lower-bound-deep-covers]), with a bound for the
arithmetic self-intersections and injectivity of small sections, gives a
uniform lower bound for heights on the covers; here Green's classification
([thm-green-parametrisation]), separation of points
([thm-angehrn-siu-point-separation]), the Fubini–Study formula for Chow
heights, Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing ([thm-kawamata-viehweg]), $L^2$
extension ([thm-ohsawa-takegoshi-points]), Serre's holomorphic sections
([thm-gaga-sections]) and Chow forms ([thm-chow-form]) enter. A lower bound
for the lifted lattices, with truncation, the bad set and the bound on
$\lambda$, then gives the covolume bound. The published theorems on Hodge
bundles ([def-hodge-bundle-inputs]) enter through Mumford's proportionality
theorem ([thm-mumford-good-metrics]).
