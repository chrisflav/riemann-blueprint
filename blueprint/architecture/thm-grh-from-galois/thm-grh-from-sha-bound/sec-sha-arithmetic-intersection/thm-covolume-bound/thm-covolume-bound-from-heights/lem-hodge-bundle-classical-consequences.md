The published theorems on Hodge bundles ([def-hodge-bundle-inputs])
imply the classical hypotheses of the covering estimates: Mumford's
proportionality theorem on toroidal flag bundles, and the facts on toroidal
compactifications, arithmetic degrees, abelian de Rham cohomology, ray
class fields, semistable reduction, strong approximation, Green's
characters, the norm theorems of Hasse and Landherr, extension of
homomorphisms of semi-abelian schemes, lowest alcoves, principal
polarisations and multiplication by $n$, with the volume inequalities.
