Assume a bound for self-intersections and injectivity of small sections;
toroidal compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties
([thm-toroidal-compactification-unitary]) and seminegativity of Lie algebras of semi-abelian
schemes ([thm-hodge-bundle-semipositive]); Green's classification
([thm-green-parametrisation]); separation of points
([thm-angehrn-siu-point-separation]); the Fubini–Study formula for Chow
heights; the published theorems on Hodge bundles
([def-hodge-bundle-inputs]); toroidal compactifications of Siegel
moduli with charts ([thm-toroidal-compactification-siegel]) and arithmetic
degrees ([thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory]); Grothendieck's deformation theorem
([thm-grothendieck-messing]); Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing
([thm-kawamata-viehweg]), $L^2$ extension ([thm-ohsawa-takegoshi-points]), Serre's
holomorphic sections ([thm-gaga-sections]) and Chow forms
([thm-chow-form]); and the lower bound for mean arithmetic volumes of
[thm-mean-arithmetic-volume-lower-bound]. Then the covolume bound of [thm-covolume-bound]
holds.
