The reference weight ([lem-reference-weights-deep-covers]) gives a
section of controlled norm. The arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorem, the mean
volume bound of [thm-mean-arithmetic-volume-lower-bound] and the Hodge-bundle inputs turn its norm
into the height bound. Semistable reduction, Grothendieck–Messing, formal
algebraisation and abelian de Rham cohomology control the integral
structure.
