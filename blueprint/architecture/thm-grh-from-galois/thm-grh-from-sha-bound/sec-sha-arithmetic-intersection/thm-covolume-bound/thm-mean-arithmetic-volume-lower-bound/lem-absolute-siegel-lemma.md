Let $\bar F$ be a hermitian lattice of rank $i \ge 1$ over a number field $K$
and $\varepsilon > 0$. Then over some finite extension $K'$ of $K$ there is a
non-zero vector $v$ whose line has degree
$$\widehat{\deg}(K'v) \ge \mu(\bar F) - \tfrac12\log i - \varepsilon.$$
