Apply the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel inequality to $\mathcal O(1)$ on the
projective space of $\bar F$, and use Moriwaki's theorem to find small
points of large degree. The comparison is made by covers of flag type
([lem-flag-cover-pullback]).
