Apply the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel inequality
([thm-arithmetic-hilbert-samuel-inequalities]) to $\mathcal O(1)$ on the
projective space of $\bar F$, with the arithmetic intersection theory of
[thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory], and use Moriwaki's theorem to find
small points of large degree. The comparison is made by covers of flag type
([lem-flag-cover-pullback]).
