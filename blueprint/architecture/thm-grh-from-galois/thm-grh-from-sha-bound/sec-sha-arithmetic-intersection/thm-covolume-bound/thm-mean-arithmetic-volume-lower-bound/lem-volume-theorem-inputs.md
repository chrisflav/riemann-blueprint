The inputs of the volume theorem hold if the following do: integral models
of unitary Shimura varieties ([thm-unitary-integral-models]) with
compatibility of toroidal compactifications under change of level and
Hecke operators; a key formula for arithmetic degrees; extension of
homomorphisms of semi-abelian schemes; numerical Segre classes; resolution
in projective space; Fargues' Hodge–Tate sequence; GAGA for sections;
lines of large degree ([lem-absolute-siegel-lemma]); the inputs on flag
models; the Hilbert–Samuel inequalities; analytic estimates; and Zhang's
bounds for heights on Grassmannians.

The inputs consist of these statements together with a slope bound for
hermitian lattices and a perturbation lemma for antisymmetric forms.
