Resolution, principalisation and the Poincaré–Lelong formula give an
arithmetic intersection theory. With Fargues' Hodge–Tate sequence, GAGA
for sections, quotients by finite flat group schemes and Zarhin's theorem,
[thm-good-towers-from-arakelov-inputs] gives good towers of flag bundles and the
volume theorem. The inputs it needs come from
[lem-volume-theorem-inputs] and [lem-absolute-siegel-lemma], with the
integral models of [thm-unitary-integral-models] and covers of flag type
([lem-flag-cover-pullback]). The mean slope bound of the volume
theorem, pulled back from the tower to $X$, is the claim.
