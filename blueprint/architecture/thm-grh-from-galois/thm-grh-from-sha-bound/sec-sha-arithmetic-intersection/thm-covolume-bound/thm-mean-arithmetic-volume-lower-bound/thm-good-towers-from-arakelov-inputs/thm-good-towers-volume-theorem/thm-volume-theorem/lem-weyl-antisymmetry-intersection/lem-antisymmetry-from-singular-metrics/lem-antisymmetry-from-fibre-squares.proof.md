For a simple reflection, the flag bundle is a $\mathbb P^1$-bundle over a
partial flag bundle, and the square formed by its two projections exchanges
$\lambda$ and $s\lambda$. Flag covers ([lem-flag-cover-pullback])
realise the square, and the fibre-square statement gives the identity.
