Let $E$, the datum and $Y$ be as in [thm-good-towers-volume-theorem]. Suppose
there are a geometric bound with constants $C_h, C_u$ for the nine geometric
quantities of the tower, a comparison of de Rham and Hodge–Tate flags at
good primes, triviality of the flag bundle on the Hodge part, a flat-degree
statement, a statement on twisted automorphisms, and the two comparison
statements for intersection numbers. Then the volume theorem of
[thm-good-towers-volume-theorem] holds.
