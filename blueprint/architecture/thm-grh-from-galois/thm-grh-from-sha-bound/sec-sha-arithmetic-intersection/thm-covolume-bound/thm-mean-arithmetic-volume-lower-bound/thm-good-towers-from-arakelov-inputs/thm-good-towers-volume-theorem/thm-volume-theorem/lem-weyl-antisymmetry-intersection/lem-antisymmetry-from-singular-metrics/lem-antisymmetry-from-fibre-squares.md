The Weyl antisymmetry of [lem-weyl-antisymmetry-intersection] follows from
statements on the existence of flag models, their algebraic and height
properties, the singular-metric statement of
[lem-antisymmetry-from-singular-metrics], the base and comparison statements,
and antisymmetry on fibre squares: a square of flag models attached to a
simple reflection makes $\Delta(\lambda) + \Delta(s\lambda)$ vanish.
