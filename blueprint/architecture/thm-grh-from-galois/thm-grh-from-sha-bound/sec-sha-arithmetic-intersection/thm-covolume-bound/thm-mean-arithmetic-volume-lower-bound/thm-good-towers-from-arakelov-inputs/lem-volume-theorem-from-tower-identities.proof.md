The proof uses heights of flags, the properties of flag models, upper and
lower bounds for successive minima, a perturbation lemma for antisymmetric
forms and Zhang's lower bound for heights on Grassmannians. *We read* the
argument as follows: the identities make the arithmetic part of the
intersection numbers an antisymmetric function of the weight, hence a
multiple of the Weyl discriminant, and the bounds on successive minima and
Zhang's bound control the remaining terms. We have not unfolded the
computation.
