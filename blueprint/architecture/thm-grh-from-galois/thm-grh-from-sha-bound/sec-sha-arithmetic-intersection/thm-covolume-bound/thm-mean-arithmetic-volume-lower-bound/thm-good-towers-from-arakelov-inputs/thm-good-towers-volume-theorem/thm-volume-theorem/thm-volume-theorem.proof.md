The tower satisfies a list of identities: proportionality, the relations
$I_1, \dots, I_8$ between arithmetic intersection numbers of root line
bundles, nefness of the models, and a lower bound for slopes. The Weyl
antisymmetry $I_8$ is [lem-weyl-antisymmetry-intersection].
[lem-volume-theorem-from-tower-identities] turns these identities into the conclusions.
Multiplication by $n$, Hasse's and Landherr's theorems and flag covers
enter in checking the identities.
