The tower satisfies a list of identities: proportionality, the relations
$I_1, \dots, I_8$ between arithmetic intersection numbers of root line
bundles ([thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory]), nefness of the models, and a
lower bound for slopes. The Weyl antisymmetry $I_8$ is
[lem-weyl-antisymmetry-intersection].
[lem-volume-theorem-from-tower-identities] turns these identities into the
conclusions. Multiplication by $n$, Hasse's and Landherr's theorems
([thm-hasse-norm-quadratic], [thm-landherr-cm]), extension of homomorphisms
of semi-abelian schemes ([thm-semi-abelian-hom-extension]) and flag covers
([lem-flag-cover-pullback]) enter in checking the identities.
