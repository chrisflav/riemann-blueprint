Let $O$ be a discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with uniformiser
$\varpi$, and $X$ a model over $O$ of an arithmetic variety. Let a family of
Dieudonné lattices over $O$, with a Frobenius permutation $\varphi$ of the
embeddings, carry bundles $\mathrm{Lie}$, $W$, $\omega$, $\omega_{HT}$, and let
the de Rham and Hodge–Tate lattices form flag pairs relative to $\varpi$.
Then the local Chern data of these flags satisfy the relations that the
Frobenius $\varphi$ prescribes.
