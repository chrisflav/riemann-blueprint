The slope bound follows from lines of large degree: a line of degree at
least $\mu - \tfrac12\log i - \varepsilon$ in every sublattice bounds the
maximal slope. The other inputs are among the assumptions. The proof also
uses multiplication by $n$ on abelian schemes, Hasse's and Landherr's norm
theorems and semistable reduction.
