Assemble the auxiliary hypotheses: toroidal charts from the Siegel and
unitary compactifications, Green's regular characters with Green's
classification, the Fubini–Study formula for Chow heights, comparison of
Chow-form heights, and injectivity of small sections. The lower bound on
mean arithmetic volumes of automorphic line bundles comes from
[thm-mean-arithmetic-volume-lower-bound]. With these, [thm-covolume-bound-from-heights] gives the covolume bound.
Semistable reduction, Hasse's and Landherr's norm theorems, multiplication
by $n$ on abelian schemes and Serre's holomorphic sections enter through the
toroidal charts.
