[thm-grh-from-theta-integrals] assumes a bound on the covolumes of the
lattices in the families of definite unitary groups. This section proves
that bound ([top-covol-slim2-use-alt-alt]). The proof bounds heights on
covers of unitary Shimura varieties from below, using arithmetic volumes of
automorphic line bundles on towers of flag bundles over toroidal
compactifications, the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorem and Moriwaki's
bigness theorem.
