Assume the inputs on flag models. Let $M \to M'$ be a cover of flag models of
type $(\Psi, N, j)$ over an arithmetic variety of dimension $d + 2$, with a
base metric and an analytic flag structure, and a realisation of the cover.
Then for every $\tau \in \Psi$ and every pair $a, b = a+1$ of indices not
separated by $j$, the realisation carries a *square system* at $(\tau, a)$: a
hermitian line bundle, obtained from the base by pulling back along the two
projections of a square, whose log norm equals the log norm of the
combination of root lines plus the archimedean potentials.

*It is not clear* what role each part of a square system plays; we read the
statement as the compatibility of flag covers with pull-back of metrics.
