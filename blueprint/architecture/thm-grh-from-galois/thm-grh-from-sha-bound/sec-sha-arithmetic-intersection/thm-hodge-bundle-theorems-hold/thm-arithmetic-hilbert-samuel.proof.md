Reduce to the case of a smooth generic fibre by principalisation
([thm-principalisation-projective]), then compare small sections with the
arithmetic degree ([thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory]). The
Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem ([thm-kawamata-viehweg]) controls the
higher cohomology.
