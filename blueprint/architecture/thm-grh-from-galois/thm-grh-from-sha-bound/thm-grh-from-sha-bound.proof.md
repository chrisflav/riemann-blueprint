Collect the published results of [chap-literature]. A few need the
hypotheses. The local Langlands correspondence for $\GL_n$, in the weak form
of [def-local-parameters], follows from the local parameters with the local
converse theorem and the theory of Bernstein and Zelevinsky. The
compatibility of Galois representations of unitary groups follows from
[def-galois-reps-polarized]. With these results, the theorem is a case of
[thm-grh-from-theta-integrals].
