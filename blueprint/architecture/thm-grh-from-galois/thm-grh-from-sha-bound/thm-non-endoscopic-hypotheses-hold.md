The non-endoscopic case of [thm-grh-from-sha-bound] uses together the results
collected in [def-non-endoscopic-package]: the local representation theory of
$\GL_n$ ([def-local-rep-theory-package]), the results on automorphic forms and
Galois representations ([def-automorphic-galois-inputs]), Galois cohomology
([def-galois-cohomology-inputs]) and the
[functional equation of Hecke $L$-functions](@thm-hecke-functional-equation-cm). The statements on lattices
and zeta denominators over CM fields, for a system of
[local parameters](@def-local-parameters), come from the
[main theorem of complex multiplication](@thm-shimura-main-theorem-cm), the
theorem that $j$ of a CM point generates the
[Hilbert class field](@thm-singular-moduli-class-number), the
[functional equation of partial zeta functions](@thm-partial-zeta-functional-equation),
the theorem of [Siegel–Klingen and Deligne–Ribet](@thm-siegel-klingen-rationality)
and the [units of Chevalley](@thm-chevalley-congruence-units).
