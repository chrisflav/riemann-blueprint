[mix-seed-of-alt] produces a Hilbert modular form over $k_0$ whose Galois
representation is residually $1 \oplus \xi$, ordinary at $l$ with different
weights at two sets of places above $l$. Ribet's construction gives a
lattice on which it is a non-split extension. By automorphy of mixed
inductions the associated 4-dimensional representation is automorphic, and
its pseudo-representation over $F$ is the required seed.
