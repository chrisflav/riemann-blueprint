Assume the standard inputs and the inputs for Hilbert modular forms
([published-hypotheses-from-patching-alt]). Fix an auxiliary pair for a
primitive character with its $\psi$-fields, large for $t$, an odd prime $l$,
$0 < e < t$ and an even character $\chi$ of $\Gal(E/\Q)$ in the second or
third case of the proof of [vwin-of-hypotheses-h-e-alt]. Let $\bar\rho$ be a
residual representation $G_\Q \to \mathrm{GSp}_4$ of the kind attached to
$(l, e, \chi)$ there. Then:

* the ring generated by the traces of lifts of $\bar\rho$ of the prescribed
  type is finite over the coefficient ring;
* no such lift has block-diagonal image spanning the full block algebra;
* every irreducible lift of the prescribed type is automorphic over $\Q$.

*We read* the residual representations as the reducible representations
built from $\chi$ and powers of $\epsilon$ whose irreducible lifts, by
Ribet's method, produce the Selmer classes to be bounded.
