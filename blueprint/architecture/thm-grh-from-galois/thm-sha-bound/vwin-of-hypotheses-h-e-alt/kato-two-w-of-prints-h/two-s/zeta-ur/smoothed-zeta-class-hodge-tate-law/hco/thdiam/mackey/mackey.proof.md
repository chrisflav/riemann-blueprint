Decompose the induced class over the double cosets given by $R$ (Mackey's
formula). Each summand is the Kummer class of a single translate
$g_{c,d}\circ\gamma$ with $\gamma \in \Gamma_0(N)$, to which [one] applies.
Sum the congruences and collect the coboundaries into $\xi$.
