The diamond operator $\langle\delta\rangle$ permutes the translates of the
Siegel units, so it suffices to treat the sum over a set of coset
representatives, which [mackey] does. The twist to weight $2e - 1$ multiplies
the result by $t(s)$, and the coboundary $s(g) - g$ collects the terms from
changing representatives.
