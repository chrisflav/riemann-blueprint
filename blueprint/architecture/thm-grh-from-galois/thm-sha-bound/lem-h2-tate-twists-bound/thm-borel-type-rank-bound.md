Let $L$ be a totally imaginary number field with $r_2$ complex places, $k$
an even integer with $k + 1 < r_2$, $l \ge 1$ and $n \ge 2(k+1) + 4$. For
$d \ge 1$ let $\psi_d : \GL_n(\mathcal O_L) \to \GL_{dn}(\mathcal O_L)$ be
$A \mapsto 1_d \otimes A$, and $s$ the stabilisation map. For a class $x$ in
$H_{k+1}(\GL_n(\mathcal O_L), \Z)$ put $z_0(x) = x$ and
$$z_{t+1}(x) = \psi_l\big(z_t(x)\big) - l^{t+2}\,s\big(z_t(x)\big).$$
Then the subgroup of $H_{k+1}(\GL_{l^k n}(\mathcal O_L), \Z)$ generated by
the classes $z_k(x)$ has rank at most $r_2$.

This is in the spirit of Borel's computation of the rank of
$K_{2m-1}(\mathcal O_L)$, which is $r_2$ for a totally imaginary field: the
operators $\psi_d$ act on the Borel classes by $d^j$, and the sequence $z_t$
kills all weights but one.
