Suppose *mixed inductions are automorphic*: if $r$ is the Galois
representation of a Hilbert modular form over a totally real field $k_0$,
residually a non-split extension of $\xi$ by $1$, and $R$ is the
4-dimensional representation of $G_\Q$ built from $r$ and the mixing data of
the tower, then $R$ is automorphic, with the expected weights, over the CM
field of the tower. Then the seeds of [thm-potential-automorphy-seeds] exist.
