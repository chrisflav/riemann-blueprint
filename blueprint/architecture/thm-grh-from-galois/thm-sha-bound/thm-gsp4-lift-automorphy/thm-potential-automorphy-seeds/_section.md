Let $\bar\rho$ be a residual representation as in [thm-gsp4-lift-automorphy],
and consider a tower of fields of the kind used for potential automorphy of
its lifts, with totally real top field $F$, deep enough and of the second
type. Then, after enlarging the coefficients, there is a lift of the
pseudo-representation of $\bar\rho|_{G_F}$ that is a *seed*: it is
continuous, reduces to $\bar\rho|_{G_F}$, is attached to an automorphic
representation, lies in the component of the deformation space that the
tower prescribes, and its reduction has nonzero entries at prescribed root
positions of $\mathrm{GSp}_4$.
