Multiply $\varphi$ by a power of a partial Hasse invariant, a form of
weight $l - 1$ at the chosen embeddings that is congruent to $1$; this
gives a form of the higher weight congruent to $\varphi$. The Deligne–Serre
lifting lemma turns it into an eigenform with congruent eigenvalues, and the
factors $l^t$ come from the normalisation of the Hecke operators at $l$.
*We read* this from the shape of the statement; the steps of the proof are
not named in a way that confirms it.
