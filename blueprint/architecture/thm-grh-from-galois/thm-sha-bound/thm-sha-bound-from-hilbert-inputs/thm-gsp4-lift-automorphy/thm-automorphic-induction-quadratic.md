Let $M/K$ be a quadratic extension of totally imaginary number fields and
$\pi$ a cuspidal representation of $\GL_2(\A_M)$ whose infinitesimal
characters at the two embeddings above each embedding of $K$ differ, and
whose bad places split in $M/K$. Then there is a cuspidal representation
$\Pi$ of $\GL_4(\A_K)$ whose Satake parameters at the unramified places are
those of the induction of $\pi$, and whose infinitesimal character at each
embedding $\tau$ of $K$ is the union of those of $\pi$ at the two embeddings
of $M$ above $\tau$.

This is a case of automorphic induction (Arthur–Clozel, 1989).
