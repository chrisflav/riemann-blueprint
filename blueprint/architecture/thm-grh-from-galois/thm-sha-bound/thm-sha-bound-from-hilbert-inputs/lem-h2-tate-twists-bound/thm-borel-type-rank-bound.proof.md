The proof follows Borel; we have not checked its details. Continuous cohomology classes of
$\mathrm{SL}_N(\C)$, written as invariant differential forms on the symmetric
space, give real-valued functionals on cycles of $\GL_N(\mathcal O_L)$; each
is compatible with stabilisation and scales by $d^j$ under $\psi_d$ for some
weight $j \in [2, k+1]$. A surjectivity statement, proved with Hecke
operators, Whittaker functions and a spectral gap, shows that these
functionals, one weight-one functional for each complex place, detect the
homology in degree $k+1$ up to torsion. The recursion defining $z_t$
removes the weights $j \ge 2$, so at most $r_2$ functionals survive.
