Suppose the following, which the proof of [thm-kato-selmer-vanishing] derives
from local–global compatibility: whenever $W : G_\Q \to \GL_2(\Qlbar)$, $l$
odd, is continuous, odd, irreducible on the kernel of $\epsilon$ and $\det W$,
with $\det W$ quadratic, ordinary at $l$ with weights $(e, -e)$, of level
prime to $l$ after a twist, attached after a twist to a newform of weight
$2e+1$, and *spherical at $l$* (every cuspidal representation of
$\GL_2(\A_\Q)$ attached to a twist of $W$ unramified at $l$ is unramified at
$l$), then a twist of $W \otimes \epsilon^{-e}\det W^{-1}$ by a character of
order $l$ is attached to an $l$-ordinary newform of weight $2e+1$ and level
prime to $l$.

Then the conclusion of [thm-kato-selmer-vanishing] holds for every $W$ as there
that is spherical at $l$: the Bloch–Kato Selmer groups of $W$ and
$W \otimes \theta$ vanish.
