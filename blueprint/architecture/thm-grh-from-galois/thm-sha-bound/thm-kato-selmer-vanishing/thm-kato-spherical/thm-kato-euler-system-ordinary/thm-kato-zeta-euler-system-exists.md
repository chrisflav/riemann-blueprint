Assume that zeta classes are integral and norm-compatible
([thm-kato-zeta-integral]), that they satisfy the explicit reciprocity
law ([thm-kato-explicit-reciprocity]), and that the Hecke algebras of the
tower of modular curves are generated by the admissible Hecke operators.
Then for every $W$ and $g$ as in [thm-kato-euler-system-ordinary] there are an Euler system for $W$
with bottom class $y$, Iwasawa data $Z_r$ over the layers $\Q(\mu_{l^r})$
specialising to $y$, and a unit-valued character $\nu$, such that the image
of the $Z_r$ at $l$ is pinned down: it equals an explicit nonzero multiple of
the value of a Hecke-equivariant functional on Eisenstein series.
