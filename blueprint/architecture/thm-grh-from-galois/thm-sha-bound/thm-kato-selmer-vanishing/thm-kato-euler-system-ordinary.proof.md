Start from the integral norm-compatible zeta classes
([thm-kato-zeta-integral]) and the explicit reciprocity law
([thm-kato-explicit-reciprocity]), and use that the Hecke algebras of the
tower of modular curves are generated by the admissible Hecke operators.
Build the tower of modular curves with its ordinary Eisenstein
eigenfunctional and choose a measure on $(\Z/l^r)^\times$; smooth the zeta
classes by $1 - d_0\langle d_0\rangle$; average over Frobenius elements, which
[Chebotarev's theorem](@thm-chebotarev-degree-one-frobenius) supplies; and
compare the resulting values with the reciprocity law term by term, the
admissible Hecke operators acting on both sides through the eigenvalues of
$g$. This gives an Euler system for $W$ with bottom class $y$, Iwasawa data
$Z_r$ over the layers $\Q(\mu_{l^r})$ specialising to $y$, and a unit-valued
character $\nu$, such that the image of the $Z_r$ at $l$ equals an explicit
nonzero multiple of the value of a Hecke-equivariant functional on
Eisenstein series. It remains to see that the bottom class is not in the
ordinary local condition at $l$: a projection to the eigenspace of $g$ that
does not vanish on that value exists, so the class is not ordinary.
