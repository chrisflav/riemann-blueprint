The proof of the Tate–Shafarevich bound ([thm-sha-bound]
and the results refining it) uses together these facts on automorphic forms
over totally real and CM fields: regular algebraic cuspidal representations
over CM fields are cohomological and stable under $\mathrm{Aut}(\C)$
([Clozel](@thm-clozel-cohomological)), and few at each level (Borel); regular
algebraic polarized cuspidal representations of $\GL_2$ over totally real
fields have Galois representations ([thm-galois-reps-csd-above-l]); strong
multiplicity one ([thm-jacquet-shalika-strong-mult-one]), with the
[explicit spherical Whittaker function](@thm-shintani-casselman-shalika) at
unramified places, admissibility,
[Gelfand–Kazhdan duality](@thm-gelfand-kazhdan) and semisimplicity of cusp
forms ([thm-cusp-bounded-gl], [thm-multiplicity-one-gl]) for $\GL_n$ over
totally real fields; quadratic and cyclic base change, through
[base change from definite unitary groups](@thm-base-change-unitary); the
cuspidal representations of $\GL_2(\A_\Q)$ attached to newforms; totally
definite quaternion algebras over totally real fields of even degree; and
*weak descent* from $\GL_n$ over a CM field, $2 \le n \le 4$, to unitary
groups, which is proved elsewhere by a trace formula.
