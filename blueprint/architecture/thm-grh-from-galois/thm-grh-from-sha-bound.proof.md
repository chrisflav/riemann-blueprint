The Galois representations of polarized pairs give, through the theorem of
Gelfand and Kazhdan, Galois representations for the cuspidal
representations that the families need: conjugate self-dual ones,
compatible above $l$ in the abelian case ([thm-galois-reps-csd-above-l]),
and polarized ones ([thm-galois-reps-polarized-cuspidal]). With these and
the Tate–Shafarevich bound, [thm-grh-from-theta-integrals] applies.
