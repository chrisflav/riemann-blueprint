The following hold for an abelian scheme $A$ over $R$: a dictionary between
Čech and hypercohomology; the Čech complex computes de Rham cohomology;
homotopy invariance, the quotient computation, Fubini for Čech complexes and
acyclicity on affines; descent along the cover; base change for the Čech
system; Stein factorisation and the cotangent complex; the comparison with
coherent cohomology and its finiteness; vanishing above degree $2g$;
perfectness over noetherian bases; base change of $H^1$ and the computation
of $H^2$ from fibres; the wedge structure; the Hopf algebra structure over
fields; and the cohomology of $1$-forms and $2$-forms.

These are the steps of the computation in [lem-hodge-filtration-abelian-scheme]; each is a
lemma, and *we have not* stated them separately.
