Below [thm-grh-from-galois] the proof is a chain of three reductions, stated
here as theorems in order, each reducing to the next.
[thm-grh-from-sha-bound] needs Galois representations of polarized pairs and
the Tate–Shafarevich bound. [thm-grh-from-theta-integrals] needs only the
Galois representations of the cuspidal representations that the families
use, with the same bound. [thm-zero-free-from-families] needs a class of
families satisfying the family hypotheses, which [thm-family-data-exists]
supplies, and hands over to [chap-zero-free].
