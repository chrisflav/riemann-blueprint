Let $K$ be a finite extension of $\Q_l$. There is a Hodge–Tate comparison map
for smooth proper $K$-varieties $M$ with a smooth model, injective in every
degree and compatible with the top-degree trace. If $M$ has pure dimension, it
is surjective in every degree $n$, and
$$\dim_{\Q_l} H^n_{\mathrm{et}}(M_{\bar K}, \Q_l) = \sum_{a=0}^{n} \dim_K H^{n-a}(M, \Omega^a)$$
(Faltings, 1988).
