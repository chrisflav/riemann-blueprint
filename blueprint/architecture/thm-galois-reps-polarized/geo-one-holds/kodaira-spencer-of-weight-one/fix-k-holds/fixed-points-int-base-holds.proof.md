*We read* the proof as the classical one: the complex points of the integral
model of [model-nonempty] are uniformised by the hermitian symmetric domain of
$G$, a coincidence point is fixed by an elliptic element of $G(\Q)$, and its
action on the tangent space is read off its eigenvalues. Serre's theorem on
holomorphic sections is also used. We have not unfolded the count.
