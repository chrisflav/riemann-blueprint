The proof uses the structure of multiplication by $n$ on abelian schemes, dual
abelian schemes, de Rham cohomology of abelian schemes, lifting of
homomorphisms along nilpotent thickenings with divided powers, and
Grothendieck's deformation theory. We have not unfolded how they combine.
