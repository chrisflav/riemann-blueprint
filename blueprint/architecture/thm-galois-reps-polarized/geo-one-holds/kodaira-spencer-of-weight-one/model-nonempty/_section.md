Let $D$ be a PEL datum of the kind of Harris and Taylor at a prime $p$: an order
$\mathcal O_D$ in a division algebra over a CM field $F$ with positive
involution, a hermitian lattice, and $a$ embeddings $t_i$ of $\mathcal O_F$ that
fix the signature. Let $R$ be a noetherian ring in which every integer prime to
$p$ is invertible and over which the embeddings $t_i$ are defined and pairwise
distinct modulo every prime of characteristic $p$. Let $N \ge 3$ be prime to $p$.

Then the moduli problem of abelian schemes with $\mathcal O_D$-action,
polarisation and level-$N$ structure of the given type, over $R$-schemes, is
represented by a quasi-projective $R$-scheme, smooth of relative dimension
$a(n-1)$ (Kottwitz 1992; Harris–Taylor 2001).
