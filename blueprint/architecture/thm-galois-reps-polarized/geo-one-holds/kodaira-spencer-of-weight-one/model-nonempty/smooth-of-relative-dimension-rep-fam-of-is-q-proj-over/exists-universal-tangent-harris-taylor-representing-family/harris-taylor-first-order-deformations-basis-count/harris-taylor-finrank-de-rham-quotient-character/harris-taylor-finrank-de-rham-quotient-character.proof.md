The characteristic polynomial of each element of $\mathcal O_F$ on
$H^{\mathrm{dR}}_1(A_0)$ is that on the Tate module
([charpoly-de-rham-homology-endomorphism-eq-map-ladic]), which the
condition on the type of the datum fixes (*we read* this as the signature
condition). Reading off the multiplicity of $\beta \circ t_i$ gives
the dimension.
