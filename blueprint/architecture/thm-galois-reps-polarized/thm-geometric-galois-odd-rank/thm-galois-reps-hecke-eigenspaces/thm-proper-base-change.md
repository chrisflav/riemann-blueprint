Let $A$ be a strictly henselian local ring with residue field $\kappa$, $q$
invertible in $A$, and $X$ proper over $A$. Restriction to the special fibre
$X_\kappa$ is an isomorphism on étale cohomology with coefficients $\Z/q(i)$.
