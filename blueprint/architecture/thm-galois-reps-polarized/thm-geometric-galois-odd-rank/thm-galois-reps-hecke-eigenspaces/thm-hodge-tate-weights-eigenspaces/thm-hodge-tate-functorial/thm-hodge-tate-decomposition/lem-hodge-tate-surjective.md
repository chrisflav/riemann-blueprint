Suppose an injective Hodge–Tate comparison map exists, compatible with the
top-degree trace, and that étale cohomology is finite-dimensional and has
classes of diagonals and squares in triple products with the expected
properties. Then the conclusion of [thm-hodge-tate-decomposition] holds.
