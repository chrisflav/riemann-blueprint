The finiteness theorem [thm-etale-finiteness] follows from a list of
about twenty-five basic statements of étale cohomology. Among them are the
projective bundle formula, the additive characteristic classes of
[thm-additive-characteristic-classes], proper base change ([thm-proper-base-change]),
Mayer–Vietoris sequences, homotopy invariance, the bound $1$ on the
cohomological dimension of smooth affine curves, and the identification of
the étale cohomology of a field with Galois cohomology. We have not checked
each of the others.
