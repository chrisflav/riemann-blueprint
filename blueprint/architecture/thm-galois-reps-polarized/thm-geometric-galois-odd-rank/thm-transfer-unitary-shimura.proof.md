Construct the group and its PEL datum from hermitian forms with the required
local invariants, using the Hasse norm theorem, and choose $\kappa$ to fix the
central character and the weights. The eigensystem $\theta$ comes from a spectral
argument on Hecke operators and their exponents. *It is not clear* from the
statements how $U$ is transferred to $G$: the proof cites no trace formula and
no published transfer result beyond the Hasse norm theorem.
