In the setting of [thm-hecke-fixed-points-kuga-sato], with all the embeddings $t_i$ equal to one
embedding $t_0$ and the datum of Harris–Taylor type with $a = m = 1$: the $k$-th
Kuga–Sato variety is smooth of relative dimension $n - 1 + kg$; the coincidence
points are finite in number, transversal, and in bijection with a finite set
of pairs (elliptic element of $G(\Q)$, point) that see the Hecke set only
trivially; and the sum of their Lefschetz terms is the weighted sum of orbital
integrals of [thm-hecke-fixed-points-kuga-sato].
