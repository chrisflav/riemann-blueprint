Coincidence points of a Hecke correspondence are abelian varieties with an
isogeny of the given type, hence CM points. [thm-coincidence-points-cm]
counts them and computes their tangent action, and the translations by the
$\alpha_s$ act on the fibres of the Kuga–Sato variety.
