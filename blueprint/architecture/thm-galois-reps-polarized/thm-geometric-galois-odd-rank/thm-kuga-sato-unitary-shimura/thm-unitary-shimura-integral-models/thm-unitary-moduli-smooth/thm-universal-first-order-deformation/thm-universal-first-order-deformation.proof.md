By representability, first-order deformations of $x$ are first-order
deformations of the abelian variety with its structure. By
[lem-first-order-deformation-count] these form a space with a
basis of $r$ elements; the deformation over
$k[\varepsilon_1, \dots, \varepsilon_r]$ in all these directions at once is $u$.
