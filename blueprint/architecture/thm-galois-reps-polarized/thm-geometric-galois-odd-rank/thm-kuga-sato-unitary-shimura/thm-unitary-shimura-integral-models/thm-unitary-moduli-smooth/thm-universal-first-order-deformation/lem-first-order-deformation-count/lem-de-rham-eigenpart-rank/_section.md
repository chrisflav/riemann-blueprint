Let $A_0$ over an algebraically closed field $k$ be as in
[lem-first-order-deformation-count], lying over a point
$\beta : R \to k$. For each $i$, the largest quotient of $H^{\mathrm{dR}}_1(A_0)$ on which
$\mathcal O_F$ acts through $\beta \circ t_i$ has dimension $mn$, for the integers $m, n$ of the
datum.
