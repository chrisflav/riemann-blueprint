Assume the holomorphic Lefschetz formula, and the fixed-point formula of
[thm-hecke-fixed-points-kuga-sato]. Then the last property in [thm-kuga-sato-unitary-shimura]
holds: for every dominant weight $\lambda$ there is $k$ such that, whenever a Hecke
eigensystem $\theta$ occurs in automorphic forms of weight $\lambda$ on $G$ (its
twisted trace against a Hecke polynomial is non-zero), the $\theta$-eigenspace in
$H^*(\Omega^h)$ of the $k$-th Kuga–Sato variety is non-zero for every Hodge
exponent $h$ of $\lambda$ at the embedding of signature $(1, n-1)$ and its
conjugates.
