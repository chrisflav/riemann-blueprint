We build the representations from definite unitary groups and theta lifts
([thm-theta-lift-nonvanishing]).

1. *Odd rank.* For a definite unitary group in an odd number $a$ of
   variables over a CM field containing $\sqrt{-d}$, we find $l$-adic
   representations matching the Satake parameters in the étale cohomology
   of a tower of varieties with Hecke correspondences, by a Lefschetz
   fixed-point formula. They are crystalline above $l$. It is not clear
   which varieties these are.
2. *Congruences.* Congruences between weights and a limiting argument in
   the style of Chenevier reach the remaining weights.
3. *Even rank.* Lift a representation of an even-rank unitary group in two
   ways to an odd-rank group. The two Galois representations have the form
   $\rho \otimes \kappa \oplus (\text{characters})$, and comparing them
   isolates $\rho$. We read this step from the names of the statements.

Base change from unitary groups to $\GL_a$ then gives the theorem.
