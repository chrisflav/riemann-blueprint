Suppose that polarized pairs have Galois representations compatible at the
unramified places, as in the conclusion of [core-all-of]. Then polarized pairs
have Galois representations in the sense of [def-galois-reps-polarized], for
the system of local parameters of [thm-local-parameters-exist].
