Suppose that the Fourier coefficients of Siegel Eisenstein series along a
parabolic subgroup split, for $\Re z$ large, as the Fourier coefficient of an
Eisenstein series on a smaller group plus a term that transforms by
$e^{\tau(n - 2z)}$ under dilations. Then the Siegel–Weil formula
[thm-siegel-weil-definite-k] holds.
