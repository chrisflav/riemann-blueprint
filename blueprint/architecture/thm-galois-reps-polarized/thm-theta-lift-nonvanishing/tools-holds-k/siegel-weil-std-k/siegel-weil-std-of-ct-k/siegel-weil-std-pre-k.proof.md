*We read* the argument as the classical one. Poisson summation makes the theta integral invariant under the Weyl element,
so it is an automorphic form with the same constant terms as the Eisenstein
series; their difference has vanishing constant term and is of moderate
growth on Siegel sets, hence zero. Landherr's classification of hermitian
forms and the Hasse norm theorem identify the global spaces that occur.
