For unitary dual pairs over a CM extension $E/E^+$, the following hold.

* The full Siegel–Weil formula, the doubling method, and the unfolding of the
  doubled theta integral to the zeta integral.
* The Weil representation has Schrödinger models attached to polarisations,
  split models, and vectors fixed by the relevant compact subgroups; the groups
  act on them as expected.
* Basic properties of theta functions; compactness and stabiliser statements in the doubled group;
  existence of the dual pairs needed in the theta tower.
* Two statements that lift identities from the real Lie algebra of the
  unitary group to the group.

These are the inputs of Rallis' argument in
[thm-theta-lift-nonvanishing]. *We read* most items from the names of the
statements; we have not unfolded them.
