Each member of the families of [chap-families] carries lattices $M_j$ of
rank $D$. This chapter proves that their covolumes are bounded in
terms of the size parameters of the family ([thm-covolume-bound]). The proof
that families exist ([thm-family-data-exists]) needs this bound.

We bound the covolumes by heights on unitary Shimura varieties. The first
step is a lower bound for the mean arithmetic volumes of automorphic line
bundles ([sec-ht-volumes]). Over an integral model we build a tower of flag
bundles of the Hodge bundles. The arithmetic intersection numbers of its
root line bundles change sign under simple reflections of the Weyl group,
and at the finite places the de Rham and Hodge–Tate flags have the same
Chern classes; together with the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel inequalities and
Moriwaki's theorem ([sec-ht-hilbert-samuel]) this bounds the volumes. The
second step turns the volume bound into a lower bound for heights on deep
covers, uniform over the members of a family, and from it into the
covolume bound ([sec-ht-deep-covers]).

The last three sections supply the geometry: integral models of unitary
Shimura varieties and Mumford's degenerating abelian varieties
([sec-ht-models]), toroidal compactifications with charts at the boundary
([sec-ht-toroidal]), and resolution of singularities in characteristic zero
([sec-ht-resolution]). These are known theorems; we state the forms the
argument uses and sketch their proofs.
