TODO-INTRO Proves [thm-covolume-bound]: the lattices on the indefinite side of the families have bounded covolume. Two parts: a lower bound for mean arithmetic volumes of automorphic line bundles on towers of flag bundles over toroidal compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties (arithmetic Hilbert-Samuel, Moriwaki's bigness, comparison of de Rham and Hodge-Tate flags), and a height argument on deep covers that turns it into the covolume bound. The geometric inputs are integral models, Mumford degenerations, toroidal compactifications with boundary charts, and resolution of singularities.
