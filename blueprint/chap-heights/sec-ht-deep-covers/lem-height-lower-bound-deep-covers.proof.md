The reference weight ([lem-reference-weights-deep-covers]) gives a section
of controlled norm. The lower bound for mean arithmetic volumes
([thm-mean-arithmetic-volume-lower-bound]), the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel
theorem ([thm-arithmetic-hilbert-samuel]) and Mumford's proportionality
through good metrics ([thm-mumford-good-metrics]) turn its norm into the
height bound. The toroidal compactifications
([thm-toroidal-compactification-siegel], [thm-toroidal-compactification-unitary])
and separation of points ([thm-angehrn-siu-point-separation]) supply the
sections. Semistable reduction ([thm-semistable-reduction]),
Grothendieck–Messing ([thm-grothendieck-messing]), formal algebraisation
([thm-grothendieck-existence]) and abelian de Rham cohomology
([thm-de-rham-abelian-schemes]) control the integral structure. Here and in
[lem-reference-weights-deep-covers] the published results on Hodge bundles
listed in [def-hodge-bundle-inputs] are used together.
