Follow Angehrn and Siu: build a multiplier ideal concentrated at the two
points, using a log resolution from principalisation
([thm-principalisation-projective]), and apply Nadel vanishing, which here
comes from the Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem ([thm-kawamata-viehweg]).
