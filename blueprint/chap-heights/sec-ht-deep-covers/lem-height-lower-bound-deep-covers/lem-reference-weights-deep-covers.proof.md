Construct the section from the Hodge bundle on the good cover, using the
published theorems on Hodge bundles ([def-hodge-bundle-inputs]); its norm is
bounded by the flat degree and the size condition. Abelian de Rham
cohomology ([thm-de-rham-abelian-schemes]), Grothendieck–Messing
([thm-grothendieck-messing]) and formal algebraisation
([thm-grothendieck-existence]) give the integral structure.
