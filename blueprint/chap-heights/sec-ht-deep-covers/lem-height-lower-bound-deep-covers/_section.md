Suppose that deep covers are good and that the size, cover-degree and
flat-degree conditions hold. Then the heights on deep covers are bounded
below: for every class of families satisfying the family hypotheses there is
$C$ such that, for every member at every large scale, every good deep cover
$Y$ and every quotient weight $u_1$, there are $c_s, c_c, X \ge 0$ and slopes
$s_Z$ on the connected components $Z$ with
$$h_{u_1}(Z) + c_s \ge 0, \qquad (1 - \rho(u_1))\max(0, s_Z + c_s) \le h_{u_1}(Z) + c_s + c_c,$$
$$\frac{1}{\#\pi_0}\sum_Z s_Z \ge -X,$$
and $X$, $c_c$ and $c_s$ times explicit dimensions are at most $C$ times the
unit error of the member, divided by the length of the tower.
