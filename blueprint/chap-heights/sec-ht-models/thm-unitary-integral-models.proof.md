The Siegel moduli come from the existence of full-level ample models
([thm-full-level-ample-models]). The unitary models are the closures of the
unitary Shimura varieties in them, and Grothendieck's semistable reduction
theorem ([thm-semistable-reduction]) controls their reductions.
