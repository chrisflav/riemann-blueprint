Heights are computed on integral models. This section builds
integral models of unitary Shimura varieties inside Siegel moduli schemes
([thm-unitary-integral-models]), starting from full-level models whose
universal abelian scheme carries an ample line bundle with theta
coordinates ([thm-full-level-ample-models]). It also gives Mumford's
construction of degenerating abelian varieties over complete normal local
rings ([thm-mumford-degeneration]), which the boundary charts of
[sec-ht-toroidal] use.
