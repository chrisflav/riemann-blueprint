Let $R$ be as in [thm-mumford-degeneration] and
$D$ a set of degeneration data over $R$. Suppose $D$ has a *labelled cover*:
a modification of $\mathrm{Spec}\,R$ carrying a semi-abelian scheme, a
polarised abelian variety over the fraction field contained in it, an odd
prime $n$ invertible in $R$, labellings of the $n^j$-torsion of all levels
$j$, and uniformisations over complete discrete valuation rings through
every point. Then, after a finite extension of $R$, the data $D$ are the
degeneration data of a semi-abelian scheme with Mumford uniformisation.
