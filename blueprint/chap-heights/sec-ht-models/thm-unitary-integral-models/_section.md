* For every genus $h$, every prime $p$ and every odd prime $n \ne p$, some
  level $n^j \ge 3$ has a Siegel moduli scheme of principally polarised
  abelian schemes over $\Z_{(p)}$.
* For every CM field $E$ and every unitary Shimura datum of signature
  $(p, q)$ with $p + q \ge 1$, there are integral models over the valuation
  rings of $\C$ above each prime $p$, at all levels and coefficient fields,
  with transition maps between levels, embeddings into Siegel moduli
  compatible with the transitions, and split fibres.
* At levels good at every prime, there is a model over the ring of integers
  of the coefficient field that descends and dominates all these local
  models.
