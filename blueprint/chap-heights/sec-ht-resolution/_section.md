Arithmetic intersection theory works on regular models, and separation of
points needs log resolutions. This section proves functorial resolution of
singularities in characteristic zero ([thm-functorial-resolution]) by the
canonical principalisation algorithm of Villamayor and Włodarczyk. The
algorithm works by induction on dimension, inside hypersurfaces of maximal
contact ([thm-principalisation-reduces-dimension]); the step that lowers the
order is [lem-order-step-reduces-order].
