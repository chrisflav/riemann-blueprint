The proof uses three facts, all proved: the strict transform of a closed
subscheme is the blow-up of that subscheme along the restricted centre;
divisor reduction has its expected properties; and the geometric facts of
principalisation hold (existence of hypersurfaces of maximal contact and the
behaviour of orders under blow-up). With these, check the four clauses one at
a time. The divisor clauses follow from the properties of divisor reduction.
The order clause is [lem-order-step-reduces-order]. The maximal-order clause
follows from it by the definition of the maximal-order step.
