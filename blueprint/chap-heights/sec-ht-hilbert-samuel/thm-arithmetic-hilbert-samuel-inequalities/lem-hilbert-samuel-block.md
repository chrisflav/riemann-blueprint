Let $X$ be an arithmetic variety of dimension $D + 1$ embedded by a block of
multihomogeneous coordinates, with a hermitian line bundle $\bar A$. Then the
arithmetic Euler characteristic of $A^n$ differs from
$\bar A^{D+1} n^{D+1}/(D+1)!$ by $o(n^{D+1})$, and there is $C$ with
$$\frac{|f(z)|}{h_A(z)^n} \le C\, n^{D/2}\, \|f\|_{L^2}$$
for every section $f$ of degree $n$ and every point $z$ (Gromov's inequality).
