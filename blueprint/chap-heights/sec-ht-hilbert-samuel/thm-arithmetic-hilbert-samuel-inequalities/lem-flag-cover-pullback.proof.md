A direct construction on the flag model. Pull the base line bundle back
along the two projections of the square. The change-of-metric and
projection formulas of arithmetic intersection theory
([thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory]) compare the two log norms; the
difference is the combination of root lines plus the archimedean
potentials.
