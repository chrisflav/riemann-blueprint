The proof combines eighteen statements about blocks: the index of block
pieces, finiteness of the block modules, the Hilbert function of module
pieces, the cycle of a block module, coordinate changes and changes of model,
the degree of projective frame graphs, transport of the Hilbert–Samuel
property, the degree and mixed terms of resolutions and of block resolutions,
the image of block resolutions, integrability of Green functions at points,
inverse images of blocks, and changes of model for graphs. They combine in
sequence: the module statements give the algebraic Hilbert function, the
resolution and graph statements compute the arithmetic degree, and
integrability gives the archimedean term. Each is proved, mostly by flag
covers ([lem-flag-cover-pullback]), using resolution in projective space
([thm-hironaka-resolution]) and the properties of arithmetic intersection
numbers ([thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory]).
