The volume bound of [sec-ht-volumes] counts small sections of hermitian line
bundles. This section proves the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel inequalities,
Moriwaki's inequality $\bar L^{D+1} \le \widehat{\mathrm{vol}}(\bar L)$ and
the arithmetic Kleiman criterion
([thm-arithmetic-hilbert-samuel-inequalities]). The proof first treats a
variety embedded by one block of multihomogeneous coordinates
([lem-hilbert-samuel-block]) and passes to the general case by pull-back
along covers of flag type ([lem-flag-cover-pullback]).
