[thm-unitary-rigid-boundary-charts] gives a compactification with the first
three properties and rigid charts at closed boundary points.
[lem-kodaira-spencer-from-rigid-charts] deduces the Kodaira–Spencer
isomorphism from the rigid charts. For the moduli form, choose a suitable
cone decomposition and apply the first part to it.
