For every $g \ge 1$ there is a polarised admissible cone decomposition
$\varphi$ such that, for every odd $n \ge 3$, the Siegel moduli scheme $A_{g,n}$
of principally polarised abelian schemes with level-$n$ structure over
$\Z[\zeta_n, 1/n]$ has a toroidal compactification $S$ with:

* $S$ projective and smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$, with $A_{g,n}$
  dense in every fibre;
* a semi-abelian scheme $G$ over $S$ of relative dimension $g$ that extends
  the universal abelian scheme, with abelian locus exactly $A_{g,n}$;
* a boundary divisor $\Delta$ with charts of Mumford type at every point;
* the Hodge bundle $\omega = \det \mathrm{Lie}(G)^\vee$ with $\omega^N$
  generated by global sections, and $\omega^{Nk}(-\Delta)$ ample for all large
  $k$.
