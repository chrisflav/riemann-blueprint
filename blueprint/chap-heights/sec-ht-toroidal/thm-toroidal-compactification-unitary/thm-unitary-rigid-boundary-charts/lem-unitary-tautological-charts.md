In the situation of [lem-unitary-boundary-separation], let $x$ be a point
of $S$ outside $X$. Then there are a field $k$, a power series ring
$k[[t_1, \dots, t_m, s_1, \dots, s_e]]$, a Mumford family for a cone of
$\varphi$ over it, of the type of the unitary datum, and a map from its
spectrum to $S$ that sends the closed point to $x$ and the generic point
into $X$.
