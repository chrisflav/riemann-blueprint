Arithmetic intersection numbers need proper models, so the chapter works on
toroidal compactifications. This section proves that unitary Shimura
varieties have smooth toroidal compactifications with boundary a divisor
with simple normal crossings and a logarithmic Kodaira–Spencer isomorphism
([thm-toroidal-compactification-unitary]). From a minimal compactification
and the blow-up that a cone decomposition determines, we build rigid charts
of Mumford type at the boundary points
([thm-unitary-rigid-boundary-charts]) and compute the Kodaira–Spencer map in
them ([lem-kodaira-spencer-from-rigid-charts]). Siegel moduli schemes are
treated the same way ([thm-toroidal-compactification-siegel]).
