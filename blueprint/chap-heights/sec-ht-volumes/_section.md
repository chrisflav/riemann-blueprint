This section proves the lower bound for mean arithmetic volumes of
automorphic line bundles on unitary Shimura varieties
([thm-mean-arithmetic-volume-lower-bound]), which the height argument of
[sec-ht-deep-covers] uses. Over an integral model we build a tower of flag
bundles of the Hodge bundles, whose root line bundles make up the
automorphic line bundles, and prove the volume theorem on it
([thm-good-towers-from-arakelov-inputs]). The proof needs two facts: the
arithmetic self-intersections, less their main term, change sign under
simple reflections of the Weyl group ([lem-weyl-antisymmetry-intersection]),
and at the finite places the de Rham and Hodge–Tate flags have the same
Chern classes ([lem-de-rham-hodge-tate-flag-comparison]), by Dieudonné theory
over $\mathcal O_C$ ([thm-dieudonne-theory-oc]). Lines of large degree in
hermitian lattices ([lem-absolute-siegel-lemma]) bound the slopes.
