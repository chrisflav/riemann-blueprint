Let $T$ be a tower of flag bundles with proportional volumes, satisfying the
identities $I_1, \dots, I_8$, nefness of its models, the propositions on
Frobenius, on positivity and on fibres with constants $C', r_d, L$, equality
of volumes, and a lower bound for slopes with constant $C$. Then $T$
satisfies the conclusions of the volume theorem
([thm-good-towers-from-arakelov-inputs], part 2) with constant $2C + 3C' + 5$.
