Results on abelian varieties and abelian schemes: torsion, principal
polarisations and Zarhin's trick, semi-abelian schemes and semistable
reduction, deformation theory and algebraisation, and toroidal
compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties. A *principally polarised
abelian scheme* here is an abelian scheme with a rigidified, symmetric,
relatively ample line bundle $L$ whose group $K(L)$ is trivial.
