Let $C$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve over a field
of characteristic $0$, and $G$ a semi-abelian scheme over $C$. For every
vector bundle $W \subset \mathrm{Lie}(G)$ of rank $r$,
$\deg \det W \le 0$.

Equivalently, the Hodge bundle $\omega_G = \mathrm{Lie}(G)^\vee$ has only
quotients of non-negative degree: it is semi-positive.
