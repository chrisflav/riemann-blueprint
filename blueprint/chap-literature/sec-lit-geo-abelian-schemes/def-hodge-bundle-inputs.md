The study of Hodge bundles and heights on unitary Shimura varieties uses
these published results together. On abelian schemes:
[thm-abelian-variety-torsion-count], [thm-principal-polarisation-one-section],
[thm-semi-abelian-hom-extension], [thm-semistable-reduction],
[thm-de-rham-abelian-schemes], [thm-grothendieck-messing-nilpotent-p],
[thm-dieudonne-theory-oc], [thm-grothendieck-existence] and
[thm-toroidal-compactification-unitary-lit]. On complex and birational
geometry: [thm-kawamata-viehweg], [thm-ohsawa-takegoshi-points],
[thm-gaga-sections], [thm-principalisation-projective] and [thm-chow-form].
On heights and Arakelov geometry: [thm-arithmetic-hilbert-samuel],
[thm-arithmetic-volume-nef], [thm-arithmetic-volume-bound],
[thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory] and [thm-mumford-good-metrics]. On
arithmetic and groups: [thm-ray-class-field], [thm-hasse-norm-quadratic],
[thm-landherr-cm], [thm-strong-approximation-su],
[thm-green-parametrisation] and [thm-jantzen-lowest-alcove].
