We collect under one name the published results that the study of Hodge
bundles and heights on unitary Shimura varieties uses:

* on abelian schemes: [thm-abelian-variety-torsion-count], [thm-principal-polarisation-one-section],
  [thm-semi-abelian-hom-extension], [thm-semistable-reduction],
  [thm-de-rham-abelian-schemes], [thm-grothendieck-messing-nilpotent-p], [thm-dieudonne-theory-oc],
  [thm-grothendieck-existence] and [thm-toroidal-compactification-unitary-lit];
* on complex and birational geometry: [thm-kawamata-viehweg],
  [thm-ohsawa-takegoshi-points], [thm-gaga-sections],
  [thm-principalisation-projective] and [thm-chow-form];
* on heights and Arakelov geometry: [thm-arithmetic-hilbert-samuel],
  [thm-arithmetic-volume-nef], [thm-arithmetic-volume-bound], [thm-arithmetic-intersection-theory] and [thm-mumford-good-metrics];
* on arithmetic and groups: [thm-ray-class-field], [thm-hasse-norm-quadratic],
  [thm-landherr-cm], [thm-strong-approximation-su], [thm-green-parametrisation] and
  [thm-jantzen-lowest-alcove].
