Let $E$ be a CM field and consider the moduli space of abelian varieties
with an action of $\mathcal O_E$, a polarisation and level structure,
attached to a hermitian space of signature $(p, q)$ with $p + q \ge 1$. For
every level and every field of coefficients there is a smooth model
containing the moduli space as the complement of a divisor with simple
normal crossings, over which the Kodaira–Spencer map extends to an
isomorphism with logarithmic poles along the boundary.

This is the theory of toroidal compactifications, for unitary PEL data.
