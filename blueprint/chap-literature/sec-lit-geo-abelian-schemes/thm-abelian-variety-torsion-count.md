Let $B$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ over an algebraically closed
field $k$, and $n$ an integer invertible in $k$. Then $B$ has exactly
$n^{2g}$ points $x$ with $nx = 0$.

This is classical (Weil 1948; Mumford, *Abelian varieties*, §4), in the
form of a count of points.
