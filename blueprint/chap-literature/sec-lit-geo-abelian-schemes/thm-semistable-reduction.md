Let $K/\Q_p$ be a finite extension and $A$ a principally polarised abelian
variety over $K$. There is a finite extension $K'/K$ and a semi-abelian
scheme over the ring of integers of $K'$ whose generic fibre is $A_{K'}$.

This is Grothendieck's theorem (SGA 7, IX, 3.6), stated here for
principally polarised abelian varieties over $p$-adic fields.
