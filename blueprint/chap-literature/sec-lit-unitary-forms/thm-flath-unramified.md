Let $E/F$ be a CM extension, $V$ a non-degenerate hermitian space over $E$
and $G = \U(V)$. Every irreducible unitary representation $\pi$ of
$G(\A_F)$ is a restricted tensor product $\pi \cong \bigotimes'_v \pi_v$ of
irreducible unitary representations, with respect to unit vectors $\xi_v$,
such that every finite factor $\pi_v$ is admissible and, for every
$\mathcal O_E$-lattice $L$ in $V$, for almost all $v$ the vectors of $\pi_v$
fixed by the stabiliser of $L_v$ form the line spanned by $\xi_v$.
