Let $E/F$ be a CM extension, $V$ a non-degenerate hermitian space over $E$ of
dimension $M \ge 2$, and $S$ a finite set of places of $F$ such that
$\mathrm{SU}(V)(F_S)$ is not compact. Then $\mathrm{SU}(V)(F)\,\mathrm{SU}(V)(F_S)$
is dense in $\mathrm{SU}(V)(\A_F)$. This is strong approximation for the
simply connected group $\mathrm{SU}(V)$.
