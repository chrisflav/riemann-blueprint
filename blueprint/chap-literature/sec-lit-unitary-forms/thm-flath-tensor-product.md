Let $E/F$ be a CM extension, $V$ a non-degenerate hermitian space over $E$
and $G = \U(V)$. Every irreducible unitary representation $\pi$ of
$G(\A_F)$ is a restricted tensor product $\pi \cong \bigotimes'_v \pi_v$ of
irreducible unitary representations $\pi_v$ of $G(F_v)$, with respect to a
family of unit vectors $\xi_v$.
