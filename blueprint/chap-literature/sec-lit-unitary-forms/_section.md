Throughout, $E/F$ is a CM extension of number fields, $V$ a non-degenerate
hermitian space over $E$ and $G = \U(V)$. The section gives the finite
volume of $G(F)\backslash G(\A_F)$, reduction theory and strong
approximation; admissibility and Flath's factorisation of irreducible
unitary representations of $G(\A_F)$; and the analytic properties of cusp
forms: uniform rapid decay, Sobolev bounds, boundedness, trace class of
convolution operators and Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem. The theta
lifts of [chap-theta] and the families of [chap-families] work with
automorphic forms on these groups.
