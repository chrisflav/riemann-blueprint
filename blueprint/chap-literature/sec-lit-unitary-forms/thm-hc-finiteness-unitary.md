Let $G = \U(V)$ for a hermitian space over a CM field, $U$ an open subgroup
of $G(\A_F)$, and fix at each archimedean place a finite-dimensional
representation $\rho_\tau$ of the maximal compact subgroup and eigenvalues
$c_\tau$ for the generators of the centre of the enveloping algebra. The
smooth automorphic functions on $G(\A_F)$ that are right invariant under
$U$, of $K_\infty$-type $\rho$ and with those eigenvalues span a
finite-dimensional space.
