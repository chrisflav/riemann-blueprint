If no $\sigma_a$ is an unramified twist of a $\sigma'_b$, then in particular
none is a twist by $1$ or $\nu^{\pm1}$, so the Bernstein–Zelevinsky
irreducibility criterion recorded in [thm-bz-finiteness] applies.
