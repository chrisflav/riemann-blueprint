Write $\pi$ as a product of unlinked segment representations
([thm-zelevinsky-segments]). Unitarity forces the segments to
pair off under $\nu^s \mapsto \nu^{-s}$, with exponents bounded by
$1/2$; the finite length and Jacquet module computations come from
[thm-bz-finiteness], [thm-bz-functors-finite-length],
[thm-jacquet-irreducible-admissible], [thm-geometric-lemma] and
[thm-geometric-lemma-filtration].
