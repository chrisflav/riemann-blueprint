Smooth complex representations of $\GL_n(F)$ for a non-archimedean local
field $F$. We write $\pi_1 \times \pi_2$ for parabolic induction and
$\nu = |\det|$. The section gives the finiteness theorems of Bernstein and
Zelevinsky with the geometric lemma, the Borel–Casselman description of
Iwahori-fixed vectors, and Zelevinsky's classification by segments; from it
come the square-integrable, tempered and generic unitary representations
and the irreducibility of products. It ends with the spherical Whittaker
function of Shintani and Casselman–Shalika, Henniart's local converse
theorem and Radford's theorem that a shuffle algebra is a polynomial
algebra.
