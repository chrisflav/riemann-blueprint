Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and $A$ a totally ordered
alphabet. The shuffle algebra on $A$ over $K$ is the polynomial algebra on
the Lyndon words: the map from the polynomial ring in one variable for each
Lyndon word, sending the variable to the word, is an isomorphism of
$K$-algebras.
