The local results about $\GL_n$ of a $p$-adic field are used together in
the non-endoscopic case ([def-non-endoscopic-package]) and in checking the
hypotheses on the members of a family
([prop-member-hypotheses-from-literature]): Bernstein–Zelevinsky theory
[thm-bz-finiteness], Bernstein's irreducibility of unitary products
[thm-bernstein-unitary-product], the geometric lemma [thm-geometric-lemma],
Zelevinsky's classification [thm-zelevinsky-segments] and the
irreducibility criterion for disjoint cuspidal supports,
[thm-moy-prasad-jacquet], the tempered dual [thm-tempered-products],
square-integrable representations [thm-square-integrable-segment], the
existence of local parameters ([def-local-parameters]), Henniart's local
converse theorem [thm-henniart-local-converse], the depth-zero criterion
[thm-depth-zero-from-support], [thm-brauer-induction-degree-zero],
[thm-chevalley-congruence-units] and [thm-green-regular-characters].
