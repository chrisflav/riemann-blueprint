Let $X$ be smooth, quasi-compact and separated over a field of
characteristic $0$, $E \subset X$ a closed subset with simple normal
crossings, and $\mathcal I$ an ideal sheaf whose support contains no
irreducible component of $X$. There is a sequence of blow-ups $X' \to X$
with centres in normal crossings with the accumulated boundary, $X'$
smooth, an isomorphism outside the support of $\mathcal I$, such that the
pull-back of $\mathcal I$ is the ideal of an effective Cartier divisor with
simple normal crossings support.

This is the principalisation theorem relative to a given boundary.
