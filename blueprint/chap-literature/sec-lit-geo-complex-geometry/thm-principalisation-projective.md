Let $Y$ be an integral smooth projective variety over a field of
characteristic $0$ and $\mathcal I \ne 0$ an ideal sheaf. There is a
sequence of blow-ups along smooth centres $s : Y' \to Y$ over the support of
$\mathcal I$, with $Y'$ integral, smooth and projective, which is an
isomorphism outside the support of $\mathcal I$ and such that
$s^{-1}\mathcal I$ is the ideal of an effective Cartier divisor with simple
normal crossings support.

This is Hironaka's principalisation theorem (1964), in the algorithmic form
of Bierstone–Milman and Villamayor.
