Let $Y$ be a smooth projective variety over $\C$ and $B$ a line bundle on
$Y$. Every holomorphic section of $B$ over the complex manifold $Y(\C)$ is
algebraic: the map from algebraic sections to holomorphic sections is a
bijection.

This is the case $H^0$ of Serre's GAGA (1956).
