Let $F$ be a number field, $l$ a prime, $S$ a finite set of finite places
containing those above $l$, $G_S$ the Galois group of the maximal extension
unramified outside $S$ and the infinite places, and $M$ a finite
$\Z/l^r$-module, $r > 0$, with a continuous action of $G_S$. Then
$H^i(G_S, M)$ is finite for $i = 0, 1, 2$, and if $l \ne 2$ it vanishes for
$i \ge 3$ (Tate, 1962).
