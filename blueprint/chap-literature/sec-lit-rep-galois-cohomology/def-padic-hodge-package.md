This is not a theorem but the conjunction of seven published results:
Fontaine's period ring $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ ([thm-fontaine-bcris]), global
Artin reciprocity ([thm-artin-reciprocity]), the Bloch–Kato local
conditions ([thm-bloch-kato-local]), and, for finite extensions of $\Q_l$,
local Tate duality ([thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic]), the local Euler
characteristic formula ([thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic]), finiteness
([thm-tate-local-finiteness-padic]) and the integral comparison theorem of Bhatt and
Scholze ([thm-bhatt-scholze-crystalline-lattices]).
