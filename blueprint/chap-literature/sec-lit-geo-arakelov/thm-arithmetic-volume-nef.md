Let $X$ be an arithmetic variety of dimension $D$ and $\bar L$ a nef
hermitian line bundle on $X$ with arithmetic self-intersection
$\widehat{\deg}(\hat c_1(\bar L)^D) = a$. Then the arithmetic volume of
$\bar L$ is $a$.

This is a theorem of Moriwaki (2009), a consequence of the arithmetic
Hilbert–Samuel formula of Gillet–Soulé and Zhang. We derive it from that
formula ([thm-arithmetic-hilbert-samuel]).
