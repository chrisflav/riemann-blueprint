Let $G = \U(p,q)$, $\sigma$ an irreducible unitary representation of $G$ on
$H$, and $E$ a smooth Fréchet representation of $G$ of moderate growth.
Every $(\mathfrak g, K)$-map $T_0$ from the $K$-finite vectors of $H$ to $E$
extends to a continuous $G$-map $T$ on the smooth vectors of $H$. The
extension is unique, its image is closed, and if $T_0 \ne 0$ every Sobolev
norm of a smooth vector $w$ is bounded by a continuous seminorm of $T(w)$.
This is the automatic continuity theorem, for unitary groups.
