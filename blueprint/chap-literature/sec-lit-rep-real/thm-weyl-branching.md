Let $N = n + 1$ with $n \ge 1$ and $\lambda$ a dominant weight of $\GL_N$.
The restriction of the irreducible representation of highest weight
$\lambda$ to $\GL_n(\C)$, and to the compact group $\U(n)$, is the direct
sum of the irreducible representations of highest weight $\mu$ over the
dominant $\mu$ that interlace $\lambda$,
$\lambda_1 \ge \mu_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge \mu_n \ge \lambda_N$, each
with multiplicity one.
