Harish-Chandra's theory of $(\mathfrak g, K)$-modules, globalisation and the
discrete series, mostly for $\U(p,q)$ with $K = \U(p)\times\U(q)$. A
*Harish-Chandra parameter* is a strictly decreasing $\lambda \in \R^{p+q}$
with every $\lambda_a - (p+q-1)/2$ an integer; $\rho_c$ and $\rho_n$ are the
half sums of compact and non-compact positive roots.
