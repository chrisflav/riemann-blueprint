For every $\U(p,q)$ and every Harish-Chandra parameter $\lambda$, a discrete
series representation with parameter $\lambda$ exists. The statement is the
same as [thm-discrete-series-exist-upq], put in terms of the notion of a
discrete series representation with parameter $\lambda$.
