Let $G = \U(p,q)$ and $\lambda$ a Harish-Chandra parameter. Every discrete
series representation of $G$ with parameter $\lambda$ contains its lowest
$K$-type $\lambda + \rho_n - \rho_c$ on the expected subspace, and any two
discrete series representations with parameter $\lambda$ are unitarily
equivalent. It follows from
[thm-hc-discrete-series-classification].
