The construction of Galois representations uses these results together:
rapid decay of cusp forms ([thm-cusp-rapid-decay]); Harish-Chandra's
finiteness theorem ([thm-hc-finiteness-unitary]); finiteness of spaces of
automorphic forms on $\GL_n$ ([thm-hc-finiteness-gl]); Jacquet–Shalika
([thm-jacquet-shalika]); the cuspidal spectrum of $\GL_n$
([thm-cuspidal-spectrum-gl]); base change from unitary groups
([thm-base-change-unitary]); Gelfand–Kazhdan ([thm-gelfand-kazhdan]);
Clozel's purity and cohomologicity theorems ([thm-clozel-purity],
[thm-clozel-cohomological]); Artin reciprocity and ray class fields
([thm-artin-reciprocity], [thm-ray-class-field]); the results on Hecke
characters above $l$ ([thm-hecke-l-adic-character-above-l],
[thm-hecke-character-wd-above-l]); Berger's theorem
([thm-p-adic-monodromy]); and the segment classification of essentially
square-integrable representations ([thm-zelevinsky-segments]).

The same collection also contains, in two slightly different forms, the
existence of Galois representations for polarized pairs
([def-galois-reps-polarized]), which is the conclusion of
[thm-galois-reps-polarized] rather than a published result.
