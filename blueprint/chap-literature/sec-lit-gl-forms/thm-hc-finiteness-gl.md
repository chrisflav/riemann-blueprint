Let $E$ be a totally complex number field, $L$ an open subgroup of
$\GL_a(\A_E^\infty)$, $\rho$ a finite-dimensional continuous representation
of the maximal compact subgroup $K_\infty$ of $\GL_a(E\otimes\R)$, and $c$ a
choice of eigenvalues of the Casimir operators at the complex places. The
automorphic forms on $\GL_a(\A_E)$ that are invariant under $L$, have
$K_\infty$-types in $\rho$ and have those Casimir eigenvalues span a
finite-dimensional space.
