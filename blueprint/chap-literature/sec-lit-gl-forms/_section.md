Cusp forms and cuspidal automorphic representations of $\GL_a(\A_E)$ for a
totally complex field $E$, usually a CM field: the cuspidal spectrum and
the boundedness of cusp forms, Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem,
multiplicity one and strong multiplicity one, the Jacquet–Shalika bound on
Satake parameters, the Gelfand–Kazhdan description of the contragredient,
and Clozel's theorems that regular algebraic cuspidal representations are
pure and cohomological. Two remarks record which of these, with results of
other sections, the construction of Galois representations and the
non-endoscopic case of the families use together.
