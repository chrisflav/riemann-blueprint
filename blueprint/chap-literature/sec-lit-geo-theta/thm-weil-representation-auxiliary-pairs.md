Over the auxiliary CM fields of the proof, let $S$ and $T$ be hermitian
spaces and $\chi_S, \chi_T$ unitary Hecke characters whose restrictions to
$\A_F^\times$ are $\eta^{\dim S}$ and $\eta^{\dim T}$. For the standard additive
character $\psi$ there is a Weil representation of
$\U(S)(\A_F) \times \U(T)(\A_F)$ attached to $\psi, \chi_S, \chi_T$.

This is the case of [thm-weil-representation-unitary-exists] that the theta lifts of the
proof use.
