Let $V$ be a hermitian space of rank $m$ and Witt index $r$ over the
auxiliary CM field, and $n \ge 1$. If $V$ is anisotropic or $n + r < m$,
then for every Haar measure on $\U(V)(\A_F)$ and every Schwartz function
$\Phi$ on $V^n(\A_E)$ of the admissible archimedean type, the integral of
$|\theta(g, h; \Phi)|$ over $\U(V)(F)\backslash \U(V)(\A_F)$ is finite.

It is stated in the unitary case.
