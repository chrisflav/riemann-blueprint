We collect under one name the published results of harmonic analysis that
the theta lifts between definite unitary groups use: Weil representations
for unitary pairs ([thm-weil-representation-auxiliary-pairs]); the existence and
classification of discrete series ([thm-discrete-series-exist],
[thm-hc-discrete-series-classification]); Harish-Chandra's analyticity theorem
([thm-hc-analyticity-density]); trace class of cusp forms
([thm-cusp-trace-class]); the Dixmier–Malliavin factorisation
([thm-dixmier-malliavin]); Flath's factorisation
([thm-flath-unramified]); the Siegel–Weil formula for unitary groups
([thm-siegel-weil-weil-range]); the local zeta integrals of the doubling method and their
unramified computation ([thm-doubling-method],
[thm-unramified-doubling-gl]); the continuation of partial Hecke $L$-functions
([thm-hecke-l-nonvanishing-line-one]); and [thm-hodge-tate-count], which *we read as* a
continuity condition on the Galois representations attached along the way.
