We collect under one name the results of Galois cohomology that the bound
on Tate–Shafarevich groups uses: Poitou–Tate duality
([thm-poitou-tate]); finiteness of local cohomology
([thm-tate-local-finiteness], [thm-tate-local-finiteness-padic]); local duality
([thm-tate-local-duality-global-places], [thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic]); the local Euler
characteristic formula ([thm-tate-euler-characteristic-global-places], [thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic]);
finiteness of global cohomology ([thm-tate-global-finiteness]); the global Euler
characteristic formula ([thm-tate-global-euler-characteristic]); the orthogonality of
unramified classes ([thm-unramified-annihilators]); the Greenberg–Wiles formula
([thm-greenberg-wiles]); and the local theory of Bloch and Kato
([thm-bloch-kato-local]).
