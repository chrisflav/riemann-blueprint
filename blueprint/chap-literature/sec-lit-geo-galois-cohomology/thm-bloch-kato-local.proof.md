The fundamental exact sequence
$0 \to \Q_l \to B_{\mathrm{cris}}^{\varphi = 1} \to B_{\mathrm{dR}}/\mathrm{Fil}^0 \to 0$
([thm-fontaine-bcris]) gives the dimension of $H^1_f$. The Euler characteristic
formula ([thm-tate-euler-characteristic-l-adic]) and local duality ([thm-tate-local-duality-l-adic]),
passed to the limit over $l^r$, give the duality statements.
