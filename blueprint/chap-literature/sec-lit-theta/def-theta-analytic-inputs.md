The theta lifts between definite unitary groups use these published results
of harmonic analysis together: Weil representations for unitary pairs
([thm-weil-representation-auxiliary-pairs]); the existence and
classification of discrete series ([thm-discrete-series-exist],
[thm-hc-discrete-series-classification]); Harish-Chandra's analyticity
theorem ([thm-hc-analyticity-density]); trace class of cusp forms
([thm-cusp-trace-class]); the Dixmier–Malliavin factorisation
([thm-dixmier-malliavin]); Flath's factorisation ([thm-flath-unramified]);
the Siegel–Weil formula for unitary groups ([thm-siegel-weil-weil-range]);
the local zeta integrals of the doubling method and their unramified
computation ([thm-doubling-method], [thm-unramified-doubling-gl]); and the
continuation of partial Hecke $L$-functions
([thm-hecke-l-nonvanishing-line-one]). They also use
[thm-hodge-tate-count], which *we read as* a continuity condition on the
Galois representations attached along the way.
