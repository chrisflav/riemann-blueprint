Let $X$ be a non-degenerate hermitian space of dimension $n$ and $W$ a
non-degenerate skew-hermitian space of dimension $d$ over $E$, of given
signatures, with a model of the Weil representation of the symplectic
space $X \otimes W$ for a standard additive character $\psi$ of $\A_F$. Let
$\chi_X, \chi_W$ be Hecke characters of $E$ whose restrictions to
$\A_F^\times$ are $\eta^n$ and $\eta^d$, where $\eta$ is the quadratic
character of $E/F$. Then there is a Weil representation $\omega$ of
$\U(X)(\A_F) \times \U(W)(\A_F)$ on that model, attached to $\psi$ and the
splitting characters $\chi_X, \chi_W$.

This is the splitting of the metaplectic cover over unitary dual pairs.
