In this range Weil's criterion [thm-weil-convergence-criterion] makes the
theta integrals on the doubled group converge, and the rapid decay of cusp
forms [thm-cusp-rapid-decay] makes the theta lifts $\theta(\Phi_i, f_i)$
converge and lie in $L^2$. *We read* the rest as the usual argument: using
the Weil representation of the doubled pair
[thm-weil-representation-unitary-exists], unfold the inner product of the
two theta lifts into a doubling integral, and identify the theta integral
on the doubled group with a Siegel Eisenstein series by the Siegel–Weil
formula in the convergent range [thm-siegel-weil-convergent-doubling].
