Let $X$ be a hermitian and $W$ a skew-hermitian space over $E$, with a
Weil representation $\omega$ as in [thm-weil-representation-unitary-exists], and let
$\mathcal P$ be the space of $K$-finite vectors of its archimedean
component in the Fock model. Consider the $(\mathfrak u(W), K_W)$-stable
subspaces $N \subset \mathcal P$ that contain $(\mathfrak u(X), K_X)$
applied to $\mathcal P$, that is, such that $\U(X)_\infty$ acts trivially on
$\mathcal P/N$. Then:

* every chain of such subspaces has bounded length;
* two proper such subspaces have a proper sum.

So the maximal quotient of $\mathcal P$ on which $\U(X)_\infty$ acts
trivially, the big theta lift of the trivial representation, has finite
length and a unique irreducible quotient. This is the case of the trivial
representation of the archimedean duality theorem.
