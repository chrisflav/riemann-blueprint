The Weil representation and the analysis of theta lifts between unitary
groups. Locally: the Stone–von Neumann theorem, Kudla's Weil representation
of a split unitary group and the Weil representation of the real
metaplectic group; globally: Weil representations of unitary dual pairs,
the rational invariance of theta functions and Weil's convergence
criterion. Then come Howe duality for $(\GL_n, \GL_{n'})$ and, at the real
places, for the theta lift of the trivial representation; the meromorphic
continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series; the doubling method with its
unramified computation; and the Rallis inner product formula. The
Siegel–Weil formula itself is in [chap-theta].
