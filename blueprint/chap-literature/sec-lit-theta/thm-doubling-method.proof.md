The first point is the meromorphic continuation of Siegel Eisenstein
series [thm-siegel-eisenstein-continuation]. For the basic identity, cusp
forms decay rapidly [thm-cusp-rapid-decay] while the Eisenstein series has
moderate growth for $\Re s > \dim V/2$, so the doubling integral converges
absolutely and unfolds along the double cosets of the Siegel parabolic;
only the open orbit contributes, since $f_1$ and $f_2$ are cuspidal. The
same estimates, applied to the continued Eisenstein series, give the
meromorphic continuation of $Z(s, f_1, f_2)$.
