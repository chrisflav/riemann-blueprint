Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field of characteristic zero, $\psi$ a
non-trivial additive character and $m \ge 1$. If two irreducible cuspidal
representations $\pi_1, \pi_2$ of $\GL_{m+1}(F)$ have the same
Rankin–Selberg $\varepsilon$-factors $\varepsilon(s, \pi_i \times \tau, \psi)$
for every irreducible representation $\tau$ of $\GL_m(F)$, then
$\pi_1 \cong \pi_2$. This is Henniart's converse theorem (1993), which
twists by $\GL_{n-1}$.
