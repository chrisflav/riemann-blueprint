Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $I \subset \GL_n(F)$ the Iwahori
subgroup, $B = TU$ the upper triangular Borel subgroup and $T(\mathcal O)$
the integral points of the diagonal torus. For every admissible
representation $V$ of $\GL_n(F)$ the projection to the Jacquet module
$$
V^{I} \longrightarrow (V_U)^{T(\mathcal O)}
$$
is a bijection (Borel, 1976; Casselman, 1980).
