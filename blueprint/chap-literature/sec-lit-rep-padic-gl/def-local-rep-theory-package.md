This is not a single theorem: it is the conjunction of the local results
about $\GL_n$ of a $p$-adic field that the proof uses, namely
[thm-bz-finiteness], [thm-bernstein-unitary-product],
[thm-geometric-lemma], the Zelevinsky polynomial ring and the classification of
[thm-zelevinsky-segments], the irreducibility criterion for
disjoint cuspidal supports, [thm-moy-prasad-jacquet],
[thm-tempered-products], [thm-square-integrable-segment],
the existence of local parameters ([def-local-parameters]),
[thm-henniart-local-converse], [thm-depth-zero-from-support], [thm-brauer-induction-degree-zero],
[thm-chevalley-congruence-units] and [thm-green-regular-characters].
