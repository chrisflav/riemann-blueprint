Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field and $n = d_1 + \dots + d_r$ with
every $d_a > 0$. For the standard parabolic subgroup of type
$(d_1, \dots, d_r)$ of $\GL_n(F)$, the Jacquet functor takes smooth
representations of finite length to representations of finite length, and so
does parabolic induction from the Levi subgroup
$\GL_{d_1}(F) \times \dots \times \GL_{d_r}(F)$.
