Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field of characteristic zero. Every
irreducible smooth representation $\pi$ of $\GL_N(F)$ that is unitarisable
and generic is a product
$$
\pi \cong \delta_1 \times \dots \times \delta_a \times
\prod_{j} \big(\delta'_j\nu^{s_j} \times \delta'_j\nu^{-s_j}\big),
$$
where the $\delta_i, \delta'_j$ are unitarisable segment representations and
$0 < s_j < 1/2$. This is the generic case of the classification of the
unitary dual.
