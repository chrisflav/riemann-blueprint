The three listed points are the results of Bernstein and Zelevinsky. Of
the statements recorded with them, the Zelevinsky polynomial ring is part 5
of [thm-zelevinsky-segments], the geometric lemma is
[thm-geometric-lemma-filtration] with its consequence [thm-geometric-lemma],
and the irreducibility criterion follows by computing Jacquet modules of
$\pi_1 \times \pi_2$ with the geometric lemma.
