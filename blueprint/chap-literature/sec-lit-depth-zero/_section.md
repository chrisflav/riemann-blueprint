title = "Depth zero and finite groups of Lie type"
order = 11

Representations of $\GL_n(F)$, $F$ a non-archimedean local field, with a
non-zero vector fixed by the first principal congruence subgroup are
controlled by representations of $\GL_n$ of the residue field. The
theorems of Moy and Prasad relate such vectors to Jacquet modules and show
that depth zero passes to the cuspidal support and back. Over a finite
field we need Gelfand's description of cuspidal representations on the
mirabolic subgroup and Green's classification of cuspidal characters, and
in characteristic $p$ Jantzen's theorem that induced modules in the lowest
alcove are simple.
