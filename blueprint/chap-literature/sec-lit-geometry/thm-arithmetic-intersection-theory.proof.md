The proof uses functorial resolution of singularities in characteristic
zero ([thm-functorial-resolution],
[thm-principalisation-reduces-dimension]) and pullback along flag covers
([lem-flag-cover-pullback]). *We read* these as the steps that pass
to a smooth model and reduce the archimedean integrals to affine charts,
where the Poincaré–Lelong formula applies.
