Let $X$ be a reduced separated scheme of finite type over a field $k$ of
characteristic $0$. There is a sequence of blow-ups $X' \to X$ with $X'$
smooth over $k$, which is an isomorphism over the smooth locus of $X$ and
under which the preimage of the singular locus is a divisor with simple
normal crossings.
