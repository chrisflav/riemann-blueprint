Algebraic and complex geometry in characteristic zero, then Arakelov
geometry. The first part gives Chow forms, Serre's GAGA for sections,
Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing, asymptotic Riemann–Roch for nef line bundles,
the Ohsawa–Takegoshi $L^2$ extension theorem, resolution of singularities
and principalisation of ideals. The second gives the arithmetic
intersection theory of hermitian line bundles, the Poincaré–Lelong formula
in integrated form, the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorem, arithmetic
volumes and Mumford's good metrics. [chap-heights] uses them to bound
arithmetic volumes and heights on unitary Shimura varieties.
