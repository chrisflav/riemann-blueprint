Classical results on zeta and $L$-functions of number fields and on primes.
First come Hecke's functional equations, for partial zeta functions of
totally real fields and for Hecke $L$-functions of CM fields, and the
continuation and non-vanishing of Hecke $L$-functions on $\Re s \ge 1$.
Then the values of partial zeta functions of totally real fields at
negative integers: rational by Siegel and Klingen, with the congruences of
Deligne and Ribet. After Brauer's induction theorem in a degree-zero form,
the section ends with Dirichlet $L$-functions and primes in progressions:
the logarithmic derivative at $s = 1$, Linnik's theorem and the prime
number theorem with an exceptional zero.
