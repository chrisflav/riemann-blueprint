Write the Dedekind zeta function of $K$ as a sum over the ideal classes of
real analytic Eisenstein series at the points $\tau_c$, and factor it as
$\zeta(s)L(s,\chi)$. The class number formula gives
$L(1,\chi) = \pi h/\sqrt d$. Compare the constant terms of the Laurent
expansions at $s = 1$: Kronecker's limit formula expresses the constant
term of each Eisenstein series through $\log(\Im(\tau_c)|\eta(\tau_c)|^4)$, and
the constant term of $\zeta(s)L(s,\chi)$ involves $\gamma$ and
$L'(1,\chi)/L(1,\chi)$. The forms with the covolume and the discriminant
$g_2^3 - 27g_3^2 = (2\pi)^{12}\eta^{24}$ follow by homogeneity.
