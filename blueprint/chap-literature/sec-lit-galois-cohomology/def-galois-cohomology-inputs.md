The bound on Tate–Shafarevich groups uses these results of Galois
cohomology together: Poitou–Tate duality ([thm-poitou-tate]); finiteness of
local cohomology ([thm-tate-local-finiteness]); local duality
([thm-tate-local-duality-global-places]); the local Euler characteristic
formula ([thm-tate-euler-characteristic-global-places]); finiteness of global cohomology
([thm-tate-global-finiteness]); the global Euler characteristic formula
([thm-tate-global-euler-characteristic]); the orthogonality of unramified
classes ([thm-unramified-annihilators]); the Greenberg–Wiles formula
([thm-greenberg-wiles]); and the local theory of Bloch and Kato
([thm-bloch-kato-local]).
