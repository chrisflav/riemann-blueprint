The first formula is Tate's. At a place $v$ of $F$, the decomposition group
is the absolute Galois group of the completion $F_v$, a finite extension of
$\Q_p$, with $[F_v : \Q_p]$ the local degree. If $p = l$, apply the first
formula. If $p \ne l$, use the formula $\# H^1 = \# H^0 \cdot \# H^2$ for
$l$-power modules over finite extensions of $\Q_p$, which we prove
separately.
