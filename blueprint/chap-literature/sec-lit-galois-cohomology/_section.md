Tate's theorems on the cohomology of finite Galois modules of $l$-power
order over local and global fields: finiteness, local duality, the local
and global Euler characteristic formulas, Poitou–Tate duality and the
Greenberg–Wiles formula for Selmer groups, with the local conditions of
Bloch and Kato for de Rham representations. Throughout, $H^i$ is continuous
cochain cohomology, $\mu_{l^r}$ the module of $l^r$-th roots of unity and
$M^\vee(1) = \mathrm{Hom}(M, \mu_{l^r})$. With them [chap-sha] bounds Selmer
groups, and so Tate–Shafarevich groups.
