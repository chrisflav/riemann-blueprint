Let $F$ be a number field, $S$ a finite set of primes containing those above
$l$, and $M$ a finite $\Z/l^r$-module, $r \ge 1$, with a continuous action of
$G_{F,S}$. Then
$$\frac{\#H^0(G_{F,S}, M)\cdot\#H^2(G_{F,S}, M)}{\#H^1(G_{F,S}, M)} = \frac{\prod_{v \mid \infty}\#H^0(G_v, M)}{\#M^{[F:\Q]}},$$
where $G_v$ is generated by a complex conjugation at real $v$.
