Let $K$ be a finite extension of $\Q_l$ inside $\Qlbar$, $r > 0$, and $M$ a
finite $\Z/l^r$-module with a continuous action of $G_K$. Then
$H^0(K, M)$, $H^1(K, M)$ and $H^2(K, M)$ are finite and $H^n(K, M) = 0$ for
$n \ge 3$. This is [thm-tate-local-finiteness] for places above $l$,
stated for the absolute Galois group of $K$.
