The classical theory of elliptic curves with complex multiplication by the
ring of integers of an imaginary quadratic field $K$. The $j$-invariants of
the ideals of $K$ generate its Hilbert class field and form one Galois orbit
of $h$ elements; Weber's function at torsion points generates its ray class
fields; Shimura's main theorem describes the action of $\mathrm{Aut}(\C/K)$
on torsion points. The section ends with Gross's curves with good reduction
outside $p$ and the Chowla–Selberg formula for the mean of the logarithms
of the periods over the ideal classes.
