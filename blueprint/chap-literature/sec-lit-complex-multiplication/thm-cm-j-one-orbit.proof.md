Take the Hilbert class field $H = K(j(\mathfrak a))$ and the Artin map of
[thm-cm-j-hilbert-class-field]: the class group acts simply transitively on
the $j(\mathfrak b)$ through $\Gal(H/K)$, and every automorphism of $\C$ over
$K$ restricts to $H$.
