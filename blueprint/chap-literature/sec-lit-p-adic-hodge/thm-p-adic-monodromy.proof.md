The matrix form is proved by induction. A de Rham representation is one with a
framing by weights; a twist by a Lubin–Tate character reduces to total weight
zero, where Sen's theorem gives potential semistability. Reducible
representations that split after an unramified extension are treated
directly, and an irreducible elementary one by modifying its weights. The proof also
uses the local Euler characteristic formula, local Tate duality and the
properties of $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$.
