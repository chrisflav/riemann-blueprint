The bounds on Tate–Shafarevich groups at a prime $l$
([thm-family-sha-error-bound] and the congruence bounds that prove it) use
seven published results together: Fontaine's period ring
$B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ [thm-fontaine-bcris], global Artin reciprocity
[thm-artin-reciprocity], the Bloch–Kato local conditions
[thm-bloch-kato-local], and, for finite extensions of $\Q_l$, local Tate
duality [thm-tate-local-duality-global-places], the local Euler characteristic
formula [thm-tate-euler-characteristic-global-places], finiteness of local
cohomology [thm-tate-local-finiteness] and the integral comparison
theorem of Bhatt and Scholze [thm-bhatt-scholze-crystalline-lattices].
