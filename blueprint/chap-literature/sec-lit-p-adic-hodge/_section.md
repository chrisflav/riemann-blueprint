Fontaine's crystalline period ring [thm-fontaine-bcris] and the facts on
de Rham, crystalline and potentially semistable representations that the
proof uses: the count of Hodge–Tate weights, Berger's theorem that de Rham
representations are potentially semistable, the description of lattices
in crystalline representations by prismatic $F$-crystals, due to Bhatt and
Scholze, and Fargues's Hodge–Tate sequence of a $p$-divisible group. Most
of them serve the bounds on Tate–Shafarevich groups at a prime in
[chap-sha].
