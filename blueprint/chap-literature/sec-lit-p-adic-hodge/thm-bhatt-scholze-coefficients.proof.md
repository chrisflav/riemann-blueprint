Forget the $\mathcal O_L$-structure: since $L$ is finite over $\Q_l$, the
representation is a crystalline representation over $\Q_l$ and $T$ is a
$G_K$-stable $\Z_l$-lattice in it. The theorem of Bhatt and Scholze
[thm-bhatt-scholze-crystalline-lattices] then gives the prismatic
$F$-crystal and the full faithfulness.
