In the situation of [thm-flath-tensor-product], the factorisation
$\pi \cong \bigotimes'_v \pi_v$ can be chosen so that, in addition, every
finite factor is irreducible and admissible
([thm-bernstein-unitary-admissible]) and, for every $\mathcal O_E$-lattice
$L$ in $V$, for almost all $v$ the vectors of $\pi_v$ fixed by the
stabiliser of $L_v$ form the line spanned by $\xi_v$. This is Flath's
theorem in its usual form (1979).
