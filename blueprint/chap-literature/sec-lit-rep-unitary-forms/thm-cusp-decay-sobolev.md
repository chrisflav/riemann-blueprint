Let $G = \U(V)$ for a hermitian space over a CM field, $U$ an open subgroup
of $G(\A_F)$ and $N, M \ge 0$. There are $d$ and $C$ such that every smooth
cuspidal automorphic function $\varphi$, right invariant under $U$, with
finite growth Sobolev norms satisfies
$$
\operatorname{decay}_M(\varphi) \le C\,\|\varphi\|_{d,N},
$$
where $\operatorname{decay}_M$ is the supremum of $|\varphi|$ times the
$M$-th power of the height on the quotient and $\|\cdot\|_{d,N}$ is the
Sobolev norm of order $d$ weighted by the $N$-th power of the height.
