Let $G = \U(V)$ for a hermitian space over a CM field. For every smooth
compactly supported function $f$ on $G(\A_F)$, the operator $R(f)$ on the
cuspidal subspace $L^2_{\mathrm{cusp}}(G(F)\backslash G(\A_F))$ is of trace
class.
