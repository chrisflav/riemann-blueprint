Flath's tensor product theorem [thm-flath-tensor-product] gives the
factorisation, with $\xi_v$ fixed by the stabiliser $K_v$ of $L_v$ for almost
all $v$. Each finite factor is irreducible, smooth and admissible by
Bernstein's theorem [thm-bernstein-unitary-admissible]. For almost all $v$
there is an anti-involution of $\U(V)(F_v)$ that fixes every double coset
of $K_v$ (Gelfand's trick), so the Hecke algebra of $K_v$ is commutative
and the $K_v$-fixed vectors of the irreducible $\pi_v$ form a line, which
is $\C\xi_v$.
