It suffices to treat a prime power $m = l^r$ and then to combine coprime
exponents. For a prime power, the units that are not $l^r$-th powers are
detected by finitely many primes, which [thm-chebotarev-degree-one] supplies
outside any given finite set, so that their residue characteristics can be
taken prime to $b$.
