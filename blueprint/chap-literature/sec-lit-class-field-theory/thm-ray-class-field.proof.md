We take for $L$ the abelian extension cut out by the Artin map
([thm-artin-reciprocity]) on the ideles that are units congruent to $1$
modulo $\mathfrak m$ and positive at the real places. The local properties
of the Artin map identify $\Gal(L/K)$ with the narrow ray class group and
the class of a prime with its Frobenius.
