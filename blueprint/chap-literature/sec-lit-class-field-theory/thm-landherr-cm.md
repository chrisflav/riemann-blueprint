Let $E$ be a CM field with complex conjugation $z \mapsto \bar z$, and let
$G, G'$ be non-degenerate hermitian $r \times r$ matrices over $E$. Suppose
that for every embedding $\tau : E \to \C$ the forms $\tau(G)$ and
$\tau(G')$ have the same signature, and that
$\det G' = z \bar z \det G$ for some $z \in E$. Then $G$ and $G'$ are
isometric over $E$.

The same proof gives the companion statement for a CM extension unramified
at every finite place: forms isometric at every finite place and at every
complex embedding are isometric. In both forms, a hermitian form over a CM
field is determined by its signatures and its
determinant modulo norms.
