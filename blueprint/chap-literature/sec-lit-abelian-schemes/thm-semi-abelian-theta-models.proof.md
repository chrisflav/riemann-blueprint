Grothendieck's [semistable reduction theorem](@thm-semistable-reduction) gives
semi-abelian models locally after a finite extension; the theta functions and
the [structure of multiplication by $n$](@thm-abelian-variety-torsion-count)
glue them and descend them. Over complete discrete valuation rings the
models have [Mumford uniformisations](@thm-degenerations-mumford-uniformisation),
and the [standard facts on polarised abelian schemes](@thm-abelian-scheme-standard-theory)
are used throughout. We have not unfolded the details.
