Spread out to a noetherian base and compute with Čech complexes. The steps,
each using the earlier ones, are: a dictionary between Čech and
hypercohomology, and the fact that the Čech complex computes de Rham
cohomology; homotopy invariance, the quotient computation, Fubini for Čech
complexes and acyclicity on affines; descent along the cover and base change
for the Čech system; Stein factorisation and the cotangent complex; the
comparison with coherent cohomology and its finiteness; vanishing above degree
$2g$; perfectness over noetherian bases; base change of $H^1$ and the
computation of $H^2$ from fibres; the wedge structure; the Hopf algebra
structure over fields; and the cohomology of $1$-forms and $2$-forms.
