Let $A$ be an abelian scheme of relative dimension $g$ over $R$. Then cup
product is an isomorphism $\wedge^2 H^1_{dR}(A/R) \cong H^2_{dR}(A/R)$,
$H^1_{dR}(A/R)$ is a vector bundle of rank $2g$, the closed $1$-forms give an
injection of $\omega_A$ onto a subbundle of rank $g$, the sequence
$$0 \to \omega_A \to H^1_{dR}(A/R) \to H^1(A, \mathcal O_A) \to 0$$
is exact, and all of this commutes with base change $R \to R'$.
