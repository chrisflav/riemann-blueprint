Abelian varieties and abelian schemes. A *principally polarised abelian
scheme* here is an abelian scheme with a rigidified, symmetric, relatively
ample line bundle $L$ whose group $K(L)$ is trivial. The section gives the
standard facts on such schemes, duality, torsion and Zarhin's trick; de
Rham cohomology with its Hodge filtration, Grothendieck–Messing deformation
theory and Grothendieck's existence theorem; semistable reduction,
semi-abelian schemes and Mumford's uniformisation of degenerating abelian
varieties; and the semi-positivity of Hodge bundles. The integral models
and compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties in [chap-galois] and
[chap-heights] rest on them.
