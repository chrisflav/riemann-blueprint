Form the dual abelian scheme $A^\vee$ ([thm-dual-abelian-scheme]) and
apply Zarhin's construction on $(A \times A^\vee)^4$, which carries a
principal polarisation built from the ample bundle and a quaternion of
integers of norm prime to its degree.
