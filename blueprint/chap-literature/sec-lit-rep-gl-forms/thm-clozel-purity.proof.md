The archimedean components of $\pi$ are unitary up to a twist, because cusp
forms are bounded ([thm-cusp-bounded-gl]). Unitarity relates the
infinitesimal characters at $\tau$ and $\bar\tau$, and algebraicity makes
the shift an integer.
