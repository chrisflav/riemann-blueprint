The non-endoscopic case of the argument
([prop-member-hypotheses-from-literature]) uses five groups of results
together: the local representation theory of $\GL_n$
([def-local-rep-theory-package]); the global results on automorphic forms
and Galois representations of [def-automorphic-galois-inputs] (rapid decay,
finiteness, Jacquet–Shalika, the cuspidal spectrum, base change,
Gelfand–Kazhdan, Galois representations of polarized pairs and Clozel's
purity); the cohomological results of [def-galois-cohomology-inputs]; the
statements on lattices and zeta denominators of
[thm-non-endoscopic-hypotheses-hold]; and the functional equation of Hecke
$L$-functions [thm-hecke-functional-equation-cm].
