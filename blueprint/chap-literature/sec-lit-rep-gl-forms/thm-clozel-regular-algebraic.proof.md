A regular algebraic $\pi$ is in particular algebraic, so Clozel's purity
lemma [thm-clozel-purity] gives the purity of its infinitesimal character,
and Clozel's Lemma 3.14 [thm-clozel-cohomological] shows that it is
cohomological.
