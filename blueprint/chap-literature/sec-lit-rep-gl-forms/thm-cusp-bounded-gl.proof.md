This is the third part of the description of the cuspidal spectrum
[thm-cuspidal-spectrum-gl]. A cusp form decays rapidly on a Siegel domain,
and when $|\varphi|$ is invariant under the split part of the centre the
quotient by that part has finite volume, so $\varphi$ is bounded.
