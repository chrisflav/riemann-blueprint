Let $E$ be a CM field, $a \ge 1$, and $\pi$ a regular algebraic cuspidal
automorphic representation of $\GL_a(\A_E)$. Then the infinitesimal
character of $\pi$ is pure: there is $w_0 \in \Z$ with
$\mathrm{inf}_{\bar\tau}(\pi) = \{ w_0 - z : z \in \mathrm{inf}_\tau(\pi)\}$
for every complex embedding $\tau$. Moreover $\pi$ is cohomological.
