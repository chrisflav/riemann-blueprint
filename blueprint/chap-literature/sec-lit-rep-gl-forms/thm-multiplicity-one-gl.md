Let $E$ be a totally complex number field. Two cuspidal automorphic
representations of $\GL_a(\A_E)$ that are isomorphic occupy the same
subspace of the space of cusp forms. This is the multiplicity one theorem of
Shalika (1974) and Piatetski-Shapiro.
