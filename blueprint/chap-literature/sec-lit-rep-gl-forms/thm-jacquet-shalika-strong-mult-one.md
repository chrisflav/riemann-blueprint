Let $E$ be a totally complex number field and let
$\pi_1, \dots, \pi_r$ and $\pi'_1, \dots, \pi'_s$ be cuspidal automorphic
representations of groups $\GL_{m_i}(\A_E)$, $\GL_{m'_j}(\A_E)$ with all
$m_i, m'_j \ge 1$. If for almost all finite places $w$ the multisets of
Satake parameters agree,
$$
\textstyle\sum_i \mathrm{Sat}_w(\pi_i) = \sum_j \mathrm{Sat}_w(\pi'_j),
$$
then $r = s$ and the two lists agree up to order. This is the theorem of
Jacquet and Shalika (1981).
