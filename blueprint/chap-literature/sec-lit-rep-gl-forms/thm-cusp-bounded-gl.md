Let $E$ be a totally complex number field and $a \ge 0$. A smooth cusp form
$\varphi$ on $\GL_a(\A_E)$ with $|\varphi(zg)| = |\varphi(g)|$ for all $z$ in
the split part of the centre is bounded. This is the classical consequence
of rapid decay (Gelfand–Piatetski-Shapiro; Moeglin–Waldspurger, I.2.18): a
cusp form with unitary central character is bounded.
