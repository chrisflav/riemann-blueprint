The cuspidal irreducible characters of $\GL_d(\mathbb F_g)$ correspond to
the Frobenius orbits of regular characters $\theta$ of
$\mathbb F_{g^d}^\times$. The character attached to $\theta$ has degree
$\prod_{i=1}^{d-1}(g^i - 1)$ and takes the value
$(-1)^{d-1}\sum_i\theta(z^{g^i})$ at an elliptic element with eigenvalue $z$.
