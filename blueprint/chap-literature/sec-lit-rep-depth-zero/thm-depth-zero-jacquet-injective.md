Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $P = MN$ a standard parabolic
subgroup of $\GL_n(F)$, $K_1$ the first principal congruence subgroup and
$K = \GL_n(\mathcal O)$. For an irreducible representation $\pi$ of
$\GL_n(F)$, a vector fixed by $K_1(N \cap K)$ whose image in the Jacquet
module $\pi_N$ is zero is itself zero. This is the injectivity half of
[thm-moy-prasad-jacquet].
