Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field and $K_1 = 1 + \varpi M_n(\mathcal O)$
the first principal congruence subgroup of $\GL_n(F)$. If an irreducible
representation $\pi$ of $\GL_n(F)$ has a non-zero $K_1$-fixed vector (depth
zero), then every member $\sigma_a$ of its cuspidal support has a non-zero
vector fixed by the first congruence subgroup of $\GL_{d_a}(F)$. This is one
half of the depth-zero theory.
