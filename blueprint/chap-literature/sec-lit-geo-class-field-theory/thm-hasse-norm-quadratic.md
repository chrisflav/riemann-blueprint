Let $F$ be a number field and $\delta, x \in F^\times$. If $x$ has the form
$a^2 - \delta b^2$ in every real completion and in every non-archimedean
completion of $F$, then $x = a^2 - \delta b^2$ with $a, b \in F$.

In other words, an element of $F^\times$ that is a local norm from
$F(\sqrt\delta)$ everywhere is a global norm. This is the norm theorem for
quadratic extensions; the complex places impose no condition.
