Let $E$ be a number field and $m, b \ge 1$. There is an integer $a \ge 1$
prime to $b$ such that every unit $u \in \mathcal O_E^\times$ with
$u \equiv 1 \bmod a$ is an $m$-th power of a unit.

This is Chevalley's theorem (1951) that the congruence subgroups of the unit
group define its profinite topology, in the form with a modulus prime to a
given integer.
