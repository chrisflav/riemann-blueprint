Let $E/F$ be a quadratic extension of number fields with non-trivial
automorphism $\sigma$, and let $G, G'$ be non-degenerate hermitian
$r \times r$ matrices over $E$. If $G$ and $G'$ are equivalent over
$E \otimes_F F_v$ for every place $v$ of $F$, finite and infinite, then
$G' = P^{\mathsf T} G\, \sigma(P)$ for some $P \in \GL_r(E)$.
