Let $G = \U(p,q)$ and $K = \U(p)\times\U(q)$.

* For an admissible representation of $G$ on a Hilbert space with $K$ acting
  unitarily, the matrix coefficient $g \mapsto \langle w, \pi(g)v\rangle$ of
  two $K$-finite vectors is real-analytic on $G$.
* Let $M$ be the Levi subgroup of the parabolic subgroup fixing an
  isotropic flag. In an irreducible unitary representation of $M$, every
  non-zero subspace of $K\cap M$-finite vectors that is stable under the
  Lie algebra of $M$ is dense.
