Let $G = \U(p,q)$, $K = \U(p)\times\U(q)$, and $\sigma$ a unitary
representation of $G$ on $H$.

* If $V \subseteq H$ is an admissible $(\mathfrak g, K)$-submodule, its
  closure is $G$-stable, and the $K$-finite vectors of the closure are
  exactly $V$.
* If $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$ are irreducible unitary and their spaces of
  $K$-finite vectors are isomorphic as $(\mathfrak g, K)$-modules, then some
  positive multiple of that isomorphism extends to a unitary equivalence of
  $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$.

In particular, infinitesimally equivalent irreducible unitary
representations are unitarily equivalent.
