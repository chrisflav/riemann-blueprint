Harish-Chandra's theory of $(\mathfrak g, K)$-modules and the discrete
series, mostly for $\U(p,q)$ with $K = \U(p)\times\U(q)$. Unless an entry
says otherwise, a *Harish-Chandra parameter* is a strictly decreasing
$\lambda \in \R^{p+q}$ with every $\lambda_a - (p+q-1)/2$ an integer, and
$\rho_c$, $\rho_n$ are the half sums of the compact and non-compact positive
roots. After Harish-Chandra modules, globalisation, Dixmier–Malliavin
factorisation and Schur orthogonality come the existence, exhaustion and
classification of the discrete series, their lowest $K$-types and formal
degrees, Zuckerman's tensor products and Weyl's branching law. The theta
lifts of [chap-theta] use them at the archimedean places.
