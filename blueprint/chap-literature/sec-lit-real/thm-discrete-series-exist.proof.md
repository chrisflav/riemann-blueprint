Harish-Chandra's existence theorem [thm-discrete-series-exist-upq] gives,
for each parameter $\lambda$, an irreducible unitary representation with
square-integrable matrix coefficients and lowest $K$-type
$\lambda + \rho_n - \rho_c$; this is what it means to be a discrete series
representation with parameter $\lambda$.
