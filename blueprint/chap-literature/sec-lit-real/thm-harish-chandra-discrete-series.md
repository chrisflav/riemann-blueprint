Let $\U(\varepsilon)$ be the unitary group of the diagonal form with signs
$\varepsilon \in \{\pm 1\}^n$. Every irreducible square-integrable unitary
representation $\sigma$ of $\U(\varepsilon)$ is, after a permutation of
coordinates that preserves the order within each sign, the discrete series
representation with some Harish-Chandra parameter $\mu$: it contains the
lowest $K$-type of $\mu$, and every $K$-type has norm at least as large.

This is Harish-Chandra's classification of the discrete series for the
groups $\U(\varepsilon)$, in the form of Schmid's lowest $K$-type theorem.
