Let $p + q \ge 1$ and let $\tilde G$ be the double cover
$\{(g, w) : g \in \U(p,q),\ w^2 = \det g\}$ of $\U(p,q)$, with maximal compact
subgroup $\tilde K$ over $\U(p)\times\U(q)$. For irreducible unitary
representations of $\tilde G$ on Hilbert spaces:

* every $K$-finite vector is smooth, the representation has a Harish-Chandra
  module (its $(\mathfrak g, K)$-module of $K$-finite vectors), and every such
  module is admissible and irreducible;
* two irreducible unitary representations with isomorphic Harish-Chandra
  modules are unitarily equivalent;
* a unitary representation whose Harish-Chandra module is admissible and
  irreducible is irreducible;
* for irreducible unitary representations of $\U(p,q)$ itself, $K$-finite
  vectors are analytic: $g \mapsto \sigma(g)v$ is real-analytic for every
  $K$-finite $v$.
