Let $G = \U(p,q)$ and $K = \U(p)\times\U(q)$, and let $\sigma$ be an
irreducible unitary representation of $G$ on a non-zero Hilbert space $H$.
Then $\sigma$ is admissible, every $K$-finite vector is analytic, the
$K$-finite vectors form a dense subspace stable under the Lie algebra, and
this subspace is irreducible as a $(\mathfrak g, K)$-module: its only
Lie-stable subspaces are $0$ and itself.
