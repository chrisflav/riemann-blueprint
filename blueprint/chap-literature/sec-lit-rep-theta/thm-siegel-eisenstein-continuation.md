Let $V$ be a hermitian space of positive rank over a CM field, $V \oplus (-V)$
its doubled space and $P$ the Siegel parabolic subgroup of
$\U(V\oplus(-V))$. For a unitary Hecke character $\mu$ and a section $\varphi$
of the degenerate principal series induced from $\mu|\det|^{s_1}$, the
Eisenstein series $E(g, s)$ of the flat section through $\varphi$ continues
meromorphically in $s$, and at each $s_0$ some power $(s - s_0)^k E(g, s)$ is
regular. This is Langlands' continuation of Eisenstein series in the case
of Siegel parabolics, made explicit by Piatetski-Shapiro–Rallis and Tan.
