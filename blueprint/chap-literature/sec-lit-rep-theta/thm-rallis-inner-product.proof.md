The proof uses the rapid decay of cusp forms [thm-cusp-rapid-decay] and the
Weil representation of the doubled pair [thm-weil-representation-auxiliary-pairs],
and goes through the doubling method. *We read* it as the usual argument:
unfold the inner product of the two theta lifts into a doubling integral
and identify the theta integral on the doubled group with an Eisenstein
series by the Siegel–Weil formula.
