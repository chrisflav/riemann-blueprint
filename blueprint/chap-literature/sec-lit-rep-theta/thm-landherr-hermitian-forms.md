* Let $K$ be a non-archimedean local field with $2 \ne 0$ and $L/K$ a
  quadratic extension. Two non-degenerate hermitian forms $G, G'$ of the same
  rank over $L$ are equivalent if and only if $\det G/\det G'$ is a norm
  from $L$.
* Let $E$ be a CM field. Two non-degenerate hermitian forms over $E$ that
  are equivalent at every finite place of the maximal totally real subfield
  and at every complex embedding are equivalent over $E$.
