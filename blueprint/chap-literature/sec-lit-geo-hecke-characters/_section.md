The $l$-adic Galois character attached to an algebraic Hecke character, and
its behaviour at the places above $l$: it is de Rham with the expected
Hodge–Tate weight, potentially crystalline, crystalline where the Hecke
character is unramified, and its Weil–Deligne representation is the one the
Hecke character predicts. The section ends with Clozel's theorem that
regular algebraic cuspidal representations are cohomological, and with the
list of automorphic inputs of the Galois representations.

Throughout, $E$ is a number field, $l$ a prime, $\iota : \Qlbar \cong \C$,
and $\mathrm{Art}_E$ the Artin map of [thm-artin-reciprocity]. A Hecke
character $\chi$ of $E$ is *algebraic of type $n$*, for integers $n_\phi$
indexed by the embeddings $\phi : E \to \C$, if
$\chi_\infty(k) = \prod_\phi \phi(k)^{n_\phi}$ for every totally positive
$k \in E^\times$.
