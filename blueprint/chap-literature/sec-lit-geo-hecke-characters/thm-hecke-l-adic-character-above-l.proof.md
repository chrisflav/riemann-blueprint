The de Rham property and the Hodge–Tate weights are
[thm-hecke-l-adic-character-de-rham], and crystallinity at unramified places
is [thm-hecke-l-adic-character-crystalline]. By Berger's theorem
[thm-p-adic-monodromy] the de Rham character is potentially semistable;
since the monodromy operator of a character vanishes, it is potentially
crystalline.
