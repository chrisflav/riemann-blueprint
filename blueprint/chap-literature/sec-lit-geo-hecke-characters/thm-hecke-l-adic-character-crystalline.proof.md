By [thm-hecke-character-wd-above-l] the Weil–Deligne representation of the
restriction is $\xi \circ \mathrm{Art}_{E_w}^{-1}$, which is unramified because
$\chi$ is unramified at $w$; [thm-artin-reciprocity] and
[thm-chevalley-congruence-units] enter through the local Artin map. A potentially
semistable character whose Weil–Deligne representation is unramified with
$N = 0$ is crystalline.
