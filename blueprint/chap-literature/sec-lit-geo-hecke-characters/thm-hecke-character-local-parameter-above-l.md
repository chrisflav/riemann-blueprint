Let $\chi$ be an algebraic Hecke character of $E$ of type $n$, $w$ a place
above $l$, $\xi$ the smooth character of $E_w^\times$ that agrees with
$\chi$ on $E^\times$, and $\rec$ any system of local parameters for the
$\GL_n(E_w)$ ([def-local-parameters]). Then the Weil–Deligne representation
of $r_{\chi,\iota}|_{G_{E_w}}$ is $\rec(\xi)$, the parameter of $\xi$ viewed
as a representation of $\GL_1(E_w)$.
