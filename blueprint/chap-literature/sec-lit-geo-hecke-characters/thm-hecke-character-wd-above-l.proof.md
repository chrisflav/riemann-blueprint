By Berger's theorem the restriction is potentially semistable, and the
monodromy of a character vanishes. The proof computes $D_{\mathrm{pst}}$
from the description of the character on inertia, using [thm-chevalley-congruence-units]
to pass to an open subgroup on which the global units contribute nothing
and the properties of $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ in [thm-fontaine-bcris].
