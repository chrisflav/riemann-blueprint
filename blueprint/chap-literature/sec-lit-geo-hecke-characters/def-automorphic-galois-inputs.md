We collect under one name the inputs of the construction of Galois
representations: rapid decay of cusp forms ([thm-cusp-rapid-decay]);
Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem ([thm-hc-finiteness-unitary]); finiteness of
spaces of automorphic forms on $\GL_n$ ([thm-hc-finiteness-gl]);
Jacquet–Shalika ([thm-jacquet-shalika]); the cuspidal spectrum of $\GL_n$
([thm-cuspidal-spectrum-gl]); base change from unitary groups
([thm-base-change-unitary]); Gelfand–Kazhdan ([thm-gelfand-kazhdan]);
Clozel's purity and cohomologicity theorems ([thm-clozel-purity],
[thm-clozel-cohomological]); Artin reciprocity and ray class fields
([thm-artin-reciprocity], [thm-ray-class-field]); the results on Hecke
characters above $l$ ([thm-hecke-l-adic-character-above-l],
[thm-hecke-character-wd-above-l]); and Berger's theorem
([thm-p-adic-monodromy]); together with the segment
classification of essentially square-integrable representations
([thm-zelevinsky-segments]).

The list also contains, as two of its items, the existence of Galois
representations for polarized pairs ([def-galois-reps-polarized]) in two
slightly different forms. So it is a list of hypotheses that includes the
conclusion of [thm-galois-reps-polarized], not a list of published results
alone.
