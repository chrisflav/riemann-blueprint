Let $E$ be a totally complex number field, $a \ge 1$, and $\pi$ a cuspidal
automorphic representation of $\GL_a(\A_E)$ that is regular algebraic: at
each embedding the infinitesimal character consists of $a$ distinct
numbers in $\Z + (a-1)/2$. Then $\pi$ is cohomological: there is an
irreducible algebraic representation $\Xi$ of $\mathrm{Res}_{E/\Q}\GL_a$
such that $\pi_\infty \otimes \Xi$ has non-zero relative Lie algebra
cohomology.
