Let $\chi$ be an algebraic Hecke character of $E$ of type $n$ with $l$-adic
character $r_{\chi,\iota}$ ([thm-hecke-l-adic-character]), and let $w$ be a place
of $E$ above $l$. Then $r_{\chi,\iota}|_{G_{E_w}}$ is de Rham, and at each
embedding $\tau : E \to \Qlbar$ inducing $w$ its single Hodge–Tate weight is
$-n_{\iota\tau}$.

This is classical; see Serre (1968) and Fontaine (1982).
