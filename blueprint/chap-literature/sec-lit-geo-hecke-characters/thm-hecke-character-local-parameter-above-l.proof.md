Rewrite with [thm-hecke-character-wd-above-l] and use that a system of local
parameters is the local Artin map on characters. *We read* the other
dependences ([thm-bz-finiteness], [thm-borel-casselman],
[thm-zelevinsky-segments], [thm-geometric-lemma]) as coming from the
notion of a system of local parameters, which is phrased through the
classification of representations of $\GL_n$ of a local field.
