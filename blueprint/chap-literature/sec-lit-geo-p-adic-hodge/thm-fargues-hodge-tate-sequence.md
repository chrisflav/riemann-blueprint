Let $C$ be an algebraically closed complete non-archimedean field of
characteristic $0$ and residue characteristic $p$, with ring of integers
$\mathcal O$, and let $G$ be a $p$-divisible group over $\mathcal O$ with dual
$G^\vee$. Let
$\alpha_G : \mathcal O \otimes T_p G \to \omega_{G^\vee}$ be the Hodge–Tate map
and $B$ the $\mathcal O$-bilinear pairing on
$\mathcal O \otimes T_p G \times \mathcal O \otimes T_p G^\vee$ induced by the
Weil pairing and a choice of compatible $p$-power roots of unity. Then
$x \in \mathcal O \otimes T_p G$ satisfies $\alpha_G(x) = 0$ if and only if
$B(x, y) = 0$ for every $y$ in the kernel of $\alpha_{G^\vee}$.

This is the orthogonality in the integral Hodge–Tate sequence of Fargues
(2008); see also Scholze–Weinstein (2013).
