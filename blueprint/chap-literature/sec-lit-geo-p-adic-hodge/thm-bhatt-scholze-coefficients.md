Let $K/\Q_l$ and $L/\Q_l$ be finite extensions and $T$ a $G_K$-stable
$\mathcal O_L$-lattice in a crystalline representation of $G_K$ over $L$.
Then the underlying $\Z_l$-lattice of $T$ is realised by a prismatic
$F$-crystal over $\mathcal O_K$, as in
[thm-bhatt-scholze-crystalline-lattices], and the realisation is fully
faithful.
