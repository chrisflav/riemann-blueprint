Let $K/\Q_l$ be a finite extension and $A_{\mathrm{inf}}$ Fontaine's ring,
viewed as a prism over $\mathcal O_K$. Then:

* for every crystalline representation $V$ of $G_K$ over $\Q_l$ and every
  $G_K$-stable $\Z_l$-lattice $T \subset V$ there is a prismatic $F$-crystal
  $\mathcal E$ over $\mathcal O_K$ with a Frobenius- and $G_K$-compatible
  embedding $\mathcal E(A_{\mathrm{inf}}) \hookrightarrow W(\C_l^\flat) \otimes_{\Z_l} T$
  that realises $T$;
* the realisation is fully faithful: every $G_K$-equivariant map $T \to T'$
  between realised lattices comes from a unique map of $F$-crystals.

This is the theorem of Bhatt and Scholze (2023) that $\Z_l$-lattices in
crystalline representations correspond to prismatic $F$-crystals, in the
form of existence and full faithfulness of the realisation.
