Throughout, $E$ is a number field, $l$ a prime, $\iota : \Qlbar \cong \C$,
and $\mathrm{Art}_E$ the Artin map of [thm-artin-reciprocity]. A Hecke
character $\chi$ of $E$ is *algebraic of type $n$*, for integers $n_\phi$
indexed by the embeddings $\phi : E \to \C$, if
$\chi_\infty(k) = \prod_\phi \phi(k)^{n_\phi}$ for every totally positive
$k \in E^\times$. Such a character has an $l$-adic Galois character
$r_{\chi,\iota}$ [thm-hecke-l-adic-character], and the section describes it
at the places above $l$: it is de Rham with Hodge–Tate weights $-n$,
crystalline where $\chi$ is unramified, and its Weil–Deligne representation
is the one $\chi$ predicts. These characters enter the Galois
representations of [chap-galois], the families of [chap-families] and the
Tate–Shafarevich bound of [chap-sha].
