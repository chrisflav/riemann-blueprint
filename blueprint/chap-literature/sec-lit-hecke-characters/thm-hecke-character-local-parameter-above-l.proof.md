By local–global compatibility above $l$ [thm-hecke-character-wd-above-l]
the Weil–Deligne representation is $\xi \circ \mathrm{Art}_{E_w}^{-1}$, and
a system of local parameters is the local Artin map on characters, so this
is $\rec(\xi)$. *We read* the other dependences ([thm-bz-finiteness],
[thm-borel-casselman], [thm-zelevinsky-segments], [thm-geometric-lemma]) as
coming from the notion of a system of local parameters, which is phrased
through the classification of representations of $\GL_n$ of a local field.
