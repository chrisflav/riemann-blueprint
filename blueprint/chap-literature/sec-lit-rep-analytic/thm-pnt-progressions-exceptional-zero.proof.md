Pass from primes to the Chebyshev function and use a smoothed and
differenced explicit formula, which leaves a sum over the zeros of the
$L$-functions modulo $M$. Split the range of $X$ into a near and a far part.
The zeros other than the exceptional one are bounded through a zero
density estimate and the Deuring–Heilbronn phenomenon: an exceptional
zero repels the other zeros. The repulsion is proved with Turán's
power-sum method.
