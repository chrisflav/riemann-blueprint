This section proves the bound that Landau's theorem needs at every scale
$x$ where slope data exist: $G_m(x) \le x^{1/2 + (e_D + e_L + 7)\varepsilon}$
([thm-core-inequality]). Each member of a family has a *gap*, the excess
of its von Mangoldt sums over the primes inert in $E$ above their main
terms. Orthogonality of the characters of $\Gal(E/\Q)$ turns the sum of the
gaps into twisted von Mangoldt sums, among them $G_m(x)$
([thm-chain-averaging]). The slope inequality bounds each gap
([thm-member-gap-bound]); we read it as Bost's slope inequality for
hermitian lattices, used as an arithmetic form of Stepanov's method.
