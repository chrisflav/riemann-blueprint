A sequence of comparisons carries the family hypotheses over to slope
data, using:

* Siegel masses and mass ratios, restriction, and admissible twists;
* selection under the decay of gaps, and bounds on division kernels;
* the existence of designs of the required size;
* sets of twists without short linear relations, of Behrend type
  ([lem-behrend-sets]);
* a bound on irreducible constituents from Green's classification of
  cuspidal characters ([thm-green-cuspidal]).

We have not unfolded these comparisons.
