The core inequality assumes slope data. This section derives them from the
family hypotheses of [chap-families] ([thm-families-connected]), by a
sequence of comparisons that we have not unfolded. Two of its inputs are
stated here: twisted admissibility, by which a suitable choice of twists
gives the second admissibility condition ([thm-twisted-admissibility]), and
large sets of twists without short linear relations, built as Behrend built
sets without three-term progressions ([lem-behrend-sets]). A third input,
the local link at inert primes, has its own section ([sec-zf-local-link]).
