For each primitive $\psi$ and each scale $x$ of a window we need an abelian
CM field $E$: the families live over it, and the prime sums run over its
Frobenius classes. [thm-auxiliary-fields] builds it from the
Tate–Shafarevich bound, with Galois group $\{\pm1\}^2 \times A$, degree about
$x^{2+8\varepsilon}$, and $|L'/L(1,\chi)| \le C\log x$ for the characters
that matter. For the real characters we do not prove this bound one
character at a time: we choose the auxiliary primes $q$ and $d$ outside a
small exceptional set, which a mean-square estimate controls
([lem-good-auxiliary-primes]).
