This section reduces zero-freeness to an upper bound on windows. The
weighted Chebyshev function $G_m$ ([def-chebyshev-weighted]) combines the
von Mangoldt sums of the characters in $\langle\psi\rangle$, with weights
that make its Dirichlet coefficients non-negative. Landau's theorem then
shows that a bound $G_m(x) \le x^\theta$ on windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$ of
arbitrarily large $Y$ excludes zeros with $\Re s > \theta$
([thm-zero-free-from-windows]).
