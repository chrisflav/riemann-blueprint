Landau's theorem on Dirichlet series with non-negative coefficients. The
Mellin transform of $G_m$ is a combination of $-\frac{L'}{L}(s,\chi)$ divided by
$s(s-1)$. A zero of some $L(\chi, s)$ with $\Re s > \theta$ would give it a
pole. Non-negativity of the coefficients puts the first singularity on the
real axis, and the bounds on windows exclude it.
