TODO-INTRO Proves [thm-zero-free-from-connected]: if families carry slope data and auxiliary CM fields exist on windows [Y, Y^lambda], primitive L-functions have no zeros right of 1/2. Landau's theorem on non-negative Dirichlet series reduces this to a bound G_m(x) <= x^{1/2+O(eps)} for the weighted Chebyshev function on windows. The auxiliary CM field E, of degree about x^2 and built from the Tate-Shafarevich bound and an averaging over the auxiliary primes q, d, is where the prime sums live. The core inequality gets the square-root bound from slope data: each member of a family bounds its von Mangoldt sums over primes inert in E by their main terms (a slope inequality for hermitian lattices), and averaging over a chain of theta lifts of length about sqrt(x) balances the errors. The last two sections show that the family hypotheses yield slope data, through the local link between prime sums and lattice gains at inert primes.
