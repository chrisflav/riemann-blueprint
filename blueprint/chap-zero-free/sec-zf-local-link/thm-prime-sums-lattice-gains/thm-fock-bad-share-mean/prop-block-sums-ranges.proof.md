The size hypotheses give the basic facts about segments, and from them
three conditions: the index sets of $P$ do not depend on the top
exponents, the size condition, and the bound on $\varepsilon$-terms with
constant $C_M$. Under these conditions, applying the mean value theorem
[thm-mean-value-bad-shares] to each product of ranges gives the bound on
block sums.
