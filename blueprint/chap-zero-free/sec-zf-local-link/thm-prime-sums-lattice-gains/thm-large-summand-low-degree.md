Let $D$ be a member of a family satisfying the second group of admissibility
conditions (on path pairs, column families, branching, global sections,
graded lattices and type decompositions). Then there is $C_0 > 0$ such that
for all $C_\Lambda, C_k \ge C_0$ there are $C_{\mathrm{rk}}, C_{\deg} > 0$
with the following property.

Let $p \ge 13$, $\mathcal O'/\mathcal O$ an unramified quadratic extension of
discrete valuation rings with residue field of order $p^{n'}$, and $\mathcal F$ a
valid system of frames of rank $r \ge 2$ on a set $T$ of tops of degrees
$\deg t \in [a, 2a]$, with at most $N_s$ letters per level, $k = r + K$,
and the size conditions $C_kN_s^2\log a \le k$,
$128k^2N_sY^2 \le p^{L_1 - 1}$ and $32pYk^2N_sQ^2(E_0 + m^* + 1) \le a$.
Let $N$ be the $\mathcal O$-module generated by the polynomials built from
the frames. Then $N$ contains a direct summand $S$, with a basis of
generators, of rank at least
$$p^{-n'rK}\Big(1 - C_{\mathrm{rk}}\frac{|T|r^2k}{Y}\Big)\prod_{t \in T}\binom{\deg t + k - 1}{k - 1},$$
and every monomial of every element of $N$ has degree at most
$C_{\deg}|T|\big(C_\Lambda N_s^2\log a \cdot a/(pk) + ap^{-L_1} + kQ\big)$.
