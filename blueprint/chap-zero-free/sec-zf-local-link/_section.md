TODO-INTRO The local inequality from which the slope inequality is assembled: at each inert prime the von Mangoldt class sums are bounded by the gains of the theta lifts. It rests on three closed-form local theorems about lattices of polynomials over unramified quadratic extensions: the local estimate at inert primes, the mean bad share of Fock lattices, and a large direct summand of low degree.
