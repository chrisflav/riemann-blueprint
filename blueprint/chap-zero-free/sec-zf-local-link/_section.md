This section proves the local inequality behind the slope inequality: at
each prime inert in the auxiliary field, the von Mangoldt class sums of a
member are bounded by the gains of its theta lifts
([thm-prime-sums-lattice-gains]). [thm-families-connected] uses it. The
proof combines three theorems about lattices of polynomials over an
unramified quadratic extension of discrete valuation rings: the local
estimate at inert primes ([thm-local-estimate-inert-primes]), a bound on
the mean bad share of Fock lattices ([thm-fock-bad-share-mean]), and a large
direct summand of low degree ([thm-large-summand-low-degree]).
