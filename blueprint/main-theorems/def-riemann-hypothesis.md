The *Riemann Hypothesis* is the statement that every zero $s$ of the Riemann
zeta function other than the trivial zeros $-2, -4, -6, \dots$ satisfies
$\Re s = \tfrac12$.

Here $\zeta$ is defined on all of $\C$ and given an arbitrary value at its
pole $s = 1$, so the statement also excludes $s = 1$.
