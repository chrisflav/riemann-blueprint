Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, and of the Generalised Riemann
Hypothesis for Dirichlet $L$-functions, has two steps. The hard step is a single statement:
no Dirichlet $L$-function vanishes in the half-plane $\Re s > \tfrac12$
([thm-half-plane]). The rest of this chapter derives the theorems about
zeros on the critical line from it by the functional equation, an argument
of a few lines. The remaining chapters prove [thm-half-plane].
