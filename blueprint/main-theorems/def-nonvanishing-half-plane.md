We say that *Dirichlet $L$-functions do not vanish right of the critical
line* if for every modulus $N \ge 1$, every Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo
$N$, primitive or not, and every $s$ with $\Re s > \tfrac12$,
$$ L(\chi, s) \neq 0, $$
the pole $\chi = 1$, $s = 1$ excepted.
