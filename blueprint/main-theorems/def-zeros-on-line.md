We say that *the zeros of Dirichlet $L$-functions in the critical strip lie
on the line* if for every modulus $N \ge 1$ and every Dirichlet character
$\chi$ modulo $N$, every zero of $L(\chi, s)$ with $0 < \Re s < 1$ has
$\Re s = \tfrac12$. We use the same phrase restricted to primitive characters.
