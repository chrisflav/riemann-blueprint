Over the auxiliary CM fields of the proof, let $A$ and $B$ be hermitian
spaces with
$$ \operatorname{rank} A < \operatorname{rank} B \le 2 \operatorname{rank} A, \qquad \operatorname{rank} A + r(B) < \operatorname{rank} B, $$
$r(B)$ the Witt index. Let $\omega$ be a Weil representation of the pair
$(\U(A \oplus -A), \U(B))$, and $f$ a Schwartz function of Fock type at the
archimedean places. Then the theta function $\theta(g, h; f)$ is integrable
over $\U(B)(F)\backslash\U(B)(\A_F)$, and its average equals the value of
the Siegel Eisenstein series on $\U(A \oplus -A)$ attached to the section
$g \mapsto \omega(g) f(0)$ at the Siegel–Weil point.

It is stated for unitary dual pairs, in the doubling setting and in Weil's
convergent range.
