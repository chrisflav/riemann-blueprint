The Siegel–Weil formula identifies the average of a theta function over an
adelic unitary group with a value of a Siegel Eisenstein series. We use it
in several ranges. In Weil's range, where both sides converge, it is Weil's
theorem [thm-siegel-weil-weil-range]; its classical form, Siegel's mass
formula for hermitian forms [thm-siegel-main-theorem-hermitian], gives the
mass constants of the later sections. Outside Weil's range we use the
formula of Kudla–Rallis and Ichino for Fock vectors
[thm-siegel-weil-outside-weil-range] and its doubling form
[thm-siegel-weil-convergent-doubling]. The forms for definite spaces,
[thm-siegel-weil-definite] and [thm-siegel-weil-definite-k], serve the
theta towers and the construction of Galois representations in
[chap-galois].
