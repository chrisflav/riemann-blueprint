TODO-INTRO The harmonic analysis of the theta lifts along the chains of a family. The Siegel-Weil formula, in all the variants used, gives the step identities of theta towers: each step up a tower costs one special value of a Hecke L-function (Rallis inner product). From these come separation of vectors by theta functionals, non-vanishing of downward lifts, rational test vectors, the control of L-values along Witt towers behind the slope bound on the indefinite side, and the isometric period identity. Archimedean Bessel transforms and models, the off-diagonal bound, counting of automorphic forms with Siegel mass constants, and p-adic measures from CM theta functions supply the estimates that the family conditions of [chap-families] need.
