A theta tower is a sequence of unitary dual pairs $\U(V) \times \U(W)$ in
which the dimension of $W$ grows by two at each step. Its step identities
([def-theta-tower-step], [thm-theta-tower-step-identities]) say that matched
theta integrals along the tower are proportional, and that each step
multiplies the constant by one partial Hecke $L$-value, as in the Rallis
inner product formula. The proof
computes the constant terms of theta integrals along the parabolic that
fixes an isotropic plane and compares them, through the definite
Siegel–Weil formula, with those of Siegel Eisenstein series. Every later
section uses these identities.
