Theta towers of unitary dual pairs over a CM extension have step
identities ([def-theta-tower-step]): triples related by theta lifts have
matching local data, matched theta integrals are proportional, and
replacing $\U(\cdot, b+2)$ by $\U(\cdot, b)$ multiplies the constant by the
partial Hecke $L$-value $L^S(\nu, \tfrac{a-(b+2)}{2} + 1)$, up to a unit and
an explicit factor.
