Let $E/F$ be a CM extension and consider theta towers of unitary dual pairs
$\U(V) \times \U(W)$ with $W$ of dimension $b + 2$ over $W_1$ of dimension $b$.
The following statements hold:

* vanishing of evaluation functionals, and their behaviour on cones;
* existence of stabilisers, the two tower properties, non-vanishing of
  factors, existence of matchings and of Levi decompositions;
* density of polynomial Schwartz functions, convergence of the Weil
  representation, continuity of $\mathrm{SO}$-actions, existence of
  splittings;
* the constant terms of theta integrals along the parabolic of $\U(W)$ that
  stabilises an isotropic plane: on the big cone the constant term equals
  $C \ne 0$ times the theta integral on $\U(W_1)$ of a partial Fourier
  transform, and the small-cone terms vanish or factor the same way;
* the archimedean statements, the real-part condition, and the local
  statements $A$ and $B$ with their end cases at real and finite places.

Together these give the matching of local data for triples of
representations related by theta lifts.
