There are constants $(n_0, A, C, 1/16, 1/16)$, depending only on the outer
constants of a family, with $C \ge 0$ and $A = 2(C_Y + 1)$, such that at
every point of every family the number of automorphic forms of each type is
given by the counting theorem of [thm-counting-automorphic-forms-unitary]
with these constants.
