The family conditions need the number of automorphic forms of each type,
with its main term in terms of Siegel's mass constant.
[thm-counting-automorphic-forms-unitary] counts automorphic forms of a
given type on unitary groups of large rank by a kernel argument, given the
volume step: splitting off an anisotropic line changes the Haar volume by
the Siegel mass ratio. Siegel's mass formula supplies the volume step
([lem-counting-constants-siegel-weil]).
[thm-counting-constant-mass-constant] identifies the constant of the count
of lattice points with the mass constant, through an archimedean volume
identity.
