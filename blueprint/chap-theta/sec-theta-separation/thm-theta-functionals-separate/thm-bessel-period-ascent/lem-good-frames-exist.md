Suppose depth is fixed, as in
[thm-bessel-period-ascent]. For every grid of
hermitian spaces with Weil representations at a member of a family, and
every volume on its core, there is a system of frames (bases of the
hermitian spaces of the tower adapted to the grid) whose ends match the
given volume, and a lattice for which the frames are good.

*We read* a frame as a choice of compatible bases of the spaces in the
theta tower, and "good" as the condition that the local theta integrals
unfold along it; the definition has many clauses.
