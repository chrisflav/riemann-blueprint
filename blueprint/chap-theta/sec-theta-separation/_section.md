Two non-vanishing results for the theta lifts of a family. Theta
functionals separate vectors ([thm-theta-functionals-separate]): Howe
duality at unramified places, the Siegel–Weil formula and a quantitative
branching law for discrete series give separation by real functionals, and
the ascent of Bessel periods up the tower ([thm-bessel-period-ascent])
turns this into separation by Bessel periods. Downward theta lifts are
nonzero and injective on Hecke eigenspaces
([thm-downward-theta-lift-nonvanishing]): by the doubling method a pole of
the partial $L$-function gives a residue of a Siegel Eisenstein series,
and this residue is a theta integral from a smaller space
([thm-siegel-eisenstein-residue-theta]).
