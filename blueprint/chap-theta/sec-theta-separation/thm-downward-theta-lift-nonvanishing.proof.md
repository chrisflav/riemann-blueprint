Rapid decay of cusp forms [thm-cusp-rapid-decay] and the unramified
doubling integral [thm-unramified-doubling-gl] give the Euler product of
the doubling zeta integral far to the right, and the continuation of the
partial Hecke $L$-function [thm-hecke-l-nonvanishing-line-one] gives it a
simple pole. The doubling method [thm-doubling-method], with the
continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series
[thm-eisenstein-continuation-growth] and, as we read it, the residue identity
[thm-siegel-eisenstein-residue-theta], turns the pole into a nonzero downward
theta lift. Howe duality at unramified places, in the Weil representation
of [thm-weil-representation-unitary-exists], shows that the lift of an
eigenform is an eigenform and that lifts generate at the finite places;
generation at the real places is separate. Detection follows from
non-vanishing and generation, and injectivity from detection.
