For each point $t$ choose a quadratic extension read off from $a$, and
take the relation between first coordinates, the volume data and a kernel
that $a$ provides. Local twist values give finite combinations of theta
functions; here we use the parametrisation of discrete series
([thm-discrete-series-parametrisation]) and Harish-Chandra's theory of
$(\mathfrak g, K)$-modules ([thm-hc-modules-unitary]). The joint twist
relation then fixes the remaining scalars. The group of characters involved
has one element, so the scalar at the top is determined. Define the second
coordinates from these coefficients and check the output conditions one at
a time.
