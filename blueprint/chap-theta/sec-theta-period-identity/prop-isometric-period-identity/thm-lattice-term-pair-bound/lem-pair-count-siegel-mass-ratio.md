For the constants and data of [thm-lattice-term-pair-bound], the weighted
sum over pairs of terms,
$$\sum_{(t, t') \in S} w_t w_{t'}\,\omega_t\,\omega_{t'},$$
is at most $m_u\,A_e(j', \delta')A_e(j'', \delta'')$.
