TODO-INTRO Evaluating the period P_{u,t} is, up to an isometry and nonzero constants, an inner product of a theta lift with a projection. This is the multiplicity condition among the basic conditions. The proof is a diagonal estimate from Siegel-Weil, a real-package version built from family characters with links, and a bound for sums over pairs of lattice terms from Siegel mass ratios.
