The multiplicity condition, one of the basic conditions of
[thm-basic-conditions-from-local-parameters], asks that evaluating the
periods $P_{u,t}$ of a family give, up to an isometry and nonzero
constants, inner products of theta lifts with projections.
[prop-isometric-period-identity] proves this at every admissible datum in
three steps: a diagonal estimate from the Siegel–Weil formula and the
archimedean bounds of [sec-theta-bessel]; conditions at the points from the
theory of discrete series; and the identity for a real package
([prop-period-identity-real-package]), built from family characters with
links. The proof also uses a bound for sums over pairs of lattice terms,
from Siegel mass ratios ([thm-lattice-term-pair-bound]).
