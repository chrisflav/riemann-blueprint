For all large scales and every choice of test data there are a sign $s$, a
constant $\kappa$ and values $z_t$ of the twisting characters such that for
every term $\beta$ representing $s$, every base term $\beta_0$ and every real
point $p$, the Fock-model values of the twisted vacuum vectors at $p\beta$
equal $\zeta$ times the corresponding combination at $p\beta_0$, with
$\zeta = \kappa z_t^{-1}$ on the staircase cosets.
