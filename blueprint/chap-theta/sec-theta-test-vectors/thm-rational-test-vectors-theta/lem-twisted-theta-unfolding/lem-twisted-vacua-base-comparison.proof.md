First work at one place, with the sign $s = -1$. At large depth the
lattice points of the frames are integral matrices, the local characters
split into components, and the local model of each arrow moves the frames;
a lattice vacuum is then a combination of local-model vectors, and a
double step of the tower acts on it explicitly. The unfolded product
depends only on the product of the local data, so the one-place comparison
gives a comparison relative to the base vacua, with one constant $\kappa$.
