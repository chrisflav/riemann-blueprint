By [lem-theta-lift-smooth-cusp-forms] the lifts are cuspidal for all smooth
test functions. Arrows of the grid go either to the right or down a flip.
For the arrows to the right this is a cusp transition; for the descending
arrows one runs through the flips of the tower. The discrete series results
identify the archimedean types of the lifts.
