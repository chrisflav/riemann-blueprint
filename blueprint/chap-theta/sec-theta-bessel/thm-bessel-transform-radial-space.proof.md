Build two radial operator identities, for the right action of $G$ and for
its adjoint, from the right group action, the action on $\pi$ and the
vanishing of one composite operator: for every real parameter $Y$ with
$Y - 1, Y, Y + 1$ in the admissible set, the composite of the level
operators $G\,\xi(Y)\,R(Y)$ is the radial part of an invariant
differential operator on $G$, and the same holds for the adjoint action.
These two identities, combined with the adjoint operator, a pairing
identity, a quadratic identity for the adjoint and a test of the function
$\xi$, give a single radial identity. That identity gives the subspaces
$K_l$ and the membership of $J_F$.
