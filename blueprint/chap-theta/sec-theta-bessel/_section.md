The archimedean estimates behind the period identity and the off-diagonal
bound take place on the real unitary group of signature $(k+r, r+1)$, with
the Bessel models of Gan, Gross and Prasad for a subgroup $\U(k)$
([def-archimedean-bessel-data]). The main theorem
([thm-bessel-transform-radial-space]) says that Bessel transforms of
compactly supported functions satisfy the radial equations of invariant
differential operators. With Capelli's identity for the quantum determinant
this gives pointwise ([thm-discrete-series-bessel-pointwise-bound]) and
weighted ([thm-weighted-bessel-sum-bound]) bounds for Bessel transforms of
discrete series coefficients, and archimedean models in which local
integrals become energy forms ([thm-archimedean-models]).
