TODO-INTRO On the real unitary group of signature (k+r, r+1), Bessel transforms of compactly supported functions lie in an explicit radial space. With Capelli's identity for the quantum determinant this gives pointwise and weighted bounds for Bessel transforms of discrete series coefficients, and archimedean models for admissible settings.
