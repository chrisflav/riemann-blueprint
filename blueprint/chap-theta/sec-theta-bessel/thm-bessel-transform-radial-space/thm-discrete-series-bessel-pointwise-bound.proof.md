Bound eigenfunctions of the Capelli operators (built from the
[quantum determinant](@def-quantum-determinant)) pointwise, and apply this to
discrete series coefficients. The two inputs are the integrability of the
Bessel kernel $\xi$ and the fact that the transforms of the relevant classes
are compactly supported, which is where [thm-bessel-transform-radial-space] enters.
