The normalised theta family satisfies the equivariance laws, and its vectors
lie in the lattices. This gives the chain theta data and the first three
laws. The two bounds come from the integral jet moments of
[lem-integral-moments-kernel-jets] together with the simplicity of
Weyl modules in the closed lowest alcove [thm-jantzen-lowest-alcove].
