Let a standard construction be given. For every member $u$ and every index
$a$ there is a datum whose chain is the twisted chain of $(u, a)$, such that
each lattice $A_i$, $i \le m$, splits relative to the level, and at each step
$i < m$ there is a kernel that

1. agrees with the kernel of the point at the representatives;
2. is compatible with dilations;
3. satisfies the unit condition;
4. is bounded at the places over the primes conjugate to the level;
5. is bounded at the places over the level prime.
