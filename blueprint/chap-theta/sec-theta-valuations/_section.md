At the level primes the family conditions ask for sharp valuation bounds on
the vectors of the reference chain and their twists
([lem-valuations-level-primes]). They follow from five laws for the kernels
of the lattice chain ([lem-five-laws-chain-kernels]), whose two bounds are
integral moments of jets of theta kernels
([lem-integral-moments-kernel-jets]). These moments come from Katz's
$p$-adic measures attached to CM theta functions
([thm-katz-measures-cm-theta]).
