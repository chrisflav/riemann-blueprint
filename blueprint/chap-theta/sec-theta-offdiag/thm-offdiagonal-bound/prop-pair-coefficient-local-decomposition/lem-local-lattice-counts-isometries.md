Let $F$ be a non-archimedean local field, $E/F$ the local algebra of the
datum $D$ with a good model, and $V$ a hermitian $E$-space of the dimension
of $D$. Let $t_1$ be generic data for a flag of length $r+1$, and $f_2, f_2'$
frames with isometries $\varphi, \varphi'$ to the spans of $t_1$, fixing the
last vectors. Let $P_1, P_2$ be the Gram matrices of $t_1$ and of
$(f_2, f_2')$, and $S$ the set of generic lattices compatible with these data.

* If a determinant $\Delta$ attached to $t_1$ is $0$, then $|S|$ is the number of
  isometries between $P_1$ and $P_2$.
* If $\Delta$ is a unit, then $|S|$ is the number of isometries between the
  $r \times r$ blocks of $P_1$ and $P_2$ that also match the last row and
  column.
