Distinct types of a datum are almost orthogonal: [thm-offdiagonal-bound]
gives $|K_0(u,u')|^2 \le (1 + b^{-6})\,d_u d_{u'}/D^*$ for $u \ne u'$. The
proof is an abstract bound for a pairing over a CM field with nineteen
inputs, which [prop-offdiagonal-hypotheses-hold] checks for the families.
The main inputs are archimedean model data ([prop-archimedean-model-data])
and a decomposition of the pair coefficients into local counts of partners
and lattices ([prop-pair-coefficient-local-decomposition]). The family
conditions use the bound through [prop-small-terms-offdiag-level].
