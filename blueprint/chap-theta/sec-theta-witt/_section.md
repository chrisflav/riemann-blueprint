TODO-INTRO The largest slope of the hermitian lattice on the indefinite side is at most its height less the mean of log kappa over the twists. The proof controls the squared theta constants along Witt towers by ratios of Hecke L-values, their non-vanishing and their denominators, using the arithmetic statements of the non-endoscopic case.
