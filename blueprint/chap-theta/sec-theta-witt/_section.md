This section proves the slope bound on the indefinite side
([thm-indefinite-slope-bound]): the largest slope of the hermitian lattice
there is at most its height minus the mean of $\log\kappa$ over the twists.
Iterating the step identities along a Witt tower expresses the ratios of
square constants of theta lifts through ratios of Hecke $L$-values
([thm-witt-tower-square-ratios]). The non-vanishing of these values, bounds
on their denominators and the functional equation then bound the square
ratios place by place ([thm-square-term-estimates]). The arithmetic of the
$L$-values comes from the results gathered in
[thm-non-endoscopic-hypotheses-hold].
