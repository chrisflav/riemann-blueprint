TODO-INTRO Proves [thm-galois-reps-polarized]: polarized pairs have Galois representations compatible with the local parameters where these are determined. In odd rank, a representation of a definite unitary group is transferred to a compact unitary Shimura variety of signature (1, a-1) and found in the l-adic cohomology of Kuga-Sato varieties (Eichler-Shimura, crystalline comparison, Hodge-Tate, holomorphic Lefschetz). Congruences, Chenevier's pseudo-representation argument and theta lifts from definite unitary groups extend this to all ranks and to GL_a by base change; depth-zero base change gives compatibility at ramified places. The last section records the forms used by the families.
