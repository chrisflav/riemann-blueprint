This section shows that the Kuga–Sato varieties over compact unitary Shimura
varieties of signature $(1, n-1)$, the fibre powers of the universal abelian
scheme, satisfy the hypotheses of [thm-galois-reps-hecke-eigenspaces]
([thm-kuga-sato-unitary-shimura]). Their smoothness and good reduction come
from the integral models of [sec-gal-shimura-models]. The Hodge numbers of
their eigenspaces come from the holomorphic Lefschetz formula: fixed points
of Hecke correspondences are CM points, and the sum over them is a sum of
orbital integrals.
