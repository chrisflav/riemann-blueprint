[thm-grh-from-theta-integrals] uses [thm-galois-reps-polarized] in two
forms, recorded here. By the Gelfand–Kazhdan theorem a conjugate self-dual
cuspidal representation forms a polarized pair, so it has a Galois
representation; in the abelian case this is compatible above $l$ as well
([thm-galois-reps-csd-above-l]). The second form states the polarization
through the inverse transpose ([thm-galois-reps-polarized-cuspidal]).
