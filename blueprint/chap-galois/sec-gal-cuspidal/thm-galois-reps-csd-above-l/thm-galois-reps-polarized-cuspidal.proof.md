By Gelfand–Kazhdan [thm-gelfand-kazhdan], $\pi^\vee \cong \pi\circ\theta$ with
$\theta(g) = {}^tg^{-1}$, so the polarization condition, stated with the
inverse transpose, becomes $\pi^\vee \cong \pi^c \otimes (\chi\circ N)$. For
pairs in this form [thm-galois-reps-polarized] gives $r$; the local
conditions are stated through the Langlands classification
[thm-zelevinsky-segments].
