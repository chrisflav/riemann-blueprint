The Kuga–Sato varieties of [sec-gal-kuga-sato] live over integral models of
unitary Shimura varieties of Harris–Taylor type. This section proves, after
Kottwitz, that the moduli problem is represented by a quasi-projective
scheme, smooth of relative dimension $a(n-1)$
([thm-unitary-shimura-integral-models]). Smoothness comes from counting
first-order deformations through the eigenparts of de Rham homology.
