Let $A_0$ be an abelian variety with $\mathcal O_D$-action, polarisation and
level structure, of the type of [thm-unitary-shimura-integral-models], over
an algebraically closed field $k$, lying over a point $\beta : R \to k$. For each $i$, the largest quotient of $H^{\mathrm{dR}}_1(A_0)$ on which
$\mathcal O_F$ acts through $\beta \circ t_i$ has dimension $mn$, for the integers $m, n$ of the
datum.
