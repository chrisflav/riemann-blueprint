This section reduces [thm-galois-reps-polarized] to two inputs
([thm-galois-reps-from-geometry-and-theta]): the
odd-rank representations of [sec-gal-odd-rank] and the non-vanishing of theta
lifts from definite unitary groups ([thm-theta-lift-nonvanishing], after
Rallis and Li). From the odd-rank representations we cut out a piece of the
right dimension, reach all weights by congruences and a limit, in the style of Chenevier, of
pseudo-representations, isolate even-rank representations between two theta
lifts to odd rank, and descend from $\GL_a$ to unitary groups. The ramified
places are left to [sec-gal-ramified].
