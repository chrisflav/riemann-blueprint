Rallis' inner product argument. For unitary dual pairs over a CM extension
$E/E^+$ it uses: the full Siegel–Weil formula, which comes from its anisotropic
case ([thm-siegel-weil-definite-k]) with reduction theory through Siegel sets
for unitary groups ([thm-reduction-theory-unitary]) and the Hasse norm theorem
([thm-hasse-norm-quadratic]); the doubling method and the unfolding of the
doubled theta integral to the zeta integral; Schrödinger models of the Weil
representation attached to polarisations, split models, and vectors fixed by
the relevant compact subgroups; basic properties of theta functions,
compactness and stabiliser statements in the doubled group, and the existence
of the dual pairs needed in the theta tower; and two statements that lift
identities from the real Lie algebra of the unitary group to the group.
*It is not clear* from the statements how the items other than the
Siegel–Weil formula are proved; we have not checked most of them in detail.
