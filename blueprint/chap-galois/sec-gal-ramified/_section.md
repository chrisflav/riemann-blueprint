[sec-gal-assembly] gives Galois representations compatible at the unramified
places; this section adds the ramified places that
[def-galois-reps-polarized] asks about ([thm-local-global-ramified]). Compatibility first extends to
principal series from characters no two of which differ by $|\cdot|$. At a depth-zero place over
$\Q_g$, a solvable base change makes the component a principal series of
that kind ([thm-depth-zero-base-change-principal-series]), and restricting to
the smaller Galois group does not change inertia. Where the definition asks only for
the dimension of the Weil–Deligne representation, there is nothing to prove.
