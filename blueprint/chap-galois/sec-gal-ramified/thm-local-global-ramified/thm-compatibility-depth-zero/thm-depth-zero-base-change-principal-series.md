Let $E$ be a totally complex field, $\pi$ cuspidal on $\GL_n(\A_E)$, and $\Pi$ a lift
of $\pi$ to an extension $K$ of $E$ in which inertia at the place $v$ acts
trivially. Let $E_v \cong \Q_g$, let $\pi_v$ have depth zero, and let $u$ be the place of
$K$ above $v$, with residue field of size $g^f$, where $f$ is divisible by the
lengths of all segments in the cuspidal support of $\pi_v$. Then $\Pi_u$ is a
principal series induced from characters $\xi_j$ obtained from the support of
$\pi_v$; if $\pi_v$ is of mixed type, no two of the $\xi_j$ differ by $|\cdot|$.
