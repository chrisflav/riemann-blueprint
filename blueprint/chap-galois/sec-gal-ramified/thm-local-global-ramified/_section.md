Suppose that polarized pairs have Galois representations compatible at the
unramified places, as in the conclusion of [thm-galois-reps-unramified-compatible]. Then polarized pairs
have Galois representations in the sense of [def-galois-reps-polarized], for
every system of local parameters ([def-local-parameters]).
