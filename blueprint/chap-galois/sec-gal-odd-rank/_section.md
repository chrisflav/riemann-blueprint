This section supplies the first input of [sec-gal-assembly]. We move an automorphic
representation of the definite unitary group $\U_a$, $a$ odd, with a cuspidal
type at one place above $l$ and lacunary weights at another, to a
Hecke eigensystem on a compact unitary Shimura variety of signature
$(1, a-1)$ ([thm-transfer-unitary-shimura]). The Kuga–Sato varieties over it
([sec-gal-kuga-sato]) and the results on Hecke eigenspaces
([sec-gal-eigenspaces]) give a Galois representation of unknown dimension on
which the Satake polynomial kills Frobenius
([thm-geometric-galois-odd-rank]).
