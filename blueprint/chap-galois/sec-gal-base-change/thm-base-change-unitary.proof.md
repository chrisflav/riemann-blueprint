*We read* the proof as Labesse's comparison of the trace formula of the
definite unitary group with the twisted trace formula of $\GL_n$ over $E$.
The bound of [thm-hecke-bound-base-change] reads off the Satake parameters at
the finite places, and [thm-twisted-trace-lowest-type] computes the twisted
characters at the archimedean places. We have not unfolded the rest.
