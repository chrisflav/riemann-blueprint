[sec-gal-assembly] passes between definite unitary groups and $\GL_n$ over CM
fields by base change. This section states weak base change in isobaric form
([thm-base-change-unitary], after Labesse) and in the form with local
components ([thm-labesse-base-change]). It also gives the local results behind
the comparison of trace formulas: a bound for Hecke operators, from wave
kernels on $\GL_n(\C)$ and the Jacquet–Rallis fundamental lemma; a twisted
trace on the lowest type at the archimedean places; and the Leibniz rule for
Bernstein–Zelevinsky derivatives.
