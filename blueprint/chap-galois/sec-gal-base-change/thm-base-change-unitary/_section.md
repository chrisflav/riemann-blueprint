Let $E$ be a CM field with $E/E^+$ unramified at the finite places. Every
automorphic representation of a definite unitary group in $n$ variables
over $E^+$ has a weak base change to $\GL_n(\A_E)$. The base change is an
isobaric sum of conjugate-self-dual discrete automorphic representations of
groups $\GL_{n_i}(\A_E)$, compatible with the local base change at split
places, at unramified inert places and at infinity.
