Let $k$ be a non-archimedean local field with a non-trivial character $\psi$, and
$\pi_1$, $\pi_2$ smooth representations of $\GL_r(k)$ and $\GL_t(k)$. For every $\ell$, the
$\ell$-th derivative of $\pi_1 \times \pi_2$ has a filtration
$F_0 \supseteq F_1 \supseteq \dots \supseteq F_{\ell+1} = 0$ whose $i$-th graded piece is
$$\pi_1^{(i)}\,|\det|^{(\ell-i)/2} \times \pi_2^{(\ell-i)}\,|\det|^{i/2}$$
when $i \le r$ and $\ell - i \le t$, and zero otherwise.
