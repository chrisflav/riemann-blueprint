Let $E$ be a CM field with $E/E^+$ unramified, $n \ge 1$, and $U$ an automorphic
representation of the definite unitary group $\U_n$ over $E^+$. For finite sets
$S$, $T$ of places, the families of Hecke operators built from $U$ on spaces of
automorphic forms of fixed level satisfy a uniform bound, used to read off
Satake parameters in the proof of base change.

*It is not clear* exactly what the bounded quantities are; we read them as the
entries of Hecke operators in bases adapted to the torus at the places of $T$.
