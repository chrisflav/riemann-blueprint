This section is general étale cohomology, with no unitary groups. For a
smooth projective variety with finite étale Hecke correspondences, each
system of Hecke eigenvalues cuts out a Galois representation on cohomology
([thm-galois-reps-hecke-eigenspaces]). A congruence relation at a place of
good reduction controls Frobenius; a good model above $l$ makes the
representation crystalline; and the Hodge–Tate decomposition, which commutes
with the correspondences, gives Hodge–Tate weights from Hodge numbers. The
classical inputs (proper base change, the projective bundle formula,
finiteness) come first.
