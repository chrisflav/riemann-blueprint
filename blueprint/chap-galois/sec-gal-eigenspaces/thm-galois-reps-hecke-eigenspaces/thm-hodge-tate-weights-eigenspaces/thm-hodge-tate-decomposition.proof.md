The proof builds an injective Hodge–Tate map, compatible with the top-degree
trace, from a comparison on the étale side. Surjectivity then uses only that
étale cohomology is finite-dimensional and has classes of diagonals and of
squares in triple products with the expected properties: cup products, a slant
product and a retraction, together with these classes, show that the map is
onto. The dimension formula follows from an equality of ranks.
