Let $K$ be a finite extension of $\Q_l$ and $V$ a continuous finite-dimensional
representation of $G_K$ over $\Q_l$. If $V$ is de Rham, then $V$ is potentially
semistable (Berger, 2002, after André, Kedlaya and Mebkhout). The statement
includes a matrix form, for representations with values in $\GL_n$ of the
completed maximal unramified extension of $\Q_l$ that are rational in a fixed
sense.
