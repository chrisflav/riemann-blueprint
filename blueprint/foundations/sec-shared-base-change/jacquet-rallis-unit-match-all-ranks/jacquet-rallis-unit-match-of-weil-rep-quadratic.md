Suppose that for every unramified quadratic extension $L/K$ of finite
extensions of $F$ and every standard character $\psi$, the Weil representation
attached to the norm form exists with the Weil constant given by the
quadratic character of $L/K$. Then the matching of
[jacquet-rallis-unit-match-all-ranks] holds in every rank. *We read* this as a
local proof by induction on the rank, in the manner of Beuzart-Plessis.
