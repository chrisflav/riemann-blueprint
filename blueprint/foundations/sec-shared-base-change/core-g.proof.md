The twisted trace on $\mathrm{Hom}_{\U(n)}(\tau, V)$ is $2^n$ times the trace of $A$ on
the highest weight vectors of the harmonic weights of $\lambda$. Only one
harmonic weight occurs, with a line of highest weight vectors, and an
involution acts on a line by $\pm 1$.
