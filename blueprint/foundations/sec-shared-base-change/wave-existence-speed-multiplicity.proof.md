From properties of the length function on $\GL_n(\C)$, unimodularity, a Sobolev
inequality on the group and the calculus of right derivatives: finite
propagation speed first, then compactness of supports and existence of
solutions, then the kernels by uniqueness of the wave kernel, and their
algebra.
